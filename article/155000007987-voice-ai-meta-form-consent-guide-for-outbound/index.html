Voice AI - META Form Consent Guide For Outbound

Facebook Lead Forms used for Voice AI outbound calling must include the correct consent disclaimer so MxP can verify that a lead agreed to receive AI-powered calls. This guide explains how to add the Voice AI disclaimer to a Meta form, map the form in MxP, and confirm that the disclaimer is recognized. Completing this process helps prevent outbound Voice AI calls from being blocked due to missing consent.

What is Meta Form Consent for Outbound Voice


Meta Form Consent for Outbound Voice AI is the process of adding a Voice AI-approved consent disclaimer to a Facebook Lead Form in Meta Business Suite, then mapping that form in MxP so the disclaimer can be recognized. Since Facebook Lead Forms are managed by Meta, the disclaimer must be added inside Meta Business Suite rather than directly inside MxP.


Meta Form Consent ensures that leads submitting Facebook Lead Forms are presented with the required consent language before Voice AI outbound calling is enabled for those submissions. MxP checks the connected form after it is mapped and refreshed to confirm whether the disclaimer is present.

Key Benefits of Meta Form Consent for Outbound Voice AI


  • Consent Recognition: Allows MxP to detect the Voice AI disclaimer on connected Facebook Lead Forms.

  • Outbound Calling Readiness: Helps prevent outbound Voice AI calls from being blocked because of missing form consent.

  • Meta Form Compatibility: Provides a clear process for adding the disclaimer in Meta Business Suite, where Facebook Lead Forms are created and managed.

  • Cleaner Troubleshooting: Makes it easier to confirm whether a form is still missing a disclaimer or has been successfully recognized.

How to Set Up Meta Form Consent for Outbound Voice AI


The setup requires work in both MxP and Meta Business Suite. Start by verifying the disclaimer in Voice AI, then add it to the Facebook Lead Form, map the form in MxP, and refresh the consent status to confirm approval.


  1. In MxP, go to AI Agents > Voice AI.

  2. Click Update Disclosure in the top-right corner.



  3. Review the existing disclosure or enter the client’s desired consent language.

  4. Use the compatibility checker to confirm the disclaimer follows the recommended structure for Voice AI outbound calling.



  5. Once the disclaimer is verified, copy the approved disclaimer text before leaving this page.

  6. In the same Voice AI disclosure area, go to Choose Applicable Products > Manual Consent Application.

  7. Locate the Facebook Forms section.

  8. Click Update Consent.

  9. Meta Business Suite will open in a new browser tab. (Note: Facebook Lead Forms cannot be modified inside MxP. Any changes to the form disclaimer must be completed in Meta Business Suite.



  10. In Meta Business Suite, create a new Facebook Lead Form or edit a drafted form. (Note: Existing published Facebook Lead Forms may not be editable. If the form cannot be edited, create a new form with the required disclaimer.)

  11. Complete the form setup until you reach the Privacy Policy section.

  12. In the Privacy Policy section, select Add Custom Disclaimer.

  13. Add a title for the disclaimer.

  14. Paste the Voice AI disclaimer text copied from MxP.

  15. Confirm the disclaimer is placed specifically in the Privacy Policy section.

  16. Click on Create Form.



  17. Return to MxP and click on Refresh in the Update Disclosure module to load the form.



  18. The form might show "No disclaimer found"



  19. To fix it, go to Settings.



  20. Then Integrations → Facebook.



  21. Click the three-dot menu and select Form Field Mapping.

  22. Select the newly created or updated Facebook Lead Form.

  23. Map all required fields.



  24. Go back to AI Agents → Voice AI → Update Disclosure.

  25. Return to Manual Consent Application.

  26. The form may initially still appear in the list of forms missing a disclaimer.

  27. Refresh the page.

  28. If the form disappears from the missing-disclaimer list, the disclaimer has been successfully recognized.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can I edit an existing Facebook Lead Form?
Some published Facebook Lead Forms may not be editable. If the form cannot be edited, create a new Facebook Lead Form in Meta Business Suite and add the Voice AI disclaimer before publishing.


Q: Where should I add the Voice AI disclaimer in Meta Business Suite?
Add the disclaimer in the Privacy Policy section of the Facebook Lead Form using Add Custom Disclaimer.


Q: Can I add the disclaimer directly in MxP?
No. Facebook Lead Forms must be edited in Meta Business Suite. MxP can help you access the correct Meta area through Update Consent, but the form changes must be made in Meta.


Q: Do I need to map the Facebook Lead Form after publishing it?
Yes. After the form is published in Meta Business Suite, return to MxP and map the form fields under Settings → Integrations → Facebook → Form Field Mapping.


Q: Why does the form still show as missing a disclaimer after I publish it?
The form may still appear until the page is refreshed. After mapping the form, return to Manual Consent Application and refresh the page.


Q: How do I know the disclaimer was recognized?
If the Facebook Lead Form no longer appears in the missing-disclaimer list after refreshing, MxP has recognized the disclaimer.


Q: Can the Facebook Lead Form have more than one disclaimer?
Yes, Meta forms can include multiple disclaimers, but the Voice AI disclaimer must be added in the Privacy Policy section so it can be recognized properly.


Q: Does disclaimer recognition guarantee that outbound Voice AI calls will always go through?
No. The form disclaimer is one consent requirement. Outbound Voice AI calls must still meet all other applicable Voice AI outbound calling requirements.
