Import Products / Price From Stripe

Importing products from Stripe into MxP allows you to use your existing subscription products without recreating them manually. You can import eligible recurring prices and make them available for use in MxP funnels, websites, and supported payment experiences. This guide explains how to import Stripe products, understand pricing requirements, and manage imported prices.


Key Benefits of Importing Products from Stripe

  • Save Time: Import existing Stripe subscription products instead of creating them from scratch.

  • Keep Your Pricing Consistent: Bring your existing Stripe recurring prices into MxP.

  • Sell Through MxP: Add imported products to supported funnels, websites, and checkout pages.

  • Simplify Product Management: Manage your imported products alongside other products in MxP.

Before You Begin

Before importing products, make sure your Stripe account is connected to MxP and your products meet the import requirements.

What you'll need:

  • A Stripe account connected to your MxP sub-account.

  • At least one recurring subscription product in Stripe.

  • A subscription price created in Stripe's Live mode.

Which Stripe prices can you import?

​Stripe Price TypeCan It Be Imported?
Monthly or yearly subscriptionsYes
Other recurring subscription pricesYes
One-time pricesNo
Metered or usage-based pricesNo
Test-mode pricesNo
Prices already imported into MxPNo, they are already available in MxP


Important: Only recurring subscription prices can be imported from Stripe. If you want to sell a one-time product, you'll need to create it directly in MxP.

How to Import Products and Prices from Stripe

Step 1: Open the Products Page

  1. Log in to your MxP account.

  2. Switch to the sub-account where you want to import the products.

  3. Go to Payments → Products.

  4. Click Import From Stripe.

Step 2: Find Your Stripe Product

  1. In the import window, search for the product you want to import.

  2. Find the product in the search results.

  3. Review the available recurring prices.

  4. Select the product and price you want to import.

Tip: Can't find your product? Make sure it has a recurring price in Stripe's Live mode and hasn't already been imported into MxP.


Step 3: Import Your Product

  1. Confirm you've selected the correct product and price.

  2. Click Import.

  3. Repeat the process for any additional eligible products or prices you want to import.

Step 4: Check Your Imported Product

After importing, you can find your product in your MxP product list.

  1. Return to Payments → Products.

  2. Find the product you just imported.

  3. Open the product to review its details.

  4. Confirm that the correct recurring price is listed.

That's it! Your Stripe subscription product is now available in MxP




How to Use Your Imported Products

After importing a Stripe product, you can add it to a supported MxP funnel or website to start selling your subscriptions.

To add an imported product to a funnel:

  1. Go to Sites → Funnels.

  2. Open the funnel where you want to sell your subscription.

  3. Select the funnel step containing your order form.

  4. Open the Products tab.

  5. Add your imported Stripe product and select the appropriate price.

  6. Save your changes and test the checkout before publishing.

Tip: If your product has multiple subscription prices, make sure you select the correct price for the offer you're selling.

What Happens After You Import a Product?

When you import a Stripe product, MxP creates the product and its selected recurring price in your MxP product list.

Here's what else you should know:

  • Your product is added to MxP: You can find it under Payments → Products.

  • Your recurring price is included: The selected Stripe subscription price is added to the product.

  • A test version is created: MxP also creates a corresponding product and price in Stripe Test mode.

  • PayPal products may also be created: If PayPal is connected, MxP creates a corresponding product and price in PayPal.

Important: Changes made directly to prices in Stripe do not automatically update the prices in MxP.

How to Update Prices for Imported Stripe Products

If you need to change the price of an imported product, the steps depend on where the product was originally created.

Understanding the difference helps you avoid displaying outdated prices in your MxP checkout pages.

Products Created Directly in Stripe

If you originally created the product in Stripe and later imported it into MxP:

  • You can update supported product pricing in MxP.

  • MxP creates a new price in Stripe when you update the pricing.

  • If you create a new price directly in Stripe, you'll need to import that price into MxP.

Products Created in MxP

If you originally created the product in MxP:

  • MxP automatically creates the corresponding pricing information in Stripe.

  • When you change the price in MxP, a new price is created in Stripe.

  • Changes made directly in Stripe won't automatically appear in MxP.

Products Created Through the SaaS Configurator

If your product was created using the agency-level SaaS Configurator, it will have an Agency Plan label in MxP.

These products work a little differently:

  • SaaS Configurator prices cannot be edited directly from MxP Payments.

  • If you create an updated price in Stripe, you'll need to import the new price into your selling sub-account.

  • After importing the updated price, select it in the appropriate funnel or website checkout settings.


Important: This process applies to recurring products only. Before making changes to subscription pricing, check where the product was originally created

 One-time prices cannot be imported from Stripe and must be created directly in MxP. 

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why can't I find my Stripe product when trying to import it?

Your product might not appear if it has a one-time or metered price, is in Test mode, or has already been imported. Check your product's pricing settings in Stripe and confirm you're using the correct connected account.

Q: Can I import one-time products from Stripe?

No. Only recurring subscription prices are supported. You can create one-time products directly in MxP under Payments → Products.

Q: Can I import more than one subscription price for the same product?

Yes, provided the prices meet the import requirements. Select and import the eligible prices you want to use in MxP.

Q: What happens if I change a product's price in Stripe after importing it?

The change won't automatically appear in MxP. If you've created a new recurring price in Stripe, import that new price into MxP and update the checkout where needed.

Q: Does importing a product automatically add it to my funnel?

No. After importing, you'll need to add the product to your funnel or website checkout.

Q: Will importing a product also import my existing Stripe customers and subscriptions?

No. Importing products and prices does not automatically transfer existing customers or subscriptions into MxP.
