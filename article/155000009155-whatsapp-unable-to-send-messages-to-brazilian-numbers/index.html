WhatsApp: Unable to Send Messages to Brazilian Numbers

How to resolve country-based messaging restrictions when sending WhatsApp messages to contacts in Brazil.

Error: This error is related to a country-based messaging restriction. Currently, only businesses with their Primary Business Location set to Brazil are eligible to send messages to Brazilian numbers.

Table of Contents

What Causes This Error How to Fix It Reference Links Frequently Asked Questions

What Causes This Error

Meta enforces country-based messaging restrictions on the WhatsApp Business Platform. Under this policy, only businesses whose Primary Business Location is set to Brazil in their Meta Business Suite settings are permitted to send WhatsApp messages to Brazilian phone numbers.

This error typically occurs because the Primary Business Location field in Business Info settings has not been set, or has been set to a country other than Brazil.

✘Primary Business Location is not set in Meta Business Suite.
✘Primary Business Location is set to a country other than Brazil.

How to Fix It

1

Open Business Info Settings

Go to Meta Business Suite → Settings → Business Info.

If you cannot locate the option directly, use this link to open the Primary Business Location editor: https://business.facebook.com/settings/info?edit_pbl=true

2

Update the Primary Business Location

Locate the Primary Business Location field and set it to Brazil. Save the changes.

3

Wait and Retry

Wait a few minutes for the change to propagate across Meta's systems, then attempt to send the WhatsApp message again.

Once the Primary Business Location is set to Brazil and the change has propagated, your messages to Brazilian numbers should send successfully.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why do I need to set my Primary Business Location to Brazil just to message Brazilian contacts?

This is a Meta policy enforcement. Meta restricts certain country-to-country messaging on the WhatsApp Business Platform to comply with local regulations and messaging policies. Setting the Primary Business Location to Brazil confirms that your business operates there and is eligible to reach Brazilian users.

Q: I updated the Primary Business Location but the error is still appearing. What should I do?

Changes to the Primary Business Location can take a few minutes to propagate across Meta's systems. Wait at least 5 minutes after saving the update before retrying. If the error persists after 15 minutes, contact our support team with your Location ID and a screenshot of the error.

Q: I cannot find the Primary Business Location field in Business Info. What should I do?

Use the direct link https://business.facebook.com/settings/info?edit_pbl=true to open the Primary Business Location editor directly. Make sure you are logged into the correct Meta Business account before opening the link.

Q: Will changing the Primary Business Location affect my ability to message contacts in other countries?

This depends on Meta's country-specific messaging policies at the time. Consult Meta's official documentation or contact Meta Support directly if you are concerned about the impact on messaging to contacts in other regions.

Q: Does this restriction apply to other countries as well?

Yes. Meta may apply similar country-based messaging restrictions to other regions. If you encounter a comparable error when messaging contacts in a different country, the same resolution applies — update the Primary Business Location to the relevant country in your Meta Business Suite settings.
