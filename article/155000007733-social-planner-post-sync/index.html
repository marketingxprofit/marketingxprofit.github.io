What is Instagram Post Sync in Social Planner?


Instagram Post Sync is a feature that automatically imports your existing Instagram posts into MxP’s Social Planner. It enables you to view, manage, and analyze both past and future content from a single platform. By syncing recent posts and updating engagement data regularly, it ensures your reporting remains accurate while reducing the need for manual content recreation.

Key Benefits of Instagram Post Sync


  • Centralized Content Management: View past and scheduled posts in one unified calendar.

  • Improved Reporting Accuracy: Access up-to-date engagement metrics without manual tracking.

  • Time Savings: Eliminate the need to recreate Instagram posts manually.

  • Better Performance Visibility: Analyze how your content is performing over time.

  • Streamlined Workflow: Manage everything from a single dashboard without switching platforms.

Instagram Post Sync Settings and Behavior


Automatically syncing your Instagram content ensures your recent posts are available inside Social Planner without any manual intervention. This keeps your content library complete and up to date.


  • Syncs posts from the last 30 days automatically.

  • Supports images, videos, carousels, and reels.

  • Imports content directly into your Social Planner calendar.

  • Insights are not real-time and refresh every 24 hours.

  • Due to Instagram API limitations:

    • Location tags, collaborator details, and mentions may not be available.

    • Deleted posts on Instagram will still appear in Social Planner (sync is one-way).

    • Supports Instagram Professional accounts connected via Facebook Login or Instagram Login.

How To Enable Instagram Post Sync 


Proper setup ensures your Instagram posts sync correctly and your analytics remain accurate. Following these steps helps establish a seamless connection and syncing process.


Method 1: Enable Post Sync While Connecting a Social Account


You can enable Post Sync directly while connecting your Instagram account.


  1. Go to Marketing > Social Planner.

  2. Click + Socials.



  3. In the Connect Socials popup, enable the toggle: Sync Posts Automatically.

  4. Click Connect Instagram and finish linking your account.

  5. Once enabled, Social Planner will begin syncing your Facebook posts automatically.



Method 2: Enable Post Sync from Social Planner Settings


If your Instagram account is already connected, you can enable Post Sync later.


  1. Navigate to Marketing > Social Planner.

  2. Click on Settings.



  3. Go to Social Accounts > Instagram.

  4. Enable the Sync Posts toggle for the page you want.

  5. The system will start syncing posts in the background.




  6. Once sync is enabled, open Planner to review synced posts.



  7. Open Statistics to review synced performance data.



  8. If you see a disconnect or expired-access message, follow the reconnect prompt to resume syncing.

Platform-Specific Support

LinkedIn (Beta)

LinkedIn Post Sync supports LinkedIn Organization Pages.

Once enabled, Social Planner automatically imports your published LinkedIn posts, allowing you to manage historical content and engagement from a single dashboard.


Supported

  • Published LinkedIn posts
  • Historical post sync
  • Reactions
  • Comments
  • Shares


Limitations

  • Supports LinkedIn Organization Pages only
  • Personal LinkedIn profiles are not supported
  • Insights refresh every 24 hours
  • PDF, Event, Poll, and Article posts sync successfully but do not display in-app previews
  • Mentions, hashtag highlighting, and tagged-user details are unavailable because of LinkedIn API limitations
  • Deleted LinkedIn posts remain visible inside Social Planner
  • Sync is one-way (LinkedIn → Social Planner)

TikTok (Beta)

TikTok Post Sync supports both Personal and Business accounts.

After connecting your account, Social Planner automatically imports your published TikTok videos so they appear alongside your other social content.

Supported

  • Published TikTok videos
  • Historical post sync
  • Likes
  • Comments
  • Shares
  • Views


Limitations

  • Supports TikTok Personal and Business accounts
  • Insights refresh every 24 hours
  • Videos are displayed using TikTok's hosted player
  • Raw video files cannot be downloaded because of TikTok API restrictions
  • Synced TikTok videos cannot be cloned into new posts
  • Location tags, tagged users, and music details are unavailable because of TikTok API limitations
  • Deleted TikTok posts remain visible inside Social Planner
  • Sync is one-way (TikTok → Social Planner)


Google Business Profile (GBP)

Google Business Profile Post Sync lets you automatically import your published GBP posts into Social Planner, so you can manage historical and new content from a single dashboard.

Once enabled through Labs, Social Planner syncs your supported Google Business Profile posts and refreshes them automatically every 24 hours.

Supported

  • Standard Posts
  • Event Posts
  • Offer Posts
  • Call-to-Action Posts
  • Historical post sync
  • Summary text
  • Images and media
  • Call-to-action buttons
  • Event details
  • Offer details
  • Automatic daily sync
  • Sync status and reconnect notifications


Limitations

  • Available only for Google Business Profile accounts
  • Feature must be enabled through Labs for each location
  • By default, posts from the last 30 days are synced. If there aren't enough recent posts, older posts (up to 365 days) are imported automatically
  • Insights refresh every 24 hours
  • Real-time sync is not supported
  • Cloning is supported only for text-only and single-image posts
  • Multi-image and video posts cannot be cloned due to composer limitations
  • Engagement metrics are limited to data provided by the Google Business Profile API
  • Deleted posts on Google Business Profile continue to appear in Social Planner
  • Sync is one-way (Google Business Profile → Social Planner)



Threads

Threads Post Sync lets you automatically import your existing Threads posts into Social Planner, so you can manage historical content, monitor engagement, and repurpose posts from a single dashboard.

Once enabled through Labs, Social Planner syncs your supported Threads posts and refreshes them automatically every 24 hours.

Supported

  • Text posts
  • Image posts
  • Video posts
  • Carousel posts
  • GIF posts
  • Poll posts
  • Historical post sync
  • Caption text
  • Media and thumbnail previews
  • Location tags
  • Views
  • Likes
  • Replies
  • Reposts
  • Quotes
  • Shares
  • Clone supported Threads posts
  • Automatic daily sync
  • Sync progress and status notifications
  • Reconnect notifications

Limitations

  • Available only for Threads accounts
  • Feature must be enabled through Labs for each location
  • Posts from the last 30 days are synced by default. If there aren't enough recent posts, older posts (up to 365 days) may be imported automatically
  • Sync runs every 24 hours
  • Real-time sync is not supported
  • Cloning is supported for text, image, video, carousel, and GIF posts
  • Polls can be viewed but cannot be cloned because poll creation is not currently supported in the Threads composer
  • Polls may display a “Poll content is not supported in preview” message
  • Text file attachments are not supported for sync or preview
  • Engagement metrics are limited to data available through the Threads API
  • Deleted Threads posts continue to appear in Social Planner
  • Sync is one-way (Threads → Social Planner)

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: How far back does Instagram Post Sync import posts?

The system automatically syncs posts from the last 30 days.


Q: Are engagement metrics updated in real time?

No, metrics such as likes and comments refresh every 24 hours.


Q: Why are some details like tags or collaborators missing?
Due to Instagram API limitations, certain data such as location tags, mentions, and collaborator details may not be available.


Q: What happens if I delete a post on Instagram?
Deleted posts will still remain visible in Social Planner because syncing is one-way.


Q: Do I need a specific type of Instagram account?
Yes, only Instagram Professional accounts (Business or Creator) are supported.


Q: Where can I see the synced posts?
Synced posts appear in your Social Planner calendar alongside scheduled content.


Q: What should I do if my posts are not syncing?
Check your account connection and reconnect Instagram if prompted.
