What Are AI Tools in MxP?

MxP AI tools help users create, communicate, automate, analyze, and take action across the platform. Some tools are designed for logged-in team members, while others interact directly with leads and customers or run automatically when a business event occurs.

The current AI ecosystem includes in-app copilots, customer-facing chat and voice agents, agent-building platforms, workflow-generation tools, website and email builders, content and review tools, prompt-testing utilities, and a centralized Knowledge Base used by supported AI products.

2

Key Benefits of MxP AI

Using the right AI product can reduce repetitive work while improving how teams create assets, respond to customers, manage data, and build automation.

  • Faster Creation: Generate workflows, websites, emails, social content, images, and other assets from prompts.
  • Scalable Customer Engagement: Use Conversation AI and Voice AI to support customer conversations, qualification, and appointment booking.
  • Autonomous Operations: Use Managed Agents to respond to business events and perform supported CRM or connected-app actions.
  • Grounded Answers: Connect supported AI tools to a Knowledge Base containing approved business information.
  • Safer Testing: Use Prompt Optimizers, previews, testing panels, and version history before changing production behavior.
  • Flexible Adoption: Choose free, included, subscription-based, or pay-per-use AI products according to the location's needs.

AI Assistants and Customer-Facing Agents

These products help users complete work directly or engage leads and customers through messaging and phone conversations.

Ask AI

Ask AI is MxP's built-in copilot for logged-in users. It can answer questions, retrieve platform data, create content and assets, manage supported records, run bulk actions, and coordinate supported tasks across many MxP modules through typed or voice prompts.

Learn more about Ask AI Assistant

Conversation AI

Conversation AI is a customer-facing messaging agent. It can answer questions, qualify leads, collect information, assist with appointment booking, and respond on supported communication channels. Response modes include Off, Suggestive, and Auto-Pilot, allowing teams to choose between manual review and automated handling.

Learn more about Conversation AI

Conversation AI Prompt Optimizer

Prompt Optimizer tests a cloned Conversation AI agent against realistic scenarios without changing the production prompt. It runs customer-style chats, evaluates outcomes, creates prompt variations, and shows a prompt comparison before an approved version is applied. Availability: Labs.

Learn more about Conversation AI Prompt Optimizer


Voice AI

Voice AI uses AI-powered voice agents to answer inbound calls, place supported outbound calls, collect information, qualify leads, book appointments, use business knowledge, and trigger supported actions. Voice AI can also support multilingual conversations, working hours, transfers, analytics, recordings, and call logs.

Learn more about Voice AI Agents

Voice AI Prompt Optimizer

Voice AI Prompt Optimizer runs real scenario-based test calls, evaluates expected outcomes, records tool activity, and generates prompt variations. The live agent remains unchanged until a user reviews the results and selects Use Prompt.

Learn more about Voice AI Prompt Optimizer

AI Agent Builders and Autonomous Agents

MxP provides both a conversational agent-building experience and a visual flow environment. Choose the approach that matches how much manual control you need.

Managed Agents

Managed Agents, formerly Super Agents, let users create autonomous CRM agents by describing the desired role in plain language. MxP generates the underlying configuration, including triggers, Knowledge Base access, CRM actions, and supported connected tools. Users can refine the agent through conversation, test it, and publish it when ready.

Learn more about Managed Agents

Agent Studio

Agent Studio is the visual, node-based environment for maintaining existing flow-based AI agents. It supports node editing, Knowledge Base and web-search tools, APIs, MCP tools, conditional routing, variables, testing, and version control.

Current direction: Managed Agents is the recommended experience for creating new agents. Agent Studio remains available for editing, testing, and maintaining existing flow-based agents.

Learn more about flow-based agents in Agent Studio

AI for Workflows and Automation

Workflow AI helps users build and modify standard workflows faster, while specific Workflow AI actions and external models can add AI reasoning or content generation inside an automation.

Workflow AI Builder

Workflow AI Builder converts a plain-language goal into a complete workflow with triggers, actions, and structure. Users can refine the result through conversational edits, point-and-edit controls, follow-up prompts, and supported workflow-setting updates.

Learn more about Workflow AI Builder

Workflow AI Actions and External Models

Workflow AI also includes specialized actions such as Decision Maker, Intent Detection, Summarize Text, and Translate. These actions can be priced separately from the free Workflow AI Builder experience. External models used through workflow actions are billed according to the applicable model or provider.

Do not treat all Workflow AI usage as one pricing category. Workflow AI Builder and Generate with AI can be included with fair-use limits, while premium actions and external models may have separate charges.

AI for Websites, Email, and Content

MxP provides different AI creation experiences depending on whether you are building within an existing module or creating a broader interactive project.

Funnel & Website AI

Funnel & Website AI creates and edits pages inside the standard builder using guided inputs, prompts, links, or image references. Users can refine pages through chat and review AI-assisted SEO suggestions before publishing. Availability: Labs.

Learn more about Funnel & Website AI

Email AI

Email AI is a guided, brand-aware experience inside Email Marketing. It can use Brand Board styling, generate an email from a prompt or reference, recommend layouts, support conversational edits, and preserve version history. Availability: Labs.

Learn more about Email AI

Content AI

Content AI generates and refines text and images across supported MxP content surfaces. Common uses include social posts, blogs, emails, website copy, headlines, and marketing assets. The available controls and placement depend on the builder where Content AI is used.

See Content AI in Social Planner  |  Create blog posts with AI

AI Studio

AI Studio is a conversational workspace for building websites, landing pages, forms, booking experiences, and interactive web projects. It can generate layouts, copy, images, and code-level changes, then publish to a preview or custom domain. Availability: Labs.

Learn more about AI Studio

AI Studio and Agent Studio are separate products. AI Studio creates web experiences. Agent Studio manages AI agent logic and tools.

Knowledge Base and Reviews AI

The Knowledge Base gives supported AI products approved business context, while Reviews AI helps businesses manage and understand customer feedback.

Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base is the centralized area for managing AI-ready source content. Supported sources include websites, FAQs, tables, rich text, and uploaded files. AI products that use Knowledge Base retrieval can search these sources to provide more grounded, business-specific answers.

Learn more about the Knowledge Base

Reviews AI

Reviews AI helps teams generate suggested responses or automate review replies. Drip Mode can process older unreplied reviews gradually instead of publishing a large backlog at once.

Learn more about Reviews AI

AI Plans, Pricing, and Availability

MxP AI is available through Pay-Per-Use, AI Employee Growth, and AI Employee Unlimited. Some products are included on every plan, while others have monthly allowances, fair-use coverage, or token-based charges.

PlanCurrent Base PriceGeneral Model
Pay-Per-UseNo monthly AI subscriptionSupported usage is billed by token, action, response, minute, image, word count, or other applicable unit.
AI Employee Growth$50/month per enabled locationIncludes defined monthly allowances across the AI suite, with overages billed at applicable rates.
AI Employee Unlimited$97/month per enabled locationIncludes unlimited or increased included usage for selected products, subject to fair use.

Current examples of product-level coverage include:

  • Conversation AI and Voice AI: Growth includes defined monthly allowances; Unlimited includes unlimited usage subject to fair use.
  • Reviews AI and Content AI: Unlimited on Growth and Unlimited, subject to fair use.
  • Prompt Optimizers: Growth includes defined message or minute allowances; Unlimited includes unlimited usage subject to fair use.
  • Ask AI and AI Studio: Usage is included on Growth, with 3x included usage on Unlimited.
  • Managed Agents: 100 runs/month on Growth and 1,000 runs/month on Unlimited; token-based on Pay-Per-Use.
  • Agent Studio: Pay-per-use on every plan.
  • Funnel & Website AI: Free with a 1,000-prompt daily limit per location.
  • Email AI and Knowledge Base: Included at no additional cost, subject to fair use.
  • Workflow AI: Builder and Generate with AI are free with daily limits; premium actions and external models can create separate charges.
  • Phone System charges: Remain separate from Voice AI plan coverage.

For agency access, rebilling, and reselling instructions, see AI Employee: Access, Rebilling and Reselling.

How To Get Started with MxP AI

A structured rollout helps ensure the AI product matches the business goal, has the right data and permissions, and performs safely before it reaches customers or runs automatically.

  1. Define the job. Decide whether the goal is content creation, internal assistance, customer messaging, phone automation, workflow building, or autonomous task execution.
  2. Select the product. Use the comparison table in this article to choose the tool designed for that job.
  3. Confirm access. Check user permissions, plan coverage, Labs requirements, phone requirements, connected channels, and any required integrations.
  4. Prepare business context. Configure Brand Voice, Knowledge Bases, prompts, approved content, calendars, workflows, and connected tools as needed.
  5. Review pricing. Confirm current plan inclusions, limits, overages, token costs, and separate communication charges.
  6. Test with sample data. Use available previews, Prompt Optimizers, testing panels, test contacts, and non-production resources.
  7. Monitor and improve. Review logs, transcripts, recordings, execution history, generated assets, and customer outcomes after launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What AI tools does MxP currently offer?
The current pricing inventory includes Ask AI, Conversation AI, both Conversation and Voice Prompt Optimizers, Voice AI, Agent Studio, Managed Agents, Workflow AI, Funnel & Website AI, Email AI, Knowledge Base, Content AI, Reviews AI, and AI Studio.
Q: What is the difference between Ask AI, Conversation AI, and Managed Agents?
Ask AI is a user-initiated in-app copilot. Conversation AI handles live lead and customer conversations. Managed Agents run custom, event-driven business processes with a higher level of autonomy.
Q: What is the difference between AI Studio and Agent Studio?
AI Studio builds websites, pages, forms, and other web experiences. Agent Studio manages visual AI agent logic, tools, routing, variables, testing, and versions.
Q: Which tool can create a workflow from a prompt?
Workflow AI Builder can generate a complete workflow from a natural-language request and then refine it through conversational or targeted edits.
Q: Which AI tools can use a Knowledge Base?
Supported tools such as Conversation AI, Voice AI, Agent Studio, and Managed Agents can retrieve approved source content from a connected Knowledge Base. Availability depends on the product and configuration.
Q: Is every AI tool included with AI Employee Unlimited?
No. Unlimited includes expanded or unlimited coverage for selected products, but Agent Studio remains pay-per-use and other products have their own allowances, run limits, or usage windows.
Q: Are Workflow AI and external AI models the same thing?
No. Workflow AI Builder is MxP's native workflow-generation and editing experience. External models are selected through specific workflow actions or integrations and can have separate provider-based charges.
Q: Why might an AI feature not appear in my account?
The feature may require a specific user permission, plan, Labs toggle, sub-account setting, phone provider, connected channel, or staged rollout. Review the product-specific setup article for exact requirements.
Q: Should AI-generated content and actions be reviewed?
Yes. Review prompts, generated content, customer-facing answers, tool permissions, actions, links, pricing claims, and business information before publishing or enabling automated behavior.
Q: Where can I find the latest AI pricing?
Use the AI Product Pricing article and confirm the current in-app pricing before quoting costs or configuring client billing.
