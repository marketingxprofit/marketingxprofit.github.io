MxP Achieves SOC 2 Type II Certification



At MxP, trust is the foundation of everything we build. Today we are proud to announce a major milestone. MxP has officially achieved SOC 2 Type II accreditation.


This certification is more than a badge on our website. It reflects months of hard work, rigorous audits, and a complete focus on protecting the data you entrust to us every day. We underwent a grueling audit process to achieve this so that you can enjoy the benefits of a secure, verified platform.

What is SOC 2 Type II?


SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2) is a voluntary compliance standard created by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA). It outlines how organizations should manage customer data based on five specific criteria.


We were accredited on Security, Confidentiality, and Availability.


A Type I report looks at a company’s security controls at a single point in time. A Type II report is much stricter. It signals that we are the "real deal." We are an enterprise-grade platform that has proven its capabilities. It confirms that our security controls are designed correctly and have operated effectively over time.

Why this matters for agencies


Agencies manage sensitive data for clients. Whether you serve local businesses or large enterprises, your clients need to know the platform powering their operations is secure.


This accreditation is a tool for you, not just a win for us


  1. Compete with the "Big Guys": You can now show best-in-class security credentials for the platform running your business. This helps you compete with larger entities.

  2. Unlock Enterprise opportunities: Most enterprise-level clients won't even entertain a conversation without a SOC 2 report. It is often a mandatory requirement in their procurement process. This certification removes that barrier, allowing you to close bigger deals.

  3. A stronger moat: The process of achieving SOC 2 compliance forces a company to identify and fix vulnerabilities they didn't know they had. By doing this work, we have strengthened our "moat" against data breaches and cyberattacks, giving you a harder, more resilient shell for your business.

  4. Easier compliance: When your platform is certified, compliance conversations with prospects become significantly easier.

A word from leadership


Abhishek Tripathi, Sr. Director, Cyber Security & IT Operations, emphasizes that this milestone proves our capability to keep customer data safe consistently, not just during an audit.


"We are very proud of being the organization which delivers on all fronts for our customers to enable them to use our platform and kick above their weight with such best-in-class credentials," says Tripathi. "SOC 2 gets them access to financial industry and other regulated prospects that need higher security and trust factors."


Tripathi adds that SOC 2 Type II is the height of security attestation. It means we are not only secure at a single point in time, but we are capable of being secure consistently.

A journey of dedication


Hitting this standard so quickly shows our team's focus. We started our Information Security Management Program in April 2025. Through the hard work of our Cybersecurity, Engineering, IT, People, and Platform teams, we stayed ready for a SOC 2 audit by the end of the year.


"Personally, I think it is a great achievement that an organization as young and focused could achieve this in such a short time," Tripathi notes.

Moving forward


We are celebrating this milestone, but we know security is a journey rather than a destination. This certification validates that our controls and processes are robust. However, our work does not stop here.


We view the trust our customers put in us as a responsibility instead of a checkbox. We will continue to scale globally and build powerful tools for agencies and the businesses they serve. Security will remain a foundation. It will never be an afterthought.
