Error: This Phone Number Is Already Registered to a WhatsApp Account

Understand what this error means, identify your current WhatsApp setup, and choose the correct disconnection, migration or reconnection path before changing or deleting anything.

What does this error mean?

You may encounter the error "This phone number is already registered to a WhatsApp account" when attempting to connect or reconnect your WhatsApp number to MxP.

This generally indicates that the phone number is already associated with an existing WhatsApp registration or Business Platform integration, preventing the requested onboarding process from continuing.

Depending on your current WhatsApp setup, you may need to disconnect an existing integration, complete a supported migration, or follow the appropriate reconnection process.

Important

If you are using WhatsApp Coexistence, you may be able to continue using the WhatsApp Business app alongside MxP. Do not delete your WhatsApp Business account without first identifying the correct onboarding or reconnection procedure.

Confirm your intended setup before removing an account

If you want to keep using the WhatsApp Business app alongside MxP, review the WhatsApp Coexistence guide before following any account-deletion instructions.

Two different actions

Disconnecting a Business Platform connection is different from selecting Delete Account in the mobile app. Deleting the mobile account permanently removes its chat history.

If a previously working Coexistence connection stops syncing and cannot reconnect, contact MxP Support. Share a screenshot of the full error, the affected sub-account, the time and timezone of the attempt, and the steps you have already completed. Support will guide you through the applicable reconnection process.

Why am I seeing this error?

You may encounter this error for one of the following reasons:

  • Existing WhatsApp registration: The phone number is already registered with WhatsApp Messenger or the WhatsApp Business app.
  • Existing Business Platform connection: The number is associated with another WhatsApp Business Platform provider or integration.
  • Previous MxP connection: The number may still be associated with an earlier MxP location or WhatsApp integration.
  • Incomplete disconnection: A previous integration was disconnected, but its registration or partner association may not have been fully updated.
  • Incorrect onboarding method: The selected onboarding process may not match the number's existing registration or intended Coexistence setup.

The appropriate resolution depends on how your phone number is currently connected.

How to fix this issue

1
Identify your current WhatsApp setup

Before disconnecting or deleting anything, determine which scenario applies to your phone number.

Scenario A: You are using WhatsApp Coexistence

Your phone number is connected, or was previously connected, to a Business Platform integration while continuing to operate through the WhatsApp Business app.

If you want to reconnect the number to MxP while retaining access to the WhatsApp Business app, follow the Coexistence-specific guidance in Step 2.

Scenario B: Your number is connected to another WhatsApp Business Platform provider

Your number may already be registered with another provider or associated with a previous MxP location.

Review Step 3 to determine the appropriate disconnection or migration process.

Scenario C: You are switching from the WhatsApp mobile app to a standard API connection

If you intend to use a standard WhatsApp Business Platform connection rather than Coexistence, the existing mobile-app registration may need to be addressed before onboarding.

Review Step 4 before making any changes.

2
For WhatsApp Coexistence users

WhatsApp Coexistence allows eligible businesses to use the WhatsApp Business app alongside a supported Business Platform integration such as MxP.

If you previously connected your number through Coexistence and are attempting to reconnect it, you may need to remove the previous Business Platform connection before starting a new onboarding attempt.

Important

Disconnecting a Business Platform integration is different from deleting your WhatsApp Business account. If your goal is to continue using the WhatsApp Business app, do not select Delete Account as a general troubleshooting step.

What should you do?
  1. Confirm that your phone number is active in the WhatsApp Business app.
  2. Check whether the number is still connected to a previous Business Platform integration.
  3. Review the official WhatsApp Coexistence disconnection instructions to determine whether the existing integration needs to be removed.
  4. Once the previous connection has been properly disconnected, follow the supported MxP Coexistence onboarding process.
  5. If the same error continues, contact MxP Support before attempting further account changes.
Note

The exact reconnection steps may vary depending on the number's current registration and partner association. Disconnecting an integration does not automatically guarantee that a number is immediately eligible for a new Coexistence connection.

3
If your number is connected to another WhatsApp provider

If your phone number is already connected to another WhatsApp Business Platform provider, you may encounter this error when attempting to onboard it to MxP.

What should you do?
  1. Identify the provider or platform currently associated with your WhatsApp number.
  2. Confirm whether you intend to migrate the existing WhatsApp connection or establish a new registration.
  3. Contact your current provider to confirm the supported migration or disconnection procedure.
  4. Follow the appropriate process before attempting onboarding again through MxP.
  5. If the number remains associated with the previous provider, contact MxP Support for further assistance.
Important

Do not delete the existing WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) or phone number registration unless the supported migration or recovery procedure specifically requires it. Deleting or removing a number without confirming the appropriate process may interrupt WhatsApp service or complicate reconnection.

4
If your number is registered in the WhatsApp mobile app

If you are attempting standard WhatsApp API onboarding rather than Coexistence, your existing mobile-app registration may prevent the number from being registered through the selected onboarding method.

Before proceeding, determine whether you want to:

  • Continue using the WhatsApp Business app alongside MxP through Coexistence, where eligible.
  • Move to a standard WhatsApp Business Platform connection without retaining the existing mobile-app setup.
If you want to retain the WhatsApp Business app

Do not delete your account. Review the WhatsApp Coexistence onboarding requirements and follow the supported setup process.

If you intend to move to a standard API connection

Follow the applicable official registration or migration instructions to address the existing WhatsApp registration.

Important

Deleting your WhatsApp account is not a universal requirement for resolving this error. Account deletion can have significant consequences, including loss of account access and chat history. Only consider it when explicitly required by a verified procedure and after reviewing the potential impact.

If you are unsure which method applies, contact MxP Support before proceeding.

5
Allow time for the registration status to update

After completing a supported disconnection or registration change:

  1. Allow WhatsApp's systems time to process the update.
  2. If the error indicates that the number status may take up to 3 minutes to refresh, wait for that period before retrying.
  3. Return to the appropriate MxP onboarding flow.
  4. Avoid repeatedly requesting verification codes or restarting onboarding in quick succession.
Note

The 3-minute period is an indicated refresh time, not a guaranteed completion time. Some registration or account-association changes may require additional investigation.

6
Retry WhatsApp onboarding

Once you have confirmed that the required disconnection or migration steps are complete:

  1. Log in to your MxP sub-account.
  2. Navigate to Settings → WhatsApp.
  3. Start the appropriate WhatsApp onboarding process.
  4. Select the correct connection method based on whether you intend to use Coexistence or a standard API connection.
  5. Complete the required Meta verification and authorization steps.
  6. Confirm that your WhatsApp number is successfully connected.

If the error continues, avoid deleting the account or repeatedly restarting the onboarding process without identifying the cause.

What if the error continues?

If you continue receiving "This phone number is already registered to a WhatsApp account" after following the applicable steps, contact MxP Support.

To help us investigate, please provide:

  • The WhatsApp phone number, including the country code.
  • Your MxP Location ID.
  • A screenshot showing the exact error message.
  • The date, time and timezone of the attempt.
  • The steps you have already completed.
  • Whether the number is currently active in the WhatsApp Business app.
  • Whether the number was previously connected to MxP or another WhatsApp provider.
  • Whether you are attempting standard WhatsApp API onboarding or WhatsApp Coexistence.
  • A screen recording of the onboarding attempt, if available.

Our Support team can review the available connection details and help determine the appropriate next steps. Some account-association or registration issues may require further investigation with the relevant provider or Meta.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to delete my WhatsApp Business account to fix this error?

Not necessarily.

The required action depends on your existing WhatsApp registration and the onboarding method you are attempting.

If you are using WhatsApp Coexistence, deleting your WhatsApp Business account may be unnecessary and could affect your existing WhatsApp data.

Always confirm the correct procedure before deleting an account.

Q: What is the difference between disconnecting a Business Platform integration and deleting a WhatsApp account?

Disconnecting a Business Platform integration removes a platform connection or authorization according to the supported disconnection process.

Deleting a WhatsApp account is a separate action that can affect your WhatsApp registration, account access, and chat history.

These actions are not interchangeable.

Q: Can I continue using the WhatsApp Business app after connecting to MxP?

Yes, if your number is eligible and successfully onboarded through WhatsApp Coexistence.

Standard API onboarding and Coexistence have different requirements. Make sure you select the appropriate setup method.

Q: What if my number was previously connected to another MxP sub-account?

The number may still have an existing registration or integration association.

Before attempting to reconnect it, confirm the previous connection status and follow the supported disconnection or migration process.

Contact MxP Support if you are unable to determine the current association.

Q: Will disconnecting WhatsApp from MxP automatically release my number?

Not necessarily.

The result depends on the integration type and the disconnection procedure used. Some registrations or partner associations may require additional steps.

Q: How long should I wait before retrying onboarding?

If the error message indicates a 3-minute refresh period, wait at least that long after completing the appropriate change.

If the issue continues, additional investigation may be required.

Important reminders

  • Identify your current WhatsApp connection type before troubleshooting.
  • Do not delete your WhatsApp Business account solely because this error appears.
  • Use the supported Coexistence disconnection and reconnection process when retaining the WhatsApp Business app.
  • Confirm existing provider associations before attempting migration or re-registration.
  • Contact MxP Support if you are unsure which steps apply to your account.
Best practice

Following the correct onboarding process helps prevent unnecessary account changes and reduces the risk of disrupting your existing WhatsApp service.
