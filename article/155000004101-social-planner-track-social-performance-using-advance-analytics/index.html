What are Advanced Analytics in the Social Planner?


Advanced Analytics provides a centralized view of your social media performance across supported platforms. You can monitor engagement, compare account performance, and use reporting insights to improve your social media strategy.

Key Benefits of Social Planner Advanced Analytics


Advanced Analytics helps you understand audience engagement and identify opportunities to improve your social media content.


  • Comprehensive Social Media Insights: Track the number of posts, likes, comments, followers, impressions, and reach across many social media platforms from a single dashboard.

  • Visualize your Social Post Performance: Interactive graphs showcase post trends and weekly performance.

  • Engagement Breakdown: Get a detailed view of engagement metrics such as total likes, comments, and shares, broken down per platform.

  • Platform Comparisons: Identify which platform drives more engagement, helping you tailor your content strategy accordingly.

  • Impressions and Post Reach: Measure your content’s visibility with Reach and Impressions tracking.

  • Gender and Age Demographics: See your audience breakdown in terms of age groups and gender, providing valuable data to optimize your content for the right audience.

Where to Find Social Planner Advanced Analytics


To access the analytics dashboard, navigate to Marketing > Social Planner > Statistics.


Supported Social Media Platforms and Limitations


Metric availability varies on each platform. Use the notes below to set expectations and keep analytics flowing without interruption.



Supported Platforms


Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, TikTok, Google Business Profile (GBP), Threads and Bluesky.


Permissions and Reauthentication Alerts


Receive real-time alerts when your connected accounts require re-authentication to access analytics. This ensures continuous access to up-to-date insights without interruptions. Users will be able to see the statistics from the next day / when they reconnect their accounts with upgraded permissions.


Post Specific Statistics


Post Specific statistics are shown in the post details modal (Planner - List View)

  • Platforms supported: Facebook, Instagram, Tiktok personal, LinkedIn page.

  • Platforms to be supported: Youtube, Pinterest.

  • Platforms not supported: Google Business profile, TikTok Business, LinkedIn profile, communities.


Platform‑Specific Notes


TikTok

  • Only BUSINESS profiles are supported

  • May take 1 or 2 days to reflect data due to the TikTok API availability.


Pinterest

  • Takes n-2 days to reflect the correct data. (Similar behaviour is observed on hootsuite and other portals owing to API constraints)


LinkedIn

  • Only Linkedin pages are supported not the Linkedin profiles owing to API constraints.

  • Verify Total followers - Followers data will be available only until n-2 days (n being the current day).


Threads

  • Impressions and Post Reach not supported

  • No follower count, gender data, or advanced engagement metrics

  • T-1 day delay for the metrics except the post count

Bluesky

  • Impressions are supported

  • Follower metrics, demographics, and advanced engagement breakdowns not supported

  • T-1 day delay for the metrics except the post count


Demographics are not supported by any of the newly released Platforms

Filter by Platform and Account


To filter based on accounts, open the dropdown in the upper-left and check the boxes for the accounts you want included in the metrics.




Use the selector bar on the left to filter results based on social media platform. Choose All for a cross‑platform rollup, or select a specific platform (e.g., Threads, Bluesky) to narrow results.


Available Reports and Metrics


Tip: Hover over the tooltip (i) next to any metric for definitions, supported platforms, and time‑range details.



KPI Overview


Track the number of posts, likes, followers, impressions and comments across all platforms at a glance.


  • Number of Posts: Total number of posts published across all selected social accounts.

  • Total Likes: Total likes on posts published across all selected accounts.

  • Total Followers: Total unique followers who joined across all selected social accounts.

  • Impressions: Total unique impressions on published posts across all selected social accounts.

  • Comments: Total count of comments on published posts across all selected social accounts.



Social Post Performance


Visualize your Social Post Performance with an interactive graph, showcasing published posts (bars) alongside daily totals for impressions, likes, and comments (lines) so you can track posting trends and weekly performance at a glance.


  • Bar Graph (Weekly Posts): Show the number of posts published per week across all selected social media platforms.

  • Line Graph (Engagement): The overlaying line graphs represent the total number of interactions (comments, likes, and shares) per day, regardless of when the posts were published.




Link Clicks by Socials


The total number of link clicks from posts published across all selected social accounts by social platform.

 

Important: Tracking is available only for shortened links created through the Social Planner. Click here for more information on the link shortener.



Link Performance


Track the performance of your shortened links across all social platforms, including total clicks per link belonging to a campaign.


Important: Tracking is available only for shortened links created through the Social Planner. Click here for more information on the link shortener.




Number of Posts


This depicts the platform-wise total number of posts published across all selected social accounts. It shows the current week's posts compared to the previous week's.




Engagement


The unique number of likes, comments, and shares received by posts published broken down by platform. The engagement percentage reflects the change in this week's data compared to the previous week.




Impressions


Impressions shows the number of times any published post was displayed on a person's screen. Impression percentage reflects change in this week's data compared to the previous week.




Post Reach


Analyze post reach to understand the number of people who have seen any content from your page/account/about your page on their screen. This includes posts, check-ins, ads, social information from people who interact with your page, and more. The post reach percentage reflects the change in this week's data compared to the previous week. 


For Google Business Profile, this number reflects the total number of Call Clicks, Website Clicks, Food orders, Bookings, Food Menu Clicks and Direction Requests.




Top Performing Posts


Use this report to see your top all-time posts. Top-performing posts are the posts with the highest number of likes among published social posts from the social planner. Click the Eye icon next to any post to view that post within the Social Media platform.




Engagement Stats


Get a detailed view of engagement metrics such as total likes, comments, and shares, broken down per platform. Use the selector ribbon to switch between Total Engagement, Total Followers and Total Likes. This report can be used to identify which platforms best drive engagement, helping you tailor your content strategy accordingly.




Gender Demography


Use this report to dive into detailed demographic data, including the gender distribution (male, female, and unknown) and age range insights. Demographic data is only available for those viewers who has shared the data for calculation to the respective Social Media Platforms.




Important: This metric is not available for Instagram users with fewer than 100 followers.

Important Considerations


Understanding reporting limitations helps you interpret performance data accurately.


  • Metric Availability: Some metrics are unavailable due to platform API restrictions.

  • Reporting Delays: Analytics may take one or more days to update, depending on the platform and metric.

  • Account Requirements: LinkedIn dashboard analytics require Pages, while TikTok dashboard analytics support Business accounts. Post-specific statistics may have different requirements.

  • Instagram Demographics: Demographic insights require at least 100 followers.

  • Account Authorization: Some accounts may require reconnection or updated permissions to continue displaying analytics.

  • Post Sync: Engagement insights for supported synchronized posts refresh approximately every 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: What types of metrics can I track using Social Planner analytics?
You can track various metrics, including engagement rates, post reach, impressions, clicks, and overall performance of your social media content. This data helps you assess the effectiveness of your strategies.


Q: Is there a way to compare the performance of different posts?
Yes, the analytics dashboard allows you to compare the performance of individual posts over time, helping you identify trends and determine which types of content resonate best with your audience.


Q: Can I export the analytics data for reporting purposes?
No, Social Planner currently doesn't provide option to export your analytics but we plan to support this in coming quarters for exporting data in various formats, making it easy to create reports and share insights with your team or stakeholders.


Q:How often is the analytics data updated?
The analytics data is typically updated in real time, allowing you to monitor your social media performance continuously and make timely adjustments to your strategy.


Q: Are there any tips for interpreting the analytics data effectively?
When interpreting analytics data, focus on key performance indicators (KPIs) that align with your goals. Look for patterns over time, compare similar posts, and consider external factors that may influence engagement.


Q: What time range can I view in Social Planner Analytics?
You can currently view analytics data on a weekly basis only.
