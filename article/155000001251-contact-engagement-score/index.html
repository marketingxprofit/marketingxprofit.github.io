Contact Engagement score

Track and prioritize leads using Contact Engagement Score in MxP. This feature assigns points to contacts based on their interactions with your business, such as email engagement, form submissions, appointments, and purchases. By configuring scoring rules, you can identify engaged contacts, improve segmentation, and automate follow-ups.

What is Contact Engagement Score?


Contact Engagement Score measures how actively a contact interacts with your business. MxP uses scoring rules to add or subtract points when specific actions occur, helping you identify contacts who may need more attention.


For example, submitting a form could add 10 points, booking an appointment could add 20 points, and unsubscribing from an email could subtract 15 points.


Note: These are example values. Actual scores depend on your configured rules.

Key Benefits of Contact Engagement Score


Engagement scoring helps sales and marketing teams prioritize contacts based on meaningful interactions rather than relying on guesswork.

  • Lead Prioritization: Identify highly engaged contacts for faster follow-ups.

  • Custom Scoring: Assign positive or negative points to specific contact actions.

  • Better Segmentation: Organize contacts using engagement scores in Smart Lists.

  • Automated Follow-Ups: Trigger workflows based on contact engagement levels.

  • Improved Visibility: Monitor engagement directly from contact records and other supported areas.

Understanding Engagement Scoring Rules


Scoring rules determine which interactions increase or decrease a contact's score. You can configure rules based on supported events and assign point values that reflect their importance to your business.

Draft and Publish Modes


  • Draft: Create or edit scoring rules before making changes live.

  • Publish: Activate configured scoring rules for qualifying interactions.

Where to View Contact Engagement Scores


MxP displays engagement scores in multiple areas, helping teams assess contact activity without switching between tools.

  • Contacts: Open a contact record and locate the Contact Engagement Score dropdown.

  • Conversations: Open a conversation and find Contact Engagement Score in the right-side contact details panel.

  • Opportunities: Use Manage Fields in the pipeline board view to display Engagement Score on opportunity cards.

Using Engagement Scores in Workflows and Smart Lists


Engagement scores can support automated communication and contact segmentation. This helps teams organize leads and respond when contact activity meets defined conditions.

  • Workflow Trigger: Start workflows when contacts meet engagement score conditions.

  • Modify Engagement Score: Add or subtract points using a workflow action.

  • Smart Lists: Filter contacts using Engagement Score with numeric conditions such as Greater Than, Less Than, and Between.


For example, you can create a Smart List of contacts with engagement scores greater than 50 or trigger a sales notification when a contact reaches a specified score.

How to Set Up Contact Engagement Score


Setting up scoring rules allows MxP to measure interactions according to your business priorities. Review your rules before publishing to avoid incorrect or duplicate point adjustments.

  1. Navigate to Settings > Manage Scoring in your sub-account.



  2. Switch to Draft Mode to configure scoring rules.

  3. Click + Add New Rule.



  4. Select an Action Type, such as Form Submitted or Appointment Booked.

  5. Configure the available conditions and assign positive or negative points.

  6. Review the new rule in the Scoring Rules Table.


  7. Switch to Publish Mode to activate your scoring rules.

  8. To modify an existing rule, open its three-dot menu and select Edit or Delete.


After publishing, review engagement scores in contact records as qualifying interactions occur.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can engagement scores trigger workflows?

A: Yes. Use the Contact Engagement Score workflow trigger to automate actions based on supported score conditions.


Q: Can I manually increase or decrease engagement scores?

A: You can adjust scores using the Modify Contact Engagement Score workflow action.


Q: Do engagement scores automatically decrease over time?

A: The existing documentation does not describe automatic score decay. Workflows can be configured to subtract points when defined conditions are met.


Q: Can I create Smart Lists based on engagement scores?

A: Yes. Use the Engagement Score filter in Smart Lists to segment contacts according to numeric score conditions.


Q: Will editing a scoring rule recalculate previous scores?

A: Historical recalculation is not established by the reviewed documentation. Verify the current behavior before modifying rules that affect existing contacts.
