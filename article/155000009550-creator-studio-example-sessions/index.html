Creator Studio: Example Sessions

Getting Started with Creator Studio

Creator Studio is MxP's AI design studio. It creates social posts, ads, flyers, images, and short videos from a single prompt.

This article walks through three example sessions to help you understand how Creator Studio works, what results to expect, and how to make adjustments.

Before You Start

Creator Studio is a Labs feature. An agency admin must enable it for each sub-account.

Enable Creator Studio

  1. Go to Agency Settings → Labs → Sub-Account.

  2. Find Creator Studio and click Activate Feature.

  3. Search for the sub-account, switch Enable on, and click Save.

Access Creator Studio

  1. Open the sub-account.

  2. Press ⌘K (or click Search).

  3. Type Creator Studio and open it.

How to Create a Design

  1. Open Creator Studio.

  2. (Optional) Choose Design, Image, or Video clip under the prompt box.

  3. Describe what you want to create. Include details such as:

    • Business name

    • Offer or promotion

    • Call to action (CTA)

  4. Click Send to generate your content.

  5. Results typically appear within 2–3 minutes.

  6. To make changes:

    • Click Edit on a design to modify individual layers.

    • Type a follow-up message to request changes.

Example 1: Creating an Instagram Post


Prompt Used

Create an Instagram post for BrightSmile Dental Clinic announcing a teeth-whitening offer: 30% off this November. Clean, modern, friendly look with a 'Book now' call to action.

What Happened

Creator Studio automatically selected Social media → Instagram Post and generated three design variations.

All three designs included the clinic name, promotional offer, and call-to-action button.

Example 2: Creating a Facebook Ad


Prompt Used

Create a Facebook ad for a free live webinar: '5 Marketing Automations Every Local Business Needs', Thursday 21 November at 7pm EST, hosted by GrowthLab. Bold, professional look with a 'Register free' call to action.

What Happened

Creator Studio generated three promotional designs in flyer format (8.5 × 11 inches).

It also provided four follow-up suggestions, including Resize to square 1:1 ad format.

Session Details

ItemValue
User Messages1
Tools Used3 design generations
Rendering3 AI-generated flyer designs (8.5 × 11 in) + 4 follow-up suggestions
Platform ActionsSaved as project "GrowthLab Webinar Ad"
Final Result3 webinar promos with title, date, host and "Register free" button; resize to 1:1 before running as a Facebook ad
Total Session Cost$0.93

Result

Three webinar ad designs for GrowthLab

All three designs include the webinar title, date, host, and registration call to action.

Tip: Facebook ad requests may initially generate designs in flyer dimensions. To get the desired format:

  • Click the Resize to square 1:1 ad format suggestion.

  • Alternatively, specify the dimensions directly in your original prompt.

Example 3: Creating a Promotional Video


Prompt Used

Create a short promo video clip for Sweet Crumbs Bakery's festive holiday menu: fresh cinnamon rolls, gingerbread cookies and a Christmas fruit cake. Warm, cozy bakery mood, ending with 'Order now for pickup'.

What Happened

With Video clip selected, Creator Studio generated one 6-second promotional video.

The video contained the following sequence:

  1. Cinnamon rolls

  2. Gingerbread cookies

  3. End card displaying the bakery name

Troubleshooting

If you encounter an issue while using Creator Studio, refer to the following solutions.

ProblemWhat to Do
Creator Studio doesn't appear in the sub-accountEnable Creator Studio in Labs. Refer to the Before You Start section.
Design comes back in the wrong sizeUse the Resize suggestion, or specify the size in your prompt (for example, "square 1:1").
Spelling mistake in a videoRegenerate the video. Text inside generated videos isn't editable.
Video is widescreen when 1:1 was selectedThis is a known issue. Regenerate the video and report the issue to the product team.

Key Takeaways

  • Create multiple content types: Generate social posts, ads, flyers, images, and short videos using natural-language prompts.

  • Customize designs: Edit individual design layers or use follow-up prompts to make changes.

  • Specify dimensions: Include the preferred format or aspect ratio in your prompt to reduce the need for resizing.

  • Review generated content: Always check spelling, layout, and details before publishing or downloading.

  • Regenerate video content when needed: Unlike design layers, text generated inside videos cannot be edited directly.
