Call Reporting

Call Reporting helps you review incoming and outgoing calls, call outcomes, and recorded sources. Use filters to focus on specific phone numbers and reporting periods. This guide explains the report, recordings, exports, and source attribution for number-pool calls.

What Is Call Reporting?

Call Reporting combines summary metrics and individual call records within a MxP sub-account. It helps you assess call activity, review outcomes, and identify recorded sources.

Key Benefits of Call Reporting

Review call activity and source information together to understand customer demand and identify follow-up needs.

  • Call visibility: Compare incoming and outgoing activity, statuses, and durations.


  • Source insight: Identify recorded advertising and referral sources for tracked calls.


  • Flexible analysis: Filter records and export selected columns for further review.

Prerequisites and Limitations

Report access and website call tracking are separate requirements. Confirm the relevant setup before evaluating attribution.

  • Report access: Call Reporting is available with the Founder Plan (formerly Agency Pro).


  • Website attribution: Configure a number pool and install its tracking snippet on the website you want to track. See How to Set Up Call Tracking (Number Pool) for setup instructions.


  • Attribution limits: Source identification depends on available referral and tracking information. Not every call can be assigned to an advertising platform.

How to Use Call Reporting

Choose the reporting period and phone numbers relevant to your review before comparing call activity.

  1. Open the relevant Sub-Account in MxP.


  2. Click Reporting in the left navigation.


  3. Select Call report in the top navigation.


  4. Use the date-range selector to choose the reporting period.


  5. Use the All numbers dropdown to review all numbers or select a specific number.


  6. Review the summary, then use All calls, Incoming, or Outgoing above the call table to focus on the relevant records.

The Source summary and Source type column show recorded attribution, including the Facebook Paid example.

Understand Number-Pool Source Attribution

Improved attribution distinguishes identifiable advertising and AI referrals. Check the Source summary and Source type column for the recorded label.

  • Facebook advertising: Identified Facebook ad calls use Facebook Paid.

  • Other advertising: Platform-specific identification covers TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft (Bing), and Google ads clicked in apps.

  • Tagged links: Ad links using utm_medium=cpc receive the platform name plus Paid, such as Bing Paid.

  • AI referrals: ChatGPT and similar tools appear as distinct sources.
Note: Existing number-pool tracking scripts update automatically. No additional setup is required for this attribution enhancement. First-time number-pool tracking still requires a configured pool and tracking snippet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why do some calls still show Direct?

A call can remain Direct when the browser hides the referral source and the link contains no ad-tracking code.

Q: Will the improved labels update historical calls?

No. The improvements apply to new calls only. Historical calls retain their previously recorded sources.

Q: What happens when a link contains multiple ad-tracking codes?

The first match wins. Platforms are checked in this order: Google → Facebook → TikTok → LinkedIn.

Q: Why can Call Reporting and Agent Reporting show different totals?

Call Reporting organizes tracking around phone numbers, while Agent Reporting uses individual team members. Their filtering and counting criteria differ, so their totals are not necessarily identical.
