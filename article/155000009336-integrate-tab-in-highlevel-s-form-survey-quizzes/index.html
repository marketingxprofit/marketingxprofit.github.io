Integrate Tab in MxP's Form, Survey & Quizzes

Learn how to embed, share, and connect Forms, Surveys, and Quizzes using the Integrate tab in MxP. The centralized experience provides live embed previews, prebuilt QR codes, email integration, and Workflow connections. You can configure and distribute your content without switching between multiple areas of the platform.

What is the Integrate Tab for Forms, Surveys & Quizzes?


The Integrate tab brings all distribution and integration options into one place within the Form, Survey, and Quiz Builders. It replaces the previous Integrate button and provides four integration options.

How to Set Up and Use the Integrate Tab


The Integrate tab allows you to embed, share, email, and connect Forms, Surveys, and Quizzes from one location. Choose the integration option that fits your goal and follow the steps below.


  1. Go to Sites > Forms, Surveys, or Quizzes and open the item you want to integrate.

  2. Select the Integrate tab in the builder.



  3. Choose your preferred integration option:

    • Embed: Select an embed type (Sidebar, Popover, Popup, or Standard), customize settings under General or Advanced, and review the live preview. Click Embed code > Copy code, then paste the code into your website editor and publish.

    • Share: Copy the direct link, select a social sharing option, or customize and download the prebuilt QR code.

    • Email: Select an existing email template or create a new email to include your Form, Survey, or Quiz.

    • Connections: View associated Workflows or click Create Workflow to configure a new automation.



  4. Click Save to save your Form, Survey, or Quiz. The post-save modal provides options to Share, Embed, or Create a Workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can I preview my Form, Survey, or Quiz before embedding it?

Yes. The Embed tab provides live previews for all existing embed options. You can adjust supported settings and see the changes reflected in real time.


Q: Do I need to manually create a QR code for each Form, Survey, or Quiz?

No. MxP automatically provides a prebuilt QR code for each Form, Survey, or Quiz. You can customize and download it directly from the Share tab.


Q: Has the Email integration functionality changed?

No. The existing Email integration functionality remains unchanged. It is now accessible from the dedicated Integrate tab.


Q: Can I see which Workflows are connected to my Form, Survey, or Quiz?

Yes. The Connections tab displays associated Workflows and allows you to initiate the creation of new Workflows directly from the builder.
