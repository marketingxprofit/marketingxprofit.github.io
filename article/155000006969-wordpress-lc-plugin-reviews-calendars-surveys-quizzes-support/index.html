WordPress LC Plugin - Reviews, Calendars, Surveys & Quizzes Support

The MxP (LC) WordPress Plugin now supports four additional MxP products—Review Widgets, Calendars, Surveys, and Quizzes—so you can build fully integrated, conversion‑focused sites without leaving WordPress. Use these instructions to create consistent branding, keep data in sync with MxP, and simplify deployment across page builders like Gutenberg, Elementor, and Divi.

What is the LC WordPress Plugin Support for Reviews, Calendars, Surveys & Quizzes?


The LC Plugin bridges WordPress and MxP so teams can place MxP experiences directly on their sites. With v3.0.13, LC expands beyond prior widgets to include Review Widgets, Calendars, Surveys, and Quizzes. These components render using your MxP configuration while syncing to your connected sub‑account—no separate plugins or manual embed codes required.


Note: For guidance on how to enable and connect the LC Plugin with WordPress, checkout this article on MxP WordPress Plugin.


Key Benefits of Reviews, Calendars, Surveys & Quizzes Support


  • Unified workflow: Manage embeds from the LC Plugin while configuration and data remain in MxP.

  • Less setup friction: Connect your WordPress site to a MxP sub‑account once; supported products are ready to embed.

  • Consistency at scale: Preserve fonts, colors, and brand styles defined in MxP across multiple pages and sites.

  • Fewer plugins to maintain: Reduce code pastes and third‑party dependencies by using official LC blocks/shortcodes.

  • Better collaboration: Non‑technical users can add calendars, surveys, quizzes, and reviews without touching code.

  • Reliable syncing: Submissions, bookings, and reviews flow to MxP so analytics and follow‑ups stay centralized.

Review Widgets


Review Widgets showcase social proof (e.g., customer feedback) on landing pages, pricing pages, and service pages to increase trust and conversion.


  • Build and customize Review Widgets inside MxP as usual.

  • In WordPress → MxP → Reviews, pick the widget to embed and copy its shortcode.

  • Place the block/shortcode within any section or column in your editor.

  • Publish the page; reviews render with your configured filters and styles from MxP.

Tips


  • Feature review carousels on the homepage; use static grids on service pages for scannability.

  • For multiple widgets on one page, place each via its own block/shortcode and arrange within your layout.


Calendars


Calendars remove booking friction by letting visitors schedule directly on your site. Use them on booking pages, footers, or contextually inside service flows.


  • Create or update calendars in MxP (availability, teams, routing, confirmations).

  • In WordPress → MxP → Calendars, select the calendar to embed.

  • Insert the Calendar by pasting the copied shortcode where you want scheduling available.

  • Update and publish the page. Appointments route to the connected MxP sub‑account.


Tips

  • Add calendars near CTAs; keep the surrounding content lightweight to focus attention on booking.

  • For pages with several calendars (e.g., different services), place each with its specific embed and add headings for clarity.


Surveys


Surveys collect structured feedback and research insights. Use them to measure NPS, gather onboarding data, or qualify leads.


  • Build the survey in MxP (questions, logic, completion actions).

  • In WordPress → MxP → Surveys, copy the survey embed.

  • Place it in your page, preview responsiveness, and publish.

  • Responses and analytics remain available in MxP for follow‑up and reporting.


Tips

  • Keep surveys concise; use progress indicators and completion messages configured in MxP.

  • Position surveys on thank‑you pages or inside blog posts for contextual insights.


Quizzes


Quizzes drive engagement and lead capture through interactive experiences. Use result mapping to segment contacts and trigger tailored automations in MxP.


  • Create your quiz in MxP (questions, scoring, outcomes, result pages).

  • In WordPress → MxP → Quizzes, and embed it with a shortcode.

  • Publish; completions sync to MxP, where you can use outcomes for automation.


Tips

  • Place quizzes on top‑of‑funnel pages; pair with a clear incentive.

  • Use outcome‑based CTAs that route to the most relevant next step.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Where are submissions, bookings, and results stored?
In MxP. WordPress displays the embed; data and analytics remain in your connected sub‑account.


Q: Can I add multiple calendars or review widgets to one page?
Yes. Place each instance via its own block/shortcode. Keep layouts simple for the best performance.


Q: Does it work with Elementor and Divi?
Yes. Use the shortcode element/widget in your page builder or embed via Gutenberg blocks when applicable.
