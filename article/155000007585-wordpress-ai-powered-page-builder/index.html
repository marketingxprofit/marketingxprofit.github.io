WordPress - AI-Powered Page Builder

AI-Powered WordPress Page Builder helps you create WordPress landing pages faster with AI in MxP. This article shows how to generate a page, customize its content and style, and continue editing it in Elementor.

What is AI-Powered WordPress Page Builder?


AI-Powered WordPress Page Builder lets you generate WordPress landing pages with AI inside MxP. After generation, new AI Pages are created in Draft mode by default so you can review, customize, and preview them before publishing. When a page is ready, you can publish it or unpublish it later when you need to take it offline.


Important: Draft & Preview Mode for AI Pages requires MxP plugin version 4.0.3 or later.


Key Benefits of AI-Powered WordPress Page Builder


  • Faster page creation: Generate a complete landing page structure in minutes instead of starting from a blank canvas.

  • Guided setup: Use business niche, business goal, and content feel selections to give the AI better direction.

  • Prompt assistance: Use the Improvise option to strengthen your prompt when you need help describing the page.

  • Built-in customization: Refine content, adjust tone, and switch color palettes directly in the page editor.

  • Elementor compatibility: Continue editing the generated page in Elementor using the workflow you already know.

  • Live style previews: Preview color palette changes in real time before applying them to the page.

  • Targeted image regeneration: Regenerate a single AI-generated image without rebuilding the full page.

  • More section variety: Choose from expanded layout and content variants for sections like testimonials, services, FAQs, and hero banners.

  • Draft-first workflow: New AI-generated pages are created in Draft mode by default, giving you time to review and customize them before they go live.

  • Preview before publishing: Preview an AI-generated page to see how it will appear on your website before publishing it.

  • Publish and unpublish controls: Publish a page when it is ready or unpublish it when you need to take it offline.

How To Use AI-Powered WordPress Page Builder


A clear setup process helps you create a stronger first draft, guide the AI more effectively, and make faster updates after the page is generated.


  1. Go to Sites > WordPress > All Sites.



  2. Click Manage Website.



  3. Go to Pages.

  4. Select AI Generated Pages.

  5. Click Generate New AI Page.



  6. Enter the page details:

    • Add the Page Name.

    • Add the Custom Site Slug.

    • Add the Name of Business.

    • Click Next.



  7. Choose your design preferences:

    • Select your business niche.

    • Select your business goal.

    • Select how the content should feel.

    • Click Next.



  8. Describe the page you want: Enter a plain-language prompt describing the type of page you want the AI to create. Include important details such as offers, sections, services, brand style, or calls to action.
    Click Improvise if you want MxP to improve your prompt.

  9. Click Generate Page.



  10. Review the generated page. New AI-generated pages are created in Draft mode by default, so the page can be reviewed and customized before it is published.

  11. Use the Customizations panel to update content and styles.

  12. Content options:

    • Edit generated text directly in the editor.

    • Use Refine to improve selected copy.

    • Use rewrite options such as Shorten or Elaborate when available.

    • Use Change Tone to adjust the voice of the content.

  13. Available tone options include:

    • Professional

    • Friendly

    • Compelling

    • Confident

  14. Regenerate a specific AI-generated image:

    • Hover over the AI-generated image you want to replace.
    • Click Regenerate Image.
    • Enter a prompt describing the image you want. You can include details like style, lighting, subject, or mood.
    • Click Generate Image and review the preview.
    • Click Insert Image to replace the selected image.
    • Click Save Changes.





  15. Style options:

    • Open the Styles tab inside Customizations.

    • Choose from available color palettes such as Default, Marine, Nature, and Earth.

    • Preview palette changes live across headings, body text, buttons, backgrounds, and accent colors.

    • Click Update Page to apply styling changes.


  16. Click Save Changes, Update Page, or Save & View as needed.



  17. Click Edit on Elementor if you want to continue editing the page in Elementor.

Preview, Publish, and Unpublish an AI-Generated Page


The AI-generated pages remain in Draft mode by default, giving you control over when they become visible on your website.


  1. Review and customize the generated page while it is in Draft mode.

  2. Use Preview to review how the page will appear before it goes live.

  3. When the page is ready, use Publish to make it live.

  4. To take a published AI Page offline, use Unpublish.



Important: Draft, Preview, Publish, and Unpublish support requires MxP plugin version 4.0.3 or later.


Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can I edit the AI-generated page after it is created?
Yes. You can update content and styles in the Customizations panel and continue editing the page in Elementor.


Q: Can I improve my prompt before generating the page?
Yes. Use the Improvise option to help optimize your prompt before generation.


Q: Can I change the tone of the generated content?

Yes. You can switch the content tone using available options such as Professional, Friendly, Compelling, and Confident.


Q: Can I change the page colors after the page is generated?
Yes. You can switch to a different color palette from the Styles tab in the Customizations panel.


Q: How many AI page generation credits are included?
MxP includes 12 AI page generation credits per site per month.


Q: Where can I see my remaining credits?
Your remaining AI page generation balance is displayed in the AI Generated Pages area.


Q: Do color palette changes apply immediately?

Color palette changes preview live while you browse available palettes. The changes are only saved after you click Update Page.


Q: Can I regenerate only one image on an AI-generated page?

Yes. Hover over the image, click Regenerate Image, enter a prompt, generate a preview, and insert the new image. Only the selected image is replaced.


Q: Can I use different section variants on the same page?

Yes. You can mix and match available section variants across the page.


Q: Are new AI-generated pages published automatically?

No. New AI-generated pages are created in Draft mode by default so you can review and customize them before publishing.


Q: Can I preview an AI-generated page before publishing it?

Yes. You can preview an AI-generated page before publishing to review how it will appear on your website.


Q: Can I take a published AI-generated page offline?

Yes. Use Unpublish to take a published AI Page offline.
