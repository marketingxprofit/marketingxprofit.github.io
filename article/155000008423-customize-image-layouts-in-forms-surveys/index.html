What are Image Layouts in Forms & Surveys?

Image layouts determine how images are displayed alongside your form fields or survey content. Choosing the right layout helps improve visual hierarchy, keeps important content in focus, and creates a more engaging experience for visitors.

With this update, you can choose from multiple image placement styles, adjust spacing and image styling for supported layouts, and customize individual survey slides with their own image layouts.

2

Key Benefits of Image Layouts

Image layout options provide greater flexibility when designing Forms and Surveys, making it easier to create experiences that align with your brand and improve usability.

More layout flexibility: Choose from five image placement options to better match your design.
Better image presentation: Keep the most important part of an image visible using Focal Point selection.
Improved visual styling: Adjust image corner radius and spacing for a cleaner, more modern appearance.
Greater survey customization: Apply different images and layouts to individual survey slides.
Professional designs: Build Forms and Surveys that feel more polished and visually balanced.
3

Available Image Layout Options

Each layout offers a different way to display images alongside your content. Selecting the appropriate layout helps improve readability while supporting your overall design goals.

Left Center

Displays the image on the left side of the form or survey while keeping it vertically centered next to the content.

Best suited for:

  • Registration forms
  • Contact forms
  • Lead capture forms
  • Marketing forms

Supports:

  • Image Corner Radius
  • Image-Field Spacing
Right Center

Displays the image on the right side of the form or survey while vertically centering it with the content.

Best suited for:

  • Marketing pages
  • Appointment forms
  • Promotional forms

Supports:

  • Image Corner Radius
  • Image-Field Spacing
Left Fixed

Displays a fixed image on the left side of the form while the form content appears alongside it.

Best suited for:

  • Hero images
  • Large promotional graphics
  • Lifestyle photography

Supports:

  • Focal Point selection
Right Fixed

Displays a fixed image on the right side of the form while content remains on the opposite side.

Best suited for:

  • Promotional campaigns
  • Event registration pages
  • Landing pages

Supports:

  • Focal Point selection
Background

Uses the uploaded image as the background behind the form or survey.

Best suited for:

  • Landing pages
  • Promotional campaigns
  • Branded experiences

Customize Image Styling

Additional styling controls allow you to refine how images appear within supported layouts, helping create cleaner spacing and a more polished design.

Image Corner Radius

Image Corner Radius lets you round the corners of images used in supported layouts.

Supported layouts:

  • Left Center
  • Right Center

Use this setting to:

  • Match your website branding
  • Create softer visual designs
  • Modernize the appearance of images
Image-Field Spacing

Image-Field Spacing controls the distance between the image and the form fields.

Supported layouts:

  • Left Center
  • Right Center

Increasing the spacing can:

  • Improve readability
  • Reduce visual clutter
  • Create a more balanced layout
Focal Point

Different screen sizes and aspect ratios may crop portions of fixed images. Focal Point allows you to choose which area of the image remains visible, helping preserve the most important content.

Supported layouts:

  • Left Fixed
  • Right Fixed

To adjust the focal point:

  1. Select a Left Fixed or Right Fixed layout.
  2. Click the uploaded image.
  3. Choose the area you want to keep centered.
  4. Save your changes.

Customize Image Layouts for Individual Survey Slides

By default, every survey slide inherits the main survey image layout. When a specific slide requires a different visual treatment, you can override the default settings and configure its own image and layout.

Note

Slide-level image layout customization is currently available only for Surveys.

You can customize:

  • Image
  • Image Layout
  • Layout behavior

This is useful when:

  • Different questions require different visuals.
  • Each slide represents a different topic.
  • You want to emphasize specific survey sections.

How to Configure Image Layouts in Forms

Properly configuring image layouts helps ensure your Forms look consistent across devices while making the best use of available design space.

Step 1

Navigate to Sites > Forms.


Step 2

Create a new form or open an existing one.

Step 3

Open the Styles and Options tab in the right sidebar.


Step 4

Expand Layout.

Step 5

Enable Show Image.


Step 6

Upload or select an image.

Step 7

Choose an Image Layout.


Step 8

Configure the available layout-specific options:

  • Left Center or Right Center
    • Image Corner Radius
    • Image-Field Spacing
  • Left Fixed or Right Fixed
    • Focal Point
Step 9

Preview your form.

Step 10

Save your changes.

How to Configure Image Layouts in Surveys

Survey image layouts can be applied globally or customized for individual slides, giving you more flexibility when designing multi-step experiences.

Configure the Survey Image Layout
Step 1

Navigate to Sites > Surveys.


Step 2

Create or edit a survey.

Step 3

Open the Styles tab.

Step 4

Enable Show Image.


Step 5

Upload or select an image.

Step 6

Choose an Image Layout.


Step 7

Configure the available layout-specific settings.

Step 8

Save your survey.

Override the Layout for an Individual Slide
Step 1

Open the survey builder.

Step 2

Select the slide you want to customize.

Step 3

Click the Settings icon for that slide.

Step 4

Locate Image Layout Settings.

Step 5

Change the setting to Independent.

Step 6

Upload a different image if desired.

Step 7

Select a different Image Layout.

Step 8

Save your changes.

Best Practices

Following a few design recommendations can help create more visually balanced Forms and Surveys while improving the overall user experience.

  • Use Center layouts for forms with smaller images.
  • Use Fixed layouts for larger promotional or hero images.
  • Select a Focal Point before publishing fixed layouts to ensure important content remains visible.
  • Increase Image-Field Spacing when forms contain many fields to improve readability.
  • Use Corner Radius to match your brand's visual style.
  • Override survey slide layouts only when individual slides benefit from unique imagery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which image layouts support Image Corner Radius?
Image Corner Radius is available only for the Left Center and Right Center layouts.
Q: Which image layouts support Image-Field Spacing?
Image-Field Spacing is available only for the Left Center and Right Center layouts.
Q: Which image layouts support Focal Point?
Focal Point is available only for the Left Fixed and Right Fixed layouts.
Q: Can every survey slide have its own image?
Yes. Enable Independent under Image Layout Settings for the slide you want to customize.
Q: Can Forms use slide-level image layouts?
No. Slide-level image layout customization is currently available only for Surveys.
Q: What happens if I don't enable Independent for a survey slide?
The slide inherits the main survey image and image layout settings.
Q: Does changing the survey image update every slide?
Yes. Unless a slide has been configured with Independent image settings, it uses the survey's default image and layout.
