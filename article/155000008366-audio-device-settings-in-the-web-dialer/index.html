Audio Device Settings in the Web Dialer

Take full control over your call audio by selecting exactly which devices ring and handle audio during calls.
What You'll Learn

The web dialer audio settings give you precise control over which devices handle your call audio, from choosing your primary speaker and microphone to ringing multiple devices simultaneously for inbound calls.

This article explains how the audio device settings work, how to configure them, and how to ensure you never miss an inbound call by ringing both your headset and computer speaker at the same time.

What Are Audio Device Settings?

Audio device settings in the MxP web dialer let you choose which hardware devices handle your call audio. You can select your primary speaker for listening to calls, pick a microphone for speaking, and optionally ring a second device simultaneously for inbound calls.

This feature is designed to help you customize your call experience based on your workspace setup. Whether you use a headset, external speakers, or your computer's built-in audio, the web dialer adapts to your preferences.

The settings panel is accessible directly from the dialer interface and includes test buttons to preview your audio setup before saving changes.

2

Key Benefits

Audio device settings provide several advantages for managing your call workflow:

Never Miss an Inbound Call — Ring both your headset and computer speaker at the same time to ensure you hear every incoming call, even when you step away from your desk.
Flexible Workspace Setup — Adapt the dialer to your physical environment by choosing the devices that work best for your office, home, or remote workspace.
Test Before You Save — Preview your speaker and additional ring device with test buttons, and verify your microphone is working with a live volume meter before committing to changes.
Audio Device Control — Choose which available microphone and speaker the Web Dialer should use for calls.

Understanding the Audio Device Options

The audio device settings panel offers three configurable options to control how your calls sound and where they ring:

Option 1
Main Speaker (Required)

This is the primary device that plays call audio. Select your preferred output device from the dropdown menu — this could be headphones, external speakers, or your computer's built-in speaker. The Main Speaker plays the other caller's audio during an active Web Dialer call.

Option 2
Additional Ring Device (Optional)

Choose a second device to ring simultaneously when inbound calls arrive. This is useful if you want to hear calls on both your headset and desk speaker, ensuring you never miss an incoming call even when you're not wearing your headset. This device only rings for inbound calls and does not play audio during active conversations.

Option 3
Microphone (Required)

Select the microphone you want to use for speaking during calls. The microphone picker displays a live volume meter so you can verify the device is picking up sound before you save your settings. This ensures your voice is captured clearly on every call.

Helpful Tip

Use the test buttons next to the speaker and additional device options to preview audio playback before saving. The microphone volume meter updates in real time, so you can confirm the device is working without needing to make a test call.

4

How to Configure Audio Device Settings

Follow these steps to customize your audio device settings in the web dialer:

Step 1
Open Audio Settings

Click the gear icon in the web dialer header to open the audio settings panel.

Step 2
Select Your Main Speaker

In the audio settings panel, choose your primary speaker from the dropdown menu under "Main Speaker." This device will play all call audio. Use the test button next to the dropdown to preview the device before saving.

Step 3
Optionally Add a Second Ring Device

If you want to ring an additional device for inbound calls, select it from the "Additional Ring Device" dropdown. For example, you might choose your computer speaker so calls ring on both your headset and desk speaker at the same time. Use the test button to confirm the device rings as expected.

Step 4
Choose Your Microphone

Select the microphone you want to use from the "Microphone" dropdown. The panel displays a live volume meter so you can verify the device is picking up sound. Speak into the microphone and watch the meter to confirm it's working correctly.

Step 5
Save Your Settings

After configuring your devices and testing them, click the save button to apply your changes. Your audio settings are now active for the Web Dialer in this browser and on this device.


If you use MxP in another browser or on another computer, configure the audio devices again.

Pro Tip

To avoid missing inbound calls, set your main speaker to your headset and your additional ring device to your computer speaker. This way, you'll hear calls on both devices, even if you're not wearing your headset at the moment the call arrives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I ring more than two devices at the same time?
No. The audio device settings support one main speaker and one optional additional ring device for inbound calls. You cannot configure more than two devices to ring simultaneously.
Q: Does the additional ring device play audio during active calls?
No. The additional ring device only rings when inbound calls arrive. Once you answer the call, all audio plays through your main speaker. Only the primary speaker handles call audio during conversations.
Q: What if I don't see my audio device in the dropdown menus?
Make sure your device is properly connected to your computer and recognized by your operating system. You may need to refresh the web dialer or restart your browser to detect newly connected devices. Check your operating system's sound settings to confirm the device is available and functioning.
Q: Will my audio settings apply to all devices where I use the web dialer?
No. Audio device settings are stored locally in your browser. If you use the web dialer on a different computer or in a different browser, you will need to configure your audio settings again for that environment.
Q: How do I know if my microphone is working before making a call?
The microphone dropdown includes a live volume meter that displays in real time as you speak. Watch the meter while speaking into the microphone — if it responds to your voice, the device is working correctly. You do not need to make a test call to verify microphone functionality.
Q: Can I use different audio devices for inbound and outbound calls?
The main speaker and microphone are used for both inbound and outbound calls. The additional ring device only activates for inbound calls to help you hear the ring. You cannot set different primary devices for inbound versus outbound calls.
Q: What happens if I unplug my audio device during a call?
If you disconnect an audio device during a call, your browser or operating system may switch to another available device. The exact behavior depends on your browser and operating system. To avoid call interruptions, keep your selected headset, speaker, or microphone connected during the call. If audio stops working, reopen Audio Settings and confirm the correct devices are selected.
Q: Do I need to save my settings every time I open the web dialer?
No. Once you save your audio settings, they persist in your browser. The web dialer remembers your device preferences and applies them automatically every time you use the dialer on that computer and browser.
