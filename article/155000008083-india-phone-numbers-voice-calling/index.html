Prerequisites

Before purchasing an India phone number, each sub-account must meet the following requirements:

  • Business operating in or targeting India
  • Valid Certificate of Incorporation (or equivalent business registration document)
  • Valid GST Registration Certificate
  • Active MxP (LC) Phone System
Important

Compliance verification is performed at the sub-account level. Each sub-account requesting India phone numbers must independently satisfy all applicable regulatory requirements.

Calling Restrictions

Outbound
Domestic Calling Only

India phone numbers can only be used to place outbound calls to destinations within India. Outbound calls to international destinations will fail due to regulatory restrictions imposed by Indian telecommunications authorities.

Inbound
Inbound Calls

India phone numbers can generally receive calls from international callers, subject to the originating carrier's ability to route calls to India.

Important — Regulatory Requirement

For India calling, regulatory requirements mandate that both legs of the call, including the media/WebRTC session, originate and terminate within India. Calls may be blocked if the media endpoint is detected outside India, even when both the From and To numbers are Indian.

Available Capabilities for India Numbers

We're actively working to bring these capabilities to India calling.
For now, the following are not supported for India calling:

  • Call Transcription
  • Blind/Warm Transfer
  • IVR Workflow Calls
  • Voice AI

Inbound calls can only be answered to a forwarding number; transfers to the web/mobile app are not supported.

Number Migration Restrictions

India phone numbers can only be transferred to accounts registered in India. To remain compliant with local regulations:

  • The phone number must be registered in India.
  • The receiving sub-account or agency must also be registered in India.
  • Transfers to agencies or sub-accounts registered outside India are not permitted.

Regulatory Compliance

All restrictions, requirements, and limitations in this article are mandated by Indian government and telecommunications regulations. These regulations apply to:

  • Phone number provisioning
  • Number ownership
  • Number transfers
  • Call routing
  • Outbound calling permissions
Important

Failure to comply with applicable regulatory requirements may prevent:

  • Regulatory Bundle approval
  • Phone number purchases
  • Number transfers
  • Successful call completion

How to Activate

Step 1
Create a Regulatory Bundle

Before purchasing an India phone number, a Regulatory Bundle must be submitted and approved. Navigate to: Settings → Phone System → Regulatory Bundle → Create

  • Select Regulatory Bundle
  • Choose India as the country
  • Select Local as the number type
  • Enter a bundle name
  • Upload required documents: Certificate of Incorporation (or equivalent business registration document), GST Registration Certificate
  • Click Update Document
  • Submit the Regulatory Bundle for review
Important

Regulatory Bundles are only supported for accounts using the MxP (LC) Phone System. Customers using non-LC telephony providers cannot create or process Regulatory Bundles and must migrate to the MxP Phone before purchasing India phone numbers.

Step 2
Purchase an India Phone Number

Once the Regulatory Bundle has been approved, navigate to: Settings → Phone System → Phone Numbers

  • Click Add Number
  • Select India as the country
  • Choose an available India phone number
  • Complete the purchase flow

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use an India phone number to call international destinations?
No. India phone numbers can only be used to place outbound calls to destinations within India. International outbound calling is restricted by Indian telecommunications regulations.
Q: Can I receive calls from international numbers on my India phone number?
Yes. International callers can generally call your India phone number, subject to their carrier's ability to route calls to India.
Q: Will a call be blocked if my number and the recipient's number are both Indian, but I'm calling from outside India?
Possibly, yes. Regulatory requirements mandate that both legs of the call — including the media/WebRTC session — originate and terminate within India. If the media endpoint is detected outside India, the call may be blocked even when both the From and To numbers are Indian.
Q: Can I transcribe, record, or transfer calls on an India phone number?
Not yet. Call Transcription, Call Recording, Blind/Warm Transfer, IVR Workflow Calls, and Voice AI are not currently supported for India phone numbers. We're actively working to bring these capabilities to India calling.
Q: Can inbound calls to my India number be transferred to the web or mobile app?
No. Inbound calls to India phone numbers can only be answered to a forwarding number. Transfers to the web/mobile app are not supported at this time.
Q: Can I send SMS or MMS messages using an India phone number?
No. SMS and MMS messaging are currently not supported for India phone numbers.
Q: Can I use my India phone number for WhatsApp messaging?
Yes. India phone numbers can be used for WhatsApp Business Messaging, subject to WhatsApp eligibility, verification, and onboarding requirements.
Q: Can I transfer an India phone number to another account?
Only if the receiving account is registered in India. Transfers to agencies or sub-accounts that are not registered in India are not permitted.
Q: Can I purchase an India phone number if my business is registered outside India?
No. India phone numbers can only be assigned to accounts that satisfy the applicable Indian regulatory requirements. The receiving account must be registered in India.
Q: Can customers using a non-LC telephony provider purchase India phone numbers?
No. India phone numbers and Regulatory Bundles are only supported for accounts using the MxP (LC) Phone System. Customers using non-LC telephony providers must migrate to the MxP Phone first.
Q: How many India phone numbers can I purchase per account?
Currently, only one additional India phone number can be purchased per eligible account due to prepaid provider model limitations. Additional purchasing capacity may be introduced in future releases.
Q: Will these restrictions change in the future?
Platform-specific limitations may evolve over time as we continue prioritizing these capabilities. However, regulatory restrictions imposed by Indian telecommunications authorities will continue to apply unless updated by the relevant regulatory bodies.
