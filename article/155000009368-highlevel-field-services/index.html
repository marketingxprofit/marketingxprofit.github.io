MxP Field Services

Field Services is MxP's end-to-end module for businesses that do their work at the customer's location, such as HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, landscaping and other home services. It brings the whole job lifecycle together in one place: capturing requests, quoting, scheduling and dispatching visits, optimizing routes, tracking the work, invoicing, and keeping customers informed.


This article explains how to enable Field Services, how to find it on the mobile app, what is included, how the pieces fit together, and the recommended order to set everything up.


Note: Field Services is currently in private beta and is available to selected accounts. Some features are still being built and may change before public release.

How to Enable Field Services


Field Services is currently a private beta. To get access:


  1. Fill out this form to register your interest for the private beta.

  2. Once your account is added to the beta, open your sub-account.

  3. In the left navigation, look for Field Services with a Beta tag and click it to open the module.

  4. On Home, complete the setup checklist (staff, service area, and services) so you can start scheduling jobs. See Field Services Home Overview.


Note: If you do not see Field Services in the left navigation yet, your account may still be waiting to be enrolled. You can register or follow up using this form. See also Guide to the Field Services Private Beta.

How to Find Field Services on the Mobile App


On mobile, Field Services appears as Jobs. Your team can manage requests and jobs, schedule and reschedule visits, change visit statuses, and use checklists, notes and timers. Changes sync with the web.


If you do not have the app installed


  1. On web, open Field Services > Home.

  2. Open the Setup guide (new accounts may also see this on the welcome page).

  3. Under Get the mobile app, scan the App Store or Google Play QR code.

  4. Install the app and sign in with the same login you use on the web.

  5. Open your location, then follow the steps below to open Jobs.
     



If you already have the app installed


  1. Open the app and select the location where Field Services is set up.

  2. Tap Apps in the bottom navigation.

  3. Under Sales & Operations, tap Jobs.

  4. You will see the Jobs screen. The bottom navigation switches to Jobs, Schedule, and Search.


Tip: If you do not see Jobs under Apps, confirm Field Services is available for that location on the web, then pull to refresh or sign out and back in. Learn more in Jobs in Field Services.

From Jobs on mobile you can also tap + to create a Request, Job, or Estimate, open Schedule (List, Week, or Map), and use Search across Jobs, Requests, Estimates, and Invoices.


For the full mobile walkthrough, see Using Field Services on the Mobile App.

What is Field Services?


Field Services is built for teams that travel to the customer. Instead of stitching together a form tool, a calendar, a dispatch spreadsheet and an invoicing app, you run the whole workflow from one place:


  • Capture work: Customers submit requests or book directly from your branded booking page, or your team creates requests and jobs in the app.

  • Organize the work: A Job holds the customer, service address, services, products and pricing. One job can have several visits.

  • Schedule and dispatch: Assign visits to staff, find open times based on working hours, skills, service areas and drive time, view the day on a calendar or map, and optimize each technician's route.

  • Do the work: Staff update visit statuses, follow checklists, add notes and, if Timesheets is on, track their time.

  • Get paid: Create estimates and invoices from the job and collect payments.

  • Keep customers informed: Send confirmations, reminders and "staff on the way" updates by email, SMS or WhatsApp.


Who uses it?


  • Owners and managers use Home, Schedule and Invoices to see how the business is running.

  • Office staff and dispatchers triage requests, create jobs, schedule visits and optimize routes.

  • Field staff see their visits, update statuses, work through checklists and add notes.


Key Benefits of Field Services


  • One workflow from request to invoice: No more switching between tools to quote, schedule and bill a job.

  • Smarter scheduling: Availability respects staff working hours, assigned services, service areas, existing bookings and drive time.

  • Less time on the road: Route optimization reorders a technician's day for the shortest drive and shows the time you save.

  • Clear coverage rules: Define the areas you serve, charge a travel fee, and decide what happens when a customer is outside your area.

  • Customers book themselves: A branded booking page checks the customer's address, shows open times and takes the booking or request.

  • One picture for the whole team: Office staff, dispatchers, owners and field staff all see the same jobs, visits and statuses.

Key Concepts: Requests, Jobs and Visits


Three objects sit at the heart of Field Services. Understanding how they relate makes everything else easier.


  • Request: A customer's ask for work. A request can include a description, photos, services and products, and an optional site visit to assess the work. You can convert a request into an estimate or a job.

  • Job: The overall piece of work for a customer at a service address. A job holds the services, products, pricing, checklists, notes, invoices and payments.

  • Visit: A scheduled trip to do part of the work. A job can have one visit or many, and each visit has a date and time, assigned staff and a status.


A few rules to keep in mind:


  • A job can exist without a visit. It stays unscheduled until you schedule one.

  • A visit must include at least one service, because the service decides the duration and which staff are eligible.

  • Adding a service from a visit also adds it to the job.

  • The job's status updates automatically as its visits move forward. You do not set it by hand.
Tip: Think of the job as "what we are doing" and the visit as "when and who is doing it".

Recommended Setup Order


Set things up in this order so each step has what it needs.


New accounts open Field Services on a welcome page with a Get ready to take bookings checklist. It covers the same setup: add your staff, review your service area and add your services. See Field Services Home Overview for a tour of the welcome page.




  1. Create your service areas. Go to Field Services > Services & staff > Service areas. A Default area is created for you. Edit it or add more. See Service Areas in Field Services.

  2. Add your staff. Go to Services & staff > Staff and set working hours, assigned services, eligible service areas, and route start and end. See Staff in Field Services.

  3. Create your services and add-ons. Go to Services & staff > Services. You can also import existing services. See Services and Add-ons in Field Services and Importing Services into Field Services.

  4. Review Field Services Settings. Choose how customers book, set up payments, and review the request form. See Field Services Settings: Online Booking and Field Services Settings: Jobs, Visits and Preferences.

  5. Customize your booking page. See Booking Page Editor in Field Services.

  6. Set up notifications for customers, assigned staff and dispatchers. See Notifications in Field Services.

  7. Create your first request or job and schedule a visit. See Requests in Field Services, Jobs in Field Services and Scheduling Visits and Finding Availability in Field Services.


Tip: Before you invite customers to book online, open your booking page yourself and complete a test booking with an address inside your service area.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: How do I get access to Field Services?
Field Services is currently a private beta. Fill out this form to register your interest. Once your account is added, you will see Field Services with a Beta tag in the left navigation of your sub-account. See How to Enable Field Services and Guide to the Field Services Private Beta.



Q: Is Field Services the same as Services?
No. Services is MxP's booking tool for appointments that happen at your business or online. Field Services is built for work that happens at the customer's location, with requests, jobs, multi-visit scheduling, dispatch and routing. You can bring your existing services into Field Services with the importer. See Importing Services into Field Services.



Q: Who are "staff" in Field Services?
Staff are the people who go out on jobs. They are users in your sub-account who you set up with working hours, services and service areas. Office staff, owners and dispatchers can use Field Services without being assigned to visits.


Q: Can one job have more than one visit?
Yes. A job can have as many visits as the work needs, for example a diagnosis visit followed by an installation visit.



Q: Can more than one technician be assigned to a visit?
Yes. Staff are assigned per service on a visit, so a larger visit can have several technicians.



Q: Can customers book themselves?
Yes. Your booking page lets customers check their address, choose services and pick a time, or send a request for you to review. See Field Services Settings: Online Booking and Booking Page Editor in Field Services.



Q: Do I need to connect a payment provider?
Not to get started. You need a payment provider only if you want customers to pay online when they book. Until one is connected, use Pay in-person on your booking page. See Field Services Settings: Online Booking.



Q: Can I use Field Services on mobile?
Yes. On mobile, Field Services appears as Jobs. If you do not have the app, open Field Services > Home on web, open the Setup guide, and under Get the mobile app scan the App Store or Google Play QR code (new accounts may also see this on the welcome page). Sign in with the same login you use on the web. If you already have the app, go to Apps > Sales & Operations > Jobs. See How to Find Field Services on the Mobile App.
