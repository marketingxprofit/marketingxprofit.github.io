Call Connection Quality and Ratings in MxP Mobile

Monitor call quality in real time with the MxP mobile app. View connection strength, check network and audio diagnostics, and get troubleshooting tips during calls. After hanging up, submit a quick rating to help MxP improve call quality.

What is Call Connection Quality Monitoring and Post-Call Ratings?


Call Connection Quality Monitoring helps users identify connection problems during active calls. A four-bar signal indicator displays the connection status as Good, Weak, or Poor. Tapping the indicator opens the Connection quality panel, which provides network, device, and audio diagnostics with relevant troubleshooting suggestions.


After a supported call ends, users can submit a star rating and report any audio issues they experienced.


Availability:

  • Apps: MxP, MxP, and Self-Serve White-Label apps

  • Minimum Version: 4.27.0+

  • Status: Live

  • WhatsApp: Reconnection tone supported; call ratings coming soon.

Key Benefits of Call Connection Quality Monitoring and Post-Call Ratings


Real-time monitoring and post-call feedback help users identify connection problems, improve conversations, and provide useful feedback about their calling experience.

  • Real-Time Monitoring: View connection quality through color-coded signal bars.

  • Faster Troubleshooting: Identify potential network, device, and audio problems during calls.

  • Helpful Recommendations: Receive tips based on current connection conditions.

  • Reconnection Alerts: Hear a soft tone during temporary call interruptions.

  • Easy Feedback: Rate calls and report audio issues after hanging up.

  • Better Call Quality: Help MxP identify recurring problems through feedback.

Understanding Connection Quality Indicators


The four-bar indicator appears when a call connects and changes color based on connection conditions.

ColorQualityMeaning
GreenGoodConnection is performing well.
OrangeWeakConnection may be unstable.
RedPoorConnection problems may affect audio.


Tap the indicator to open Connection quality, which displays a summary such as "Your connection is good" or "Your connection is poor."

Connection Quality Diagnostics


The Connection quality panel helps users identify factors that may affect their conversations. Each diagnostic category displays a Good, Weak, or Poor assessment.

  • Network: Displays the current connection-quality assessment.

  • Device: Shows connection type, Wi-Fi strength, phone temperature, battery saver, and low data mode.

  • Audio: Displays microphone, incoming audio, and audio output information.


These details help users understand whether network conditions, device settings, or audio configuration may be affecting their calls.

Troubleshooting Call Quality Issues


MxP provides recommendations based on detected connection conditions, helping users take appropriate action during a call.

IssueRecommended Action
Weak Wi-FiMove closer to your router.
Unstable mobile dataSwitch to a stable Wi-Fi connection.
Offline connectionCheck your internet connection.
Battery saver enabledTurn off battery saver if appropriate.


The app also provides quick troubleshooting options for common problems:

  • They can't hear me: Check microphone settings and permissions.

  • I can't hear them: Check volume and audio output.

  • Audio keeps breaking up: Review network strength and connection recommendations.


For additional guidance, see How to Fix Bad Call Quality in MxP.

Call Reconnection Tone


When a call temporarily loses connectivity, a soft tone plays until the connection returns. This replaces the previous vibration and banner notification. The reconnection tone works with regular phone calls and WhatsApp calls.


Note: The tone indicates a temporary connection interruption, not necessarily a disconnected call.

Post-Call Quality Ratings


Post-call ratings let users share feedback about their calling experience. Ratings help MxP identify and investigate audio and connectivity problems. After a supported call ends, users can select a rating from one to five stars.

  • 4–5 Stars: The rating is submitted immediately.

  • 1–3 Stars: The app requests additional feedback.


Available feedback reasons include:

  • I couldn't hear them

  • They couldn't hear me

  • Choppy or robotic audio

  • Call dropped

  • Something else (optional note)


The rating sheet appears automatically at most once per week. Users can also tap ☆ Rate this call on the call end screen to rate other calls manually.

How to Check Connection Quality and Rate Calls


Checking connection quality during a call helps users identify problems early, while post-call ratings provide an easy way to share feedback afterward.

  1. Update the App: Ensure you're using MxP, MxP, or a supported White-Label app running version 4.27.0 or later.

  2. Make or Answer a Call: Start or receive a supported phone call in the mobile app.

  3. Check Connection Quality: Look at the four-bar indicator on the active call screen.

  4. View Diagnostics: Tap the indicator to open Connection quality and review Network, Device, and Audio information.

  5. Troubleshoot: Follow the recommended action if the connection is Weak or Poor.

  6. End the Call: Hang up and open the call end screen.

  7. Rate the Call: Select a star rating when prompted, or tap ☆ Rate this call. For ratings of three stars or fewer, choose a reason and complete the feedback flow.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Does a Poor connection mean the MxP app is having problems?

Not necessarily. Call quality can be affected by your network, device, voice provider, or the other participant's connection.


Q: Why does my connection indicator change during a call?

The indicator updates as network and connection conditions change.


Q: Can I rate a call if the automatic rating prompt doesn't appear?

Yes. Tap ☆ Rate this call on the call end screen.


Q: How often does the rating sheet appear automatically?

At most once per week. Manual ratings remain available for other calls.


Q: What does the reconnection tone mean?

It indicates that the call has temporarily lost connectivity and is reconnecting.


Q: Can I provide additional details about a poor call?

Yes. Ratings of three stars or fewer let you select a problem category. You can also provide an optional note under 

Something else.


Q: Are post-call ratings available for WhatsApp calls?

Not yet. WhatsApp call ratings are planned for a future release.
