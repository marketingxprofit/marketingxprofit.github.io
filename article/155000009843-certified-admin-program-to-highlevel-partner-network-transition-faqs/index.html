Certified Admin Transition FAQs


Find answers to common questions about the Certified Admin Program transition, including important deadlines, Founder Plan eligibility, existing certifications, billing, and Partner Network requirements.


Important Dates:

  • December 31, 2026: Deadline to claim the eligible Founder Plan introductory offer.

  • January 4, 2027: Paid Certified Admin Program sunsets and the new Partner Directory launches.

Changes to the Certified Admin Program


Q: What is changing with the Certification Program?

MxP is transitioning from the paid Certified Admin Program to the new MxP Partner Network, designed to help qualified MxP professionals showcase their expertise, connect with potential customers, and grow their businesses.


The paid Certified Admin Program will officially sunset on January 4, 2027, including its Directory, matching services, and associated membership benefits.


To support this transition, active Certified Admin subscribers have been offered an exclusive opportunity to upgrade to the Founder Plan, normally $497/month, with eligible subscribers receiving promotional pricing for 12 months based on their existing MxP Agency plan. This offer must be claimed by December 31, 2026.


MxP has also introduced a free Admin Certificate, which is separate from both the former paid Certification Program and the new Partner Network.



Q: Will my current paid Certification Program membership end automatically if I don’t opt-in to the Founder Plan?

The paid Certified Admin Program and its associated benefits, including the Certified Admin Directory, will sunset on January 4, 2027. If you don’t opt-in to the Founder Plan by that date, your Certification Program membership will end automatically. You will not be automatically migrated to or charged for the Founder Plan.



Q: Does my existing Certified Admin certification still count?

Yes. Your original certification remains recognized. If you completed both Part A and the live-proctored Part B, that achievement also satisfies the Builder Exam requirement if you choose to pursue the Partner Program.



Q: What does the new free Certification Program certificate require?

The new free certificate requires passing the Builder Exam, previously called Part A. It does not require the former live-proctored Part B or a MxP agency subscription. Earning the certificate is separate from subscribing to the Founder Plan or joining the Partner Program.



Q: I'm partway through the original certification. What should I do?

Continue toward the Builder Exam (formerly Part A). Under the new free-certificate path, you do not need to complete the former Part B. If you are unsure what remains on your record, contact the Certification team for guidance.



Q: I passed Part A but have not taken Part B. Do I need to start again?

Part B is not required for the new certificate, which is based on the Builder Exam (formerly known as Part A). Email certifications@MxP.com so the team can confirm your recorded completion and help with your certificate status.

Founder Plan Eligibility and Transition 


Q: What is the Founder Plan, and how is it different from the Partner Program?

The Founder Plan is a MxP subscription with platform and business-building features, including unlimited sub-accounts, rebilling with markup, advanced API access, automated sub-account creation, SaaS Mode and reporting. The Partner Program is a separate, optional opportunity for Founder Plan customers. Buying the Founder Plan does not automatically make you a qualified Partner.

Learn more about the Founder Plan here: https://founder.MxP.com/



Q: Do I have to upgrade to Founder or join the Partner Program?

No. Moving to the Founder Plan and joining the Partner Program are optional. You can subscribe to the Founder Plan without joining the Partner Program. However, if you wish to participate in the Partner Program, an active Founder Plan subscription is required.



Q: Who qualifies for the introductory Founder Plan offer?

The introductory offer is available only to people with an active paid Certification Program subscription. The rate depends on their existing MxP agency plan and is offered for 12 months. Having been certified in the past does not, by itself, meet the active-subscription requirement.



Q: How long does the introductory price last, and what happens afterward?

The introductory rate lasts 12 months. After that, the standard Founder Plan rate of $497 per month applies. You can choose another available MxP plan if Founder no longer suits your business, but continued Partner Program participation requires Founder.



Q: Can I subscribe to the Founder Plan if I do not have a MxP agency plan?

Yes! If you do not currently have a MxP agency account, you will first need to sign up for a free trial of the Starter Plan. This creates the relationship number our Billing team needs to migrate your account to the Founder Plan.

Once your Starter trial is active, you can complete the Founder Plan opt-in form to request your migration.

Please note: The exclusive introductory offer for Certified Admins is only available to those with an active paid Certified Admin subscription.



Q: How do I request the move to the Founder Plan?

Complete the Founder Plan opt-in form sent to you by email from the Certification Program. Your request will be sent to Billing for processing.



Q: Does submitting the opt-in form change my plan or charge me immediately?

No. Submitting the form sends your request to Billing; it does not instantly complete the move. Billing will process the transition and confirm your invoice details and effective date. Once your migration is processed, your Founder Plan subscription becomes active and the applicable Founder Plan charge is processed immediately.



Q: I have multiple employees in my agency who are subscribed to the Certification    Program. Does everyone need to submit the transition form?

No. Only the agency owner needs to submit the transition form. You only need one transition form per agency. Your employees do not need to submit their own forms because the Founder Plan and Partner Network access are linked to your agency, not to individual employees.


Here's how it works:

  1. The agency owner submits the transition form using their agency's relationship number.

  2. Once the transition is complete, the agency moves to the Founder Plan and is eligible to join the Partner Network.

  3. Once the Partner Portal is available to your agency, you can add your other employees as team members under the same agency.

  4. If your employees have separate Certification Program subscriptions, you can cancel those individually, if you choose.

Subscriptions, Billing and Cancellations


Q: Why am I still paying for Certification, and what happens after I move to Founder?

Your paid Certified Admin subscription and your Founder Plan subscription are two separate products. When completing the Founder Plan opt-in form, you will be asked to select whether you would like to keep or cancel your existing Certified Admin subscription.


If you choose to keep it, your subscription and its benefits will remain active until the program sunsets in January of 2027, and you will continue to be billed separately. If you choose to cancel, monthly subscriptions will end at the conclusion of the current billing cycle, while annual subscriptions will be canceled immediately, with a credit for the unused portion applied to your agency account.



Q: What happens if I prepaid for a yearly Certification Program membership?

If you have an annual Certified Admin subscription and choose to cancel it as part of your Founder Plan transition, your subscription will be canceled immediately. A prorated credit for the unused portion of your annual subscription will be calculated by our Billing team and applied to your MxP agency account.


This is an account credit rather than a direct refund to your original payment method.



Q: How can I cancel my Certification membership or leave the Founder Plan?

To cancel your paid Certification Program membership, submit the Certification Program cancellation form. Your membership remains active through the current billing cycle and then stops renewing. For Founder cancellation or downgrade, contact Billing. Leaving Founder ends Partner Program eligibility, but does not erase your original certification.

Joining and Qualifying for the MxP Partner Network


Q: How do I apply to join the Partner Program?

Apply separately at partners.MxP.com. Signing up for a Founder Plan is not the same as applying to the Partner Program. You can express interest while your Founder change is processing, but the Founder Plan must be active before the Partner application can proceed.



Q: What happens after I submit my Partner application?

You will receive an application confirmation. The team will review your Founder Plan status and guide you through the assessments and Partner Intake steps that apply to you. An application confirmation does not mean you are already qualified for matching.



Q: What types of Partners can join?

There are three business-model types: Expert (an individual providing MxP services), Solutions Provider (a team or agency delivering services), and SaaSPRENEUR (a business offering MxP as a managed SaaS service). These are not levels of seniority.



Q: What do I need to complete before becoming a qualified Partner?

The standard path includes the Builder, Advisor and AI Implementation assessments, a Partner Intake Call, and applicable profile and readiness steps. Your existing certification credit or an approved accelerated path may change which assessments you personally need.



Q: Which assessments do already-certified MxP Admins need to take?

If you completed the original certification exams, you do not need to retake Builder. You complete the Advisor and AI Implementation assessments, followed by Partner onboarding. The team can confirm whether an approved alternative path applies to your business.



Q: What do the Advisor and AI Implementation Exams cover?

Advisor focuses on understanding customer needs, recommending suitable MxP solutions and explaining them clearly. AI Implementation assesses your ability to implement MxP AI solutions. Both go beyond the foundational Builder requirement.



Q: Can different people in my agency complete different Partner Exams?

Yes. Solutions Providers can meet the exam requirements through appropriately linked team members, and the business must still complete Partner Intake. Individual Experts complete the exams required for their own path themselves.



Q: Is there an accelerated qualification path for experienced businesses?

Some established agencies and SaaSPRENEURs may qualify for an accelerated assessment path. The Partner team will explain the requirements that apply. Experience alone does not automatically waive assessments or onboarding.



Q: Can I retake an exam, and will my results expire?

Yes, retakes are available. The team can provide preparation resources, instructions and next steps if you do not pass. Exam results do not currently expire. Instructions and passing requirements will be shared before each relevant assessment; any future renewal rules will be communicated separately.



Q: What happens during the Partner Intake Call, and how should I prepare?

This is a 30-minute conversation with a Partner Growth Specialist about your business, services, ideal customers, experience, team, fees, service regions, availability, capacity and goals. It helps the team assess your readiness. Be prepared to discuss real customer examples and the services you are ready to deliver.



Q: What training and ongoing support will Partners receive?

Qualified Partners have access to ongoing education, practical training, Partner Growth Playbooks and a dedicated community experience. An assigned Partner Growth Specialist provides guidance after onboarding. Weekly group orientation is also planned as part of the program, alongside not instead of the individual Intake Call.

Directory Listings, Reviews, and Customer Matching


Q: What happens to my existing Directory listing and reviews if I stay or move to Founder?

The existing Certified Admin Directory will officially sunset on January 4, 2027, when the new Partner Directory launches.


If you choose to keep your paid Certified Admin subscription during the transition period, your existing Directory listing and reviews will remain available until the Directory closes.


If you cancel your Certified Admin subscription before the sunset date, your listing will no longer remain active once your subscription ends.


The new Partner Directory is a separate experience available to qualified members of the MxP Partner Network.


We're evaluating options for migrating existing Certified Admin profile information and reviews. Details regarding what information may transfer have not yet been finalized.



Q: Will there be a new Partner Directory, and will my current profile or reviews transfer?

Yes! The new MxP Partner Directory officially launches on January 4, 2027, replacing the existing Certified Admin Directory.


The new Directory will feature qualified MxP Partners who have completed the applicable Partner Network requirements.


We're currently evaluating options for transferring existing Certified Admin profile information and reviews into the new Directory. Automatic migration has not yet been confirmed, and we'll share additional details as those decisions are finalized.



Q: When will customer matching begin, and how will it work?

Customer matching through the MxP Partner Network officially begins on January 4, 2027. MxP plans to recommend qualified Partners based on customer needs and factors such as services, industry experience, location, language, budget and availability. This target is not a promise of when any individual Partner will receive an opportunity.



Q: Will becoming a qualified Partner guarantee leads or clients?

No. Completing the required assessments and onboarding makes you eligible for opportunities, not guaranteed leads, clients or revenue. Matches are not distributed equally. Founder Plan enrollment by itself does not qualify you for matching: you must also apply to the Partner Program and complete its requirements.



Q: How are opportunities distributed, and can I decline one?

Opportunities are intended to reflect customer fit, Partner readiness and performance, rather than equal allocation. Customers may be shown a small group of qualified Partners, so exclusivity is not promised. You may decline an unsuitable opportunity; the effect on future recommendations has not yet been finalized.



Q: Who sets service pricing, and does MxP take a percentage of my fees?

You and your customer agree directly on prices, scope, deliverables and commercial terms. Under the current Partner Program model, MxP does not take a percentage of your independent service fees. Separate incentives may have their own terms.



Q: Who is responsible for technical issues or problems with a Partner project?

MxP Support handles platform functionality, product defects and MxP-specific technical issues. Partners remain responsible for the services they agree to deliver, such as strategy, setup, implementation and training. Partner Growth helps with program participation and growth guidance.

Additional Support


Q: Who should I contact if I still have questions?

Email certifications@MxP.com for Certification Program transition, membership or assessment-record questions, or for help reaching Billing. Once you have an assigned Partner Growth Specialist, contact them for Partner Program guidance. Do not share private account or payment details in public Community posts.
