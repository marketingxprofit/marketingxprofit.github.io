Creator Studio - Pricing

Creator Studio can create everything from a single social media post to full sets of ad designs, flyers, images and short promotional videos, all from a natural-language prompt. Because each request differs in format and scope, the work Creator Studio performs varies from one session to the next: the number of designs generated, whether images or video are created, and how many follow-up edits are requested.

This article explains how Creator Studio sessions work, how usage is priced, and what influences the amount of work involved, using real-world examples.

Understanding Creator Studio Sessions


A Creator Studio session is one or more conversational turns. In each turn the AI interprets your request, chooses a format, generates designs, images or video, and returns results you can edit or download. Each session is measured by the work performed, not simply by the number of prompts you send.

A typical session may include:

  • Understanding your request and selecting a format (for example, Instagram Post or flyer)
  • Generating multiple design variations for a single request
  • Generating AI images or short video clips
  • Rendering designs with individually editable layers
  • Offering follow-up suggestions, such as resizing to a different format
  • Applying changes requested in follow-up messages
  • Saving the session as a project


Video generation, more design variations and repeated refinements generally involve more processing than a single design or image.

Creator Studio Pricing Overview


Creator Studio uses usage-based pricing. Included usage and available output quality depend on your access type and AI Employee subscription. Agency users get Creator Studio usage at no additional cost, subject to fair use. Sub-account users can access Creator Studio through an AI Employee subscription or pay-per-use billing.

Usage Limits: Creator Studio usage is measured in rolling five-hour windows, with additional usage protections. Included allowances vary by plan. When an allowance is reached, additional usage may be billed at pay-per-use rates if Extra Usage is enabled. Otherwise, access pauses until the allowance resets.

Pay-Per-Use Pricing: Creator Studio charges are based on the AI models, output quality and generations used during a session. Costs vary with the type and amount of content created.

Factors that influence Creator Studio usage include:

  • Content type (designs, images or video)
  • Number of design variations generated
  • Video generation
  • Selected output quality (Standard, High or Ultra)
  • Follow-up edits and regenerations
  • Resizing designs to new formats

Before You Start


Creator Studio is a Labs feature. An agency admin must enable it for each sub-account.


Enable Creator Studio


  1. Go to Agency Settings → Labs → Sub-Account.
  2. Find Creator Studio and click Activate Feature.
  3. Search for the sub-account, switch Enable on, and click Save.


Access Creator Studio


  1. Open the sub-account.
  2. Press ⌘K (or click Search).
  3. Type Creator Studio and open it.


How to Create a Design


  1. Open Creator Studio.
  2. (Optional) Choose Design, Image, or Video clip under the prompt box.
  3. Describe what you want to create. Include details such as:
    • Business name
    • Offer or promotion
    • Call to action (CTA)
  4. Click Send to generate your content.
  5. Results typically appear within 2–3 minutes.
  6. To make changes:
    • Click Edit on a design to modify individual layers.
    • Type a follow-up message to request changes.

What Affects Creator Studio Usage?


Creator Studio usage is based on the work performed during a session rather than the number of prompts alone. Sessions become more resource-intensive as more content is generated or refined. Factors that increase overall work include:

  • Generating video rather than designs or images
  • Generating more design variations per request
  • Selecting higher output quality
  • Follow-up edits and multiple refinements
  • Regenerating content, such as a video with incorrect text
  • Resizing designs to new formats


Based on the examples above:

  • Video generation is the largest single driver: one 6-second video cost more than three Instagram post designs.
  • Design sessions cost roughly the same whether they produce social posts or flyers, with small differences based on the design generated.
  • Getting it right the first time keeps usage down. Include the format, dimensions and exact text in your prompt to avoid resizing or regenerating.

Creator Studio Usage and Spending Notifications


Creator Studio sends usage notifications to help users monitor consumption and spending before they reach their limits. Notifications are enabled automatically for users with access to Creator Studio, so no additional setup is required.


Usage Notifications


Agency users and AI Employee subscribers receive email and in-app notifications as their usage increases:

  • 75% usage: Usage alert
  • 90% usage: Warning


Depending on billing permissions, the notification can provide actions to adjust usage limits or request an increase from the Agency Admin.


Pay-Per-Use Notifications


While an account is using pay-per-use billing, a persistent banner shows that usage is being charged at pay-per-use rates.

For detailed information about configuring spending limits, requesting additional usage, and managing limit-increase requests, see AI Usage Limits.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: How much does Creator Studio cost?

Creator Studio is included at no additional cost for agency users on all MxP agency plans, subject to fair use. Sub-account users can access Creator Studio through an AI Employee Starter ($20/month), Growth ($50/month), or Unlimited ($97/month) subscription, or through pay-per-use billing. The Starter plan is available exclusively to agencies on the $497/month plan.


Q: Does every prompt do the same amount of work?

No. The work varies with the type of content, the number of designs generated, the output quality selected, and any follow-up edits.


Q: Does video generation involve more work than designs?

Generally, yes. Generating video typically requires more processing than generating designs or images.


Q: Does regenerating content count toward my usage?

Yes. Each regeneration is a new generation and counts toward your usage. Including the format, dimensions and exact text in your prompt helps avoid regenerations.


Q: Which plans include Ultra output quality?

Ultra quality is available to agency users on the $497 plan and to sub-accounts on the AI Employee Growth and Unlimited plans.


Q: Does Creator Studio usage affect my Ask AI or AI Studio usage?

No. On AI Employee plans, Ask AI, AI Studio and Creator Studio each have their own usage allowance.
