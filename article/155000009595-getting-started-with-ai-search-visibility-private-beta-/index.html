Getting Started with AI & Search Visibility (Private Beta)

AI & Search Visibility brings website SEO audits, AI search tracking, and content creation tools together in MxP. It helps you understand how your business appears in traditional and AI-powered search, identify opportunities to improve visibility, and take action on recommendations. Available FREE DURING THE BETA, the tool gives you a starting point for monitoring and improving your online presence.

AI and Search Visibility Intro


AI & Search Visibility helps you understand how search engines and AI platforms discover, evaluate, and mention your business. Instead of reviewing website health and AI search performance separately, you can monitor both from one project, identify opportunities for improvement, and implement recommended fixes directly on your website from the tool.


The beta includes three primary modules:


  • SEO Audit: Scan your website to identify technical SEO issues, review recommendations, and implement fixes directly on your website to improve site health.

  • AI Search: Monitor how your brand appears in AI-generated answers, track mentions and citations, compare visibility with competitors, and take action on recommended website improvements directly from the tool.

  • Content: Create SEO- and AEO-focused blog content using AI-assisted research, drafting, and quality checks when the module is enabled for your account.


Key Benefits of AI & Search Visibility


  • Centralized Visibility Tracking: Monitor website SEO health and AI search visibility from a single workspace.

  • Actionable SEO Recommendations: Identify technical website issues and review recommended fixes instead of manually searching for problems.

  • AI Search Monitoring: Understand whether AI platforms mention your business, reference your website, or recommend competitors.

  • Competitive Insights: Compare your brand's AI search presence with businesses competing for the same customers.

  • Prioritized Improvements: Focus on recommended actions that help you decide what to address first.

  • AI-Assisted Content Creation: Create data-backed, SEO & AEO optimized blog posts based on relevant keywords, search trends, competitor performance, and your brand’s products and services. The tool suggests topics and content briefs based on what users and AI agents are actively searching for, as well as the types of content AI platforms reference and cite in their responses. Once approved, it generates relevant, SEO- and AEO-optimized blog content, adds internal links to appropriate pages, and can publish the post directly if your website is hosted within MxP.

AI Search


Customers increasingly use AI-powered search experiences to discover businesses, compare services, and find recommendations. AI Search helps you understand whether your business appears in those answers and how its visibility changes over time.


During the beta, you can track your brand across up to 4 supported AI search platforms:


  • Google AI Overview

  • Google AI Mode

  • ChatGPT

  • Gemini


AI Search provides insights such as brand visibility, mention rate, citation rate, average position, share of voice, sentiment, and competitor comparisons.


You can also inspect captured AI answers to see which brands were mentioned, where your brand appeared, and which sources were cited.


Tip: AI-generated answers can vary between runs. Look at trends across multiple completed runs instead of treating one result as a definitive measure of your brand's visibility.


What Are Prompts in AI Visibility?


Prompts are questions or search queries that users ask on AI platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini when looking for information about a brand, its products, services, or related topics.


AI & Search Visibility analyzes your brand's online presence to identify relevant prompts that potential customers might use when searching for businesses like yours. These prompts help you understand how your brand appears in AI-generated responses.


How are prompts suggested?


  • AI-Generated Suggestions: The tool recommends relevant prompts based on your brand, products, services, and online presence.

  • Number of Suggestions: AI can suggest up to 10 prompts, but the actual number may vary depending on the information available about your brand. Some businesses may receive only 2–3 suggestions.

  • Brand Relevance: Suggestions focus on questions potential customers are likely to ask when researching your brand or looking for similar products and services.


Prompt Categories


Prompt Categories help you organize related prompts by grouping those that share a common objective under a single thematic name. This makes it easier to analyze AI search visibility across similar questions without reviewing each prompt individually.


Categories appear in the Prompts tab and other relevant dashboard tabs, allowing you to:


  • View Grouped Metrics: Analyze performance and visibility metrics for an entire category of related prompts.

  • Identify Trends: Understand how your brand performs across different topics or search objectives.

  • Drill Down into Individual Prompts: Explore the prompts within a category to see their individual metrics and identify areas for improvement.

SEO Audit


A technically healthy website is easier for search engines to crawl and understand. SEO Audit examines your website for issues that may affect discoverability and provides recommendations to help you improve its technical health.


Once a scan finishes, the report includes:


  • A website health score out of 100.

  • Counts of errors, warnings, and notices.

  • Pages requiring attention.

  • Crawlability, indexability, and redirect findings.

  • Internal and external linking issues.

  • Core Web Vitals and performance findings.

  • Resource, image, and content-related issues.


Each identified action includes information to help you understand the issue and determine how to address it.


Important: Your first SEO audit starts automatically when you create a project. Allow a few minutes for the scan to complete before reviewing its results.


Ask Visibility


Ask Visibility provides a conversational way to explore your project's search performance. Instead of navigating through multiple reports, you can ask questions or select a suggested action to investigate a particular area.


The chat interface includes suggestions such as:


  • Run SEO Audit: Access the SEO audit workflow.

  • Check AI Visibility: Explore your brand's presence in AI-generated search answers.

  • Chat with AI: Ask questions about the data across dashboard modules, including SEO Audit and AI Search, to uncover insights, explore trends, and dig deeper into your website's performance and AI search visibility.


You can also enter your own questions in the chat box.

How to Get Started with AI & Search Visibility?


Creating a project establishes the business and website you want to monitor. Accurate business information is important because it helps the tool recognize your brand in AI-generated answers and produce relevant reports and recommendations.


Follow these steps to begin:


  1. Open the MxP location (sub-account) that has access to the AI & Search Visibility beta.

  2. Go to AI & Search Visibility from main menu in the old UI & Marketing > AI & Search Visibility in the new UI and start creating a new project.

  3. Enter your project's information, including the project name, business name, and business website.


    Important: Use the official customer-facing business name as it normally appears in search results and AI-generated answers. Inconsistent naming can affect how accurately brand mentions are recognized.

  4. Complete the available project configuration, including AI search prompts and competitors. You can refine your AI search tracking configuration later.

  5. Finish creating the project. Your first SEO & AI Search audit starts automatically.

  6. Allow the audit to finish, then review the initial website health findings in SEO & AI Search Audit.




For detailed instructions, see Create Your First Project and Run Your First Site Audit in the Related Articles section.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: My brand appears in AI-generated answers but is not being counted. What should I check?

Check the Business Name in your project's settings. Make sure it matches the name customers and AI platforms commonly use when referring to your business. Differences in naming can affect brand recognition. If the issue continues, share an example in the GoKollab beta community.


Q: Why did my AI visibility score change when I haven't made changes to my website?

AI-generated answers can vary between tracking runs, even when your website remains unchanged. Review results across several completed runs before determining whether your visibility has significantly improved or declined.


Q: Can I track the same website domain in multiple projects?

Technically, you can create a new project with the same domain. There are no issues, but it's not recommended.


Q: Why do some AI Search results appear incomplete?

When a project is first created, the initial scan takes approximately 30–60 minutes to collect all the data. However, results from some AI/LLM platforms become available within 5–10 minutes, and we start displaying them as soon as they are ready. For the complete dataset, users should refer to the report after the next scan, which refreshes daily at 10:00 AM UTC, automatically.

Q: What happens when I reach my SEO audit limit?

Each project receives 10 audits per monthly cycle during the beta. When the limit is reached, the latest completed report remains accessible, allowing you to continue working on identified issues. Additional audits become available according to the project's monthly allowance cycle.


Q: What if the SEO Audit identifies an issue that I cannot change on my MxP website?

We’ve prioritized the most common, high-impact issues for Auto-Fix support. If Auto-Fix isn’t available for a specific issue that’s important to you, you can raise a request in our community channel to help us prioritize it. In the meantime, you can copy the recommended fix and manually apply it to your website, regardless of where it was built. We’ll continue expanding Auto-Fix capabilities to support more issues over time.


Q: Where can I ask questions, provide feedback, or report a beta issue?

Use the GoKollab beta community for product questions, feedback, and issue reporting. Include the relevant module, a description of the issue, and screenshots where helpful.
