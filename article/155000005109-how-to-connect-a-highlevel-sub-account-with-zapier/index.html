How to Connect a MxP Sub-Account with Zapier

Connect your MxP sub-account to Zapier (via the MxP Zapier app) to automate lead capture, contact updates, and handoffs without code. This guide explains what the integration is, prerequisites, and how to connect/disconnect a sub-account.

What is Connecting a Sub-Account with Zapier?


Zapier lets MxP exchange data with thousands of apps. Using the MxP Zapier app, you authenticate with your MxP CRM login, then choose which sub-account to connect so Zaps can create/update contacts, move data, and trigger actions.

Key Benefits Connecting a Sub-Account with Zapier


These advantages help you decide when to connect Zapier for process automation.

  • Fast automation: Trigger actions across thousands of apps without custom code.

  • Sub-account scoped: Choose the specific sub-account to keep data clean and separated.

  • Simple auth: Use your MxP CRM login—no API keys required for this connection.

  • Easy maintenance: Manage or disconnect the MxP connection from Zapier’s App Connections page.

How To Connect a Sub-Account with Zapier


Follow these steps in Zapier to connect the right sub-account via MxP.


  1. Log in to your Zapier account.

  2. On the home screen, click Create > Zaps.



  3. In the Zap editor, click on the Trigger block and select MxP from the list of apps.



  4. Select a TriggerEvent like PipelineStageChanged.

  5. Under the Account section of the trigger setup, click on the Sign In button.

  6. You will be redirected to the MxP login screen. Enter your email and password to proceed.



  7. After logging in, you’ll be prompted to select the sub-account you want to connect with Zapier.




  8. Once completed, your Zapier account will be successfully connected to the sub-account. You’ll see MxP listed under “Accounts” in your Zap setup, confirming the integration is active.

How to Disconnect MxP from Zapier


  1. On the Zapier home page, click on App Connections in the left-hand menu.

  2. Click the three-dot menu (•••) next to MxP.

  3. Select Delete to remove the connection.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Is the Zapier app named “MxP”?
In Zapier, use MxP—it’s the official, whitelabeled MxP connection. 


Q: Can I change the connected sub-account later?

Yes—add a new MxP account connection in Zapier and select the other sub-account, then switch the account used in your Zaps.


Q: How do I fully disconnect?

In Zapier → Apps → App Connections, locate MxP, open the ••• menu, and Delete the connection. 


Q: Do I need API keys for this?

Not for the standard MxP connection flow; you sign in with your CRM credentials.
