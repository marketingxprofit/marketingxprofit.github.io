Voice intelligence

Voice Intelligence
Automatic call summaries, QA scores, field suggestions and dispositions after every recorded call.
Overview

Every call ends with work to do. You write notes, update the contact and log how the call went. Voice Intelligence does this for you after each recorded call, so your team can move on to the next one.

Labs Feature

Voice Intelligence is a Labs feature. We are still improving it, and your feedback helps.

What you get after each call

ResultWhat it gives you
Call summaryWhy the contact called, the key points and the next steps. No more typing notes.
QA scorecardA score out of 10, a score for each area you care about, and tips on what to do better.
Field suggestionsContact details mentioned on the call, like a name or email, ready to save.
Call disposition suggestionThe call outcome, picked from your own disposition list.

Before you start

Turned on in Labs — Voice Intelligence must be turned on for your account in Labs.
Call recording is on — Voice Intelligence only works on recorded calls.
MxP numbers — it works on calls made and received with MxP phone numbers.
Real conversations only — it skips unanswered calls and very short calls. See Which calls get Voice Intelligence.
No extra charge — there is no charge beyond your usual call transcription. Hover on "Charges apply" on the settings page for details.
3

Which calls get Voice Intelligence

Voice Intelligence works on any recorded call that uses one of your MxP phone numbers. It doesn't matter where the call is answered or made.

Call typeSupported?
Web app and dialerYes
Mobile appYes
Desk phones (SIP)Yes
Calls forwarded to another phone (e.g. a cell phone)Yes. The call still goes through your MxP number, so it is recorded.
Voice AI callsYes, when call recording is on for that number. The summary, scorecard and suggestions cover the call your Voice AI agent handled.
IVR calls and calls placed by a workflowYes, when they are recorded.
Voicemails left for youYes. You get a summary of who called, why, and whether they asked for a call back.
Your calls that reach a voicemail greetingYes. The summary says the call went to voicemail, and the suggested disposition is your voicemail option if your list has one.
Not Covered

These calls don't get Voice Intelligence:

  • Calls made from a personal phone outside the app, because they don't use your MxP number.
  • Calls on phone numbers connected from another phone provider.
  • Calls on numbers where call recording is off.
  • Calls that weren't answered, and calls too short to have real content, like a hang-up after a few words.
Where You See the Results
It depends on where the call happened

Every result is saved on the call in the contact's conversation.

  • Calls in the web app dialer: the results open in the dialer as soon as the call ends. Confirm the disposition and save field suggestions right away.
  • Desk phone, forwarded, Voice AI, IVR and workflow calls: there is no screen to review during the call. Open the contact's conversation, then select View full details on the call. You can check and save field suggestions there at any time.
Good to Know

On calls outside the web app dialer, the suggested disposition shows as a tag on the call, marked with a sparkle icon. You can see it in Conversations, but you can't change or save it there.

Setup Guide
Turn it on and tailor it to your team
Enable Voice Intelligence, then shape the summary, scorecard, fields, dispositions and language.
4

Turn on Voice Intelligence

  1. Go to Settings → Phone System.
  2. Select the Voice tab, then Call Recording & Transcription.
  3. Select Voice Intelligence on the left.
  4. Turn on the Voice intelligence switch.

The transcript and the call summary are always included. Under Additional features, check the boxes for the QA scorecard, field suggestions and call disposition suggestions you want.

On by Default

All three additional features are on by default.

5

Make it fit your business

Admins set Voice Intelligence up once, and every call follows it.

Note

Changes apply to new calls only. Past calls keep their results.

Customize 1
Choose what goes in the call summary
  1. Next to the summary line, select Configure.
  2. You see the summary sections. By default these are Summary, Next steps, Key points, and Concerns and objections.
  3. To change a section, select the edit icon. You can change its name, its description and its format: a single sentence, a bullet list or a short paragraph.
  4. To add a section, select Add section. To remove one, select the X. Drag sections to change their order.
  5. Select Save changes.
Tip

The description tells Voice Intelligence what to write. Keep it short and specific, for example "Each objection the contact raised and how the agent answered it."

Customize 2
Score calls on what matters to you
  1. Next to QA scorecard, select Configure.
  2. You see the scoring categories. By default these are Greeting and introduction, Understanding the need, Clear answers, Handling concerns, and Closing and next step.
  3. Edit a category to change its name or description. The description says what a good call looks like, so match it to your call script.
  4. Select Add category to add one, or select the X to remove one. Drag to change the order.
  5. Select Save changes.
How Scoring Works

Each category gets a score from 0 to 10, and the overall score is the average. If a category doesn't come up on a call, it is skipped, not counted as 0.

Customize 3
Pick which contact fields to fill in
  1. Next to Field suggestions, select Configure.
  2. You see each contact field and its instruction. By default these are First Name, Last Name and Email.
  3. Select Add field and choose any contact field, including your own custom fields. Then write a short instruction, for example "The contact's budget in US dollars. Leave empty if not mentioned."
  4. Turn on Only suggest values for empty fields if you never want to replace data a contact already has.
  5. Select Save changes.
Customize 4
Use your own call outcomes

Voice Intelligence picks the call disposition only from your own list. To change the list, select Manage list next to Call disposition suggestions. This opens the Custom Dispositions tab.

Customize 5
Choose the language of your results

Under Settings, pick an Output language. Summaries, notes and other results are written in this language.

6

See the results in Conversations

  1. Open the contact's conversation. After a call, the call card shows the start of the summary.
  2. Select View full details.
  3. Use the tabs to see the Summary, Field suggestion, QA scorecard, and Recording & transcript. The top of the window shows the call disposition and the overall score.
Timing

Results are usually ready a few seconds after the call recording is saved.

Then
Save field suggestions to the contact
  1. Open the Field suggestion tab.
  2. Check each value you want to keep. You can edit a value before you save it.
  3. Select Save Updates. Only the checked values are saved to the contact.
7

See the results in the dialer

When a call ends in the dialer, the results open next to the call summary. The Review & update tab shows the suggested call disposition and the suggested contact fields.

  1. Check the disposition. Pick a different one from the list if needed.
  2. Check the field values you want to keep, and edit them if needed.
  3. Select Save Updates.

The QA scorecard, Summary and Recording & transcript tabs show the same results as in Conversations.

You're All Set

Review, tweak if needed, save, and move straight on to your next call.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Why didn't a call get any results?
Check that:
  • Call recording was on for that phone number.
  • The call used a MxP phone number.
  • Voice Intelligence was on when the call happened. It does not run on older calls.
  • The call was answered and long enough to have real content. Very short calls are skipped.
Results can also take a few seconds after the call. Refresh the page and try again.
Q: Does Voice Intelligence change my contacts on its own?
No. Field suggestions and the call disposition wait for someone on your team to check and save them.
Q: If I change my settings, do older calls change?
No. New settings apply to new calls only.
Q: Can I turn off a feature I don't need?
Yes. Uncheck the QA scorecard, field suggestions or call disposition suggestions in settings. The transcript and summary are always included while Voice Intelligence is on.
Q: Can I use my own custom fields?
Yes. When you add a field under Field suggestions, you can choose any contact field, including custom fields.
Q: Can I edit the suggested disposition from Conversations?
Not for calls outside the web app dialer. There it shows as a tag with a sparkle icon that you can see but not change or save. For calls in the web app dialer, confirm or change it in the Review & update tab.
Q: Does Voice Intelligence cost extra?
No. There is no charge beyond your usual call transcription. Hover on "Charges apply" on the settings page for details.
