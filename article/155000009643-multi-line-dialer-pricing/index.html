Phone System · Pricing & Billing
Multi-Line Dialer Pricing
What each charge on a multi-line dialing session means, how it's calculated, and how to check it in your wallet.
Overview

Multi-line dialing calls several contacts from your call list at once. You pay for the connections you use, not for the contacts you add. A single dialing round can produce several wallet transactions, one for each connection, and these are not duplicate charges.

This guide explains each part of the bill with worked examples. It builds on the Phone System Pricing & Billing Guide, which lists the current per-minute and per-region rates.

How Multi-Line Dialing Is Billed

Think of multi-line dialing as a small shared phone room. Your browser joins the room, and the system calls the contacts on your list. The first contact who answers is connected to you. Any other contact who answers hears your callback message instead and is placed in your callback queue.

Every dialing round is billed in three parts:

Your agent connection + Answered contact calls + Conference room usage = Core call cost
PartWhat It IsWhat Affects the Amount
Agent connectionYour browser's live connection to the phone systemHow long you stay connected, not just how long you talk to a contact
Contact callThe call to each contact who answers, including a contact who only hears the callback messageThe destination country, call duration and outbound rate
ConferenceThe shared room that connects you to the answering contactEach participant's time in the room and the conference region
Optional Charges

Call recording, transcription and text-to-speech are billed separately at their standard rates when you use them. They are not included in the examples below.

Good to Know

There is no flat fee for multi-line dialing. It uses the same Outbound Call and Conference Call rates as the rest of the phone system. Adding contacts to a call list is free, and a contact waiting in your callback queue doesn't add call minutes.

Which Pricing Applies to Your Call

Not every call made from a call list uses the shared room. Find your scenario first:

What You DoHow It's Billed
Make a normal outbound callStandard outbound pricing: your agent connection plus the contact call, often shown as one transaction
Dial one line from a call listStandard outbound pricing, the same as a normal outbound call
Dial two or three lines at onceMulti-line pricing: agent connection, each answered contact, and conference usage
Call back a contact from the callback queueA new, standard outbound call. The caller ID may differ from the original round.
Heads Up

Some phone providers only support dialing one contact at a time from a call list. Those calls are billed as standard outbound calls, and the shared-room and transfer examples in this guide don't apply to them.

Rounding

Calls are billed in full minutes, rounded up. A call lasting 1–60 seconds is billed as 1 minute, and 61–120 seconds as 2 minutes. Conference time is rounded up separately for each participant.

Example 2: Two Contacts Answer

You dial two US contacts at once, and both answer. Contact A is connected to you and talks for 21 seconds. Contact B hears your callback message for 16 seconds, hangs up, and waits in your callback queue. You stay connected for under a minute.

Step 1
You start the round

Your browser connects to the room. Adding the two contacts costs nothing.

Step 2
Contact A answers and joins you in the room

Their call is billed, and the room now has two participants: you and Contact A.

Step 3
Contact B answers and hears the callback message

Their call is billed because they answered, but they never join the room, so they add no conference usage. Once they hang up, waiting in the queue costs nothing.

ComponentCalculationAmount
Agent connection1 min × $0.004$0.0040
Contact A (connected, 21 sec)1 min × $0.0126$0.0126
Contact B (callback message, 16 sec)1 min × $0.0126$0.0126
Conference (you + Contact A)(1 + 1) participant-min × $0.0018$0.0036
TotalBase usage$0.0328
Why This Matters

A contact who answers and hears the callback message is billed for their call, but they are not counted as a conference participant. Only connections that actually join the room add conference usage.

Example 3: A Longer Session with a Warm Transfer

You dial three US contacts at once and stay connected and in the room for 10 minutes:

  • Contact A answers, talks with you for 5 minutes, then you warm-transfer them to a colleague. They talk for 3 more minutes, so Contact A is connected for 8 minutes in total.
  • The transfer recipient is on the call for 3 minutes.
  • Contact B answers and hears the callback message for under a minute.
  • Contact C is still ringing when the round ends and never answers.
ComponentCalculationAmount
Agent connection10 min × $0.004$0.0400
Contact A8 min × $0.0126$0.1008
Transfer recipient3 min × $0.0126$0.0378
Contact B (callback message)1 min × $0.0126$0.0126
Contact C (never answered)No answered usage$0.0000
Conference (you + Contact A + transfer recipient)(10 + 8 + 3) participant-min × $0.0018$0.0378
TotalBase usage$0.2290
Important

A transfer doesn't end the original contact's call. Contact A is billed for the full 8 minutes they were connected, including the time after the transfer, not just the 5 minutes they spent talking with you. Your actual bill is based on the real connection and room times recorded for each call.

7

How Conference Minutes and Transfers Are Counted

Participant-Minutes
Every participant in the room counts

One minute with two participants is two participant-minutes. One minute with three participants is three. Each participant's room time is rounded up to the next full minute on its own.

  • You and a contact, each in a US-region room for 20 seconds: (1 + 1) × $0.0018 = $0.0036
  • You, a contact and a transfer recipient, each under a minute in a Singapore-region room: (1 + 1 + 1) × $0.003 = $0.009
Who Leaves When
Usage stops per connection, not per room

Conference usage doesn't need three people in the room. If you leave while a contact and a transfer recipient are still talking, your agent connection stops, but their calls and conference usage continue until they hang up. If you stay connected between calls, your agent connection keeps running.

Transfers add a new connection. They aren't a free continuation of the original call:

Transfer TypeWhat's Billed
Blind transferA new call to the transfer recipient. The original contact stays billable until they hang up, and your agent connection stops when you disconnect.
Warm transferA new call to the transfer recipient plus conference usage while people are joined. You and the original contact are each billed for your own connected time.
Transfer during a multi-line roundThe transfer uses the round's existing room. The recipient adds conference usage, but no second room is created.

Rebilling: What Your Clients Pay

Multi-line charges follow the same rebilling settings as the rest of the phone system. Your agency wallet is charged for usage, and your rebilling configuration decides what the sub-account pays.

Rebilling SettingSub-Account PaysExample 2 at $0.0328 Base
Covered by agencyNothing. The agency absorbs usage.$0.0000
Pay per use, 1×The base usage rate$0.0328
Pay per use, 2×Twice the base rate (100% markup)$0.0656
Rebilling for phone usage is configured in the wallet settings.
Watch Out

Agency discounts can make your agency debit lower than the base rate shown in a transaction's details, so don't simply multiply the agency debit by your markup. To check a charge, match the same call in both the agency and sub-account wallets.

If rebilling shows a payment-method hold, resolve it before expecting usage to be rebilled.

Reading Your Wallet Transactions

A multi-line round usually appears as several transactions: an Outbound Call for each answered contact, a transaction for your agent connection, and a Conference Call for the room. Expand each one to see its role, duration and rate.

Timing
When charges appear

Contact charges post after each call ends. Agent and conference charges post after the round's connections and room have closed. Charges are processed in the background, so they may not appear right away or in the order the calls happened. Recording, transcription and text-to-speech charges may post later still.

Checklist
How to check a round
  1. Note the contacts you dialed and the time of the round.
  2. Find and expand the matching wallet transactions.
  3. Sort them into agent connection, contact calls and conference.
  4. Check each duration against the rounding rules and your rates.
  5. If rebilling is on, compare the same transactions in the sub-account wallet.
Tip

When a transaction shows both a total and its component amounts, add up the total only. Adding the components on top would count the same usage twice. In conference details, use the participant count and per-participant minutes; a Total minutes field showing 0 doesn't mean there was no conference usage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is there a separate fee for using the multi-line dialer?
No. There's no flat multi-line fee. You pay the standard Outbound Call rates for your agent connection and each answered contact, plus Conference Call rates for the shared room.
Q: Why do I see several transactions for one dialing round?
Each transaction represents a different connection: your agent connection, each answered contact, and the conference room. Expand them to see the role of each one. They are not duplicate charges.
Q: Am I charged for a contact who only heard the callback message?
Yes. They answered, so their call is billed at the outbound rate for its duration. They don't add conference usage because they never joined the room.
Q: Am I charged for contacts who don't answer?
No. A contact whose call rings and is canceled, or who never picks up, has no answered usage and no charge.
Q: Do contacts waiting in the callback queue cost money?
No. Once a contact hangs up, waiting in the queue adds no call minutes. When you call them back, that's billed as a new standard outbound call.
Q: Does dialing more lines at once cost more?
Only if more contacts answer. Each answered contact adds a contact call charge. Dialing more lines doesn't add charges for contacts who don't pick up.
Q: Why does my agent connection cost more than my talk time?
Your agent connection is billed for the whole time your browser is connected, including time spent waiting for contacts to answer or between calls, not just the time you spend talking.
Q: Why don't my charges show up right after the call?
Charges are processed in the background after calls and the conference room close, so they can take a little time to appear and may not post in call order. Recording and transcription charges can post later.
Q: Are multi-line charges included in rebilling?
Yes. Agent, contact and conference charges follow your phone usage rebilling settings, the same as other calls. For example, at a 2× multiplier, the sub-account pays twice the base usage.
