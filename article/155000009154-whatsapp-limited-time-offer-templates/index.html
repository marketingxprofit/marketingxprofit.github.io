WhatsApp Limited-Time Offer Templates

Create and send WhatsApp templates with built-in expiration dates and countdown timers to drive urgency and engagement for time-bound promotions.

What Are Limited-Time Offer Templates?

A Limited-Time Offer (LTO) template is a Meta-supported WhatsApp template type that displays an expiration date and a running countdown timer directly inside the message. Unlike standard marketing templates, an LTO template makes the deadline visible to the customer — the timer counts down in real time.

LTO templates also include a Copy Code button, allowing customers to copy a promotional code with a single tap without having to remember or retype it.

These templates are only available under the Marketing category and must go through the standard Meta template approval process before they can be sent.

WhatsApp renders the countdown directly in the customer's chat. It updates as the deadline approaches and turns red during the final hour before the offer expires.

How the Template Looks to the Customer

An approved LTO template renders in the customer's WhatsApp as:

ComponentWhat the Customer Sees
HeaderAn image or video.
BodyOffer text with personalization variables — e.g., "Hi {{1}}, use code {{2}} for 25% off!"
ButtonsBoth Copy code and URL buttons are required for Limited-Time Offer templates. Neither button can be removed.

The mobile example illustrates how an approved Limited-Time Offer appears in WhatsApp.

Availability note: The countdown timer is only visible on WhatsApp for iOS and Android. WhatsApp Web and desktop may display an unsupported-message notice instead of the Limited-Time Offer content.

Before You Begin

An active WhatsApp connection, the required permissions, and complete offer details help you prepare the template before submitting it for approval.

  • The sub-account has an active WhatsApp subscription and has completed onboarding.

  • You have the Create/Edit WhatsApp Templates permission in your Roles & Permissions settings.

  • Your offer details are ready — offer text, promotional code, expiration date and time, and any header media.
  • Your contacts have opted in to receive WhatsApp messages from your business.

  • Labs feature: If the Limited time offer template type is unavailable, open the MxP Sub-Account, go to Settings → Labs, and look for WhatsApp Limited Time Offers. Enable it if available.

How to Create an LTO Template

Creating an LTO template involves selecting the Marketing category, configuring the offer, and submitting the finished template to Meta for approval.

  1. Open the Template Builder

    From the MxP Sub-Account, select Settings → WhatsApp → Templates. Click Create Template, then choose Blank Template.



  2. Set Up the Template

    Under Set up template, select Marketing, choose Limited time offer, then click Next.



  3. Configure Template Details

    Under Template details, configure the offer information.

    • Template name and Language: Enter the template name and select its language.

    • Header: Add an image or video if needed.

    • Limited time offer: Enter the offer Heading and review the Expiration date and time fields.

    • Message body: Enter the offer message and supported personalization variables, such as {{1}} for the customer name and {{2}} for the offer code. Provide realistic sample values for all variables.

    • Buttons: Complete the required Copy code and URL button details. Both are mandatory.


    • Note: LTO templates do not support a footer component. Do not add a footer — it will cause the template to be rejected by Meta.

  4. Preview and Submit

    Review the Live preview, check the offer details, and correct any validation errors. Click Submit for approval to send the template to Meta for review.

  5. Monitor Approval Status

    Return to Settings → WhatsApp → Templates to check the approval status. A template marked In Review is still being assessed. Once Approved, it is ready to send. If rejected, review Meta's reason, correct the issue, and resubmit.

Using AI: MxP's AI template writer can also generate Limited Time Offer drafts, including the offer heading, expiration, promotional code, and image. Review the generated content before submitting it for approval.

How to Send an LTO Template

After Meta approves a Limited-Time Offer template, you can reuse it for future campaigns. The expiration used by WhatsApp is supplied when the message is sent, rather than permanently fixed in the approved template.

The template builder also displays Expiration date and time fields during creation. Before sending, confirm the deadline applied to your campaign and review the promotional code.


From Conversations

Select an approved Limited-Time Offer template from the contact's conversation and review the message before sending.

  1. Open the MxP Sub-Account and select Conversations.

  2. Open the recipient's conversation and select WhatsApp as the messaging channel.

  3. Choose the WhatsApp template option in the conversation composer.

  4. In Send WhatsApp Template, select the approved template from Select WhatsApp Template.

  5. Review the message, media, and Preview.

  6. If needed, click Replace Image to change the image for this send.

  7. Confirm the applicable offer deadline and promotional code before sending.

  8. Click Send.


Through Workflows

Add a WhatsApp action in your workflow, select the approved LTO template, and review the available offer code and expiration settings. Verify the values used by the workflow before activating it.

Through Bulk Actions

Go to Contacts, select your audience, and start a WhatsApp Bulk Action. Choose the approved LTO template, review the offer code and expiration details where supported, then launch or schedule the campaign.

Through Calendar WhatsApp Sends

Approved Limited-Time Offer templates are also supported in applicable Calendar WhatsApp sending flows. Select the approved template and review the campaign's offer information and expiration before sending.


Tip: You can reuse an unchanged, approved LTO template for later promotions without requesting another approval. Review the applicable deadline and promotional code for each campaign before sending.

Constraints and Limitations

Meta requirements, supported devices, recipient restrictions, and expiration settings determine how LTO messages can be created and delivered.

  • Marketing only: LTO templates cannot be created under the Utility or Authentication categories.

  • U.S. recipient restriction: Meta currently blocks WhatsApp Marketing template messages to recipients with U.S. phone numbers. Limited-Time Offer templates are Marketing templates, so these messages cannot be delivered to U.S. recipients while this restriction remains in effect.

  • No footer: LTO templates do not support a footer component. Adding one will result in Meta rejection.
  • Mobile only: The countdown timer is only visible on WhatsApp for iOS and Android. WhatsApp Web and desktop may show an unsupported-message notice.

  • Meta approval: A new Limited-Time Offer template requires Meta approval before use. Once approved, an unchanged template can be reused for later campaigns without creating a new one.

  • Required buttons: Both Copy code and URL buttons are mandatory for this template type and cannot be removed.

  • Expiration: Offer deadlines are campaign-specific. The builder displays expiration fields, while available send-time controls may vary. Verify the final deadline in the chosen sending flow.

  • Expired offers: Sending is not necessarily blocked solely because the selected deadline has passed. WhatsApp displays an already-expired offer as expired.

  • Messaging limits apply: LTO messages sent outside the 24-hour customer service window count toward your Meta messaging tier limits.

Use Cases

Limited-Time Offer templates can support promotions where a visible expiration deadline helps customers understand how long an offer remains available.

  • Flash Sales: Send a 4-hour flash sale message with a copy-code button. Customers see exactly how much time is left, reducing hesitation and driving immediate action.

  • Abandoned Cart Recovery: Trigger an LTO template via Workflow when a contact abandons a cart, offering a discount code valid for 24 hours to encourage completion.

  • Appointment Promotions: A salon or clinic can promote same-week availability at a discounted rate with a countdown that expires at end of day, filling empty slots efficiently.

  • Seasonal Campaigns: Run weekend or holiday promotions using a Bulk Action to reach a large audience simultaneously, with the timer creating shared urgency across all recipients.

  • Membership or Subscription Renewals: Alert customers to a renewal discount expiring at a specific date. The visible countdown replaces vague "limited time" language with a concrete deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a new template for each promotion?

No. You can reuse an approved Limited-Time Offer template for future campaigns with different expiration details and promotional codes. You do not need to submit a new template when the approved template itself is unchanged.

Q: Why can't my customer see the countdown timer?

The native countdown is supported on WhatsApp for iOS and Android. WhatsApp Web and desktop may display an unsupported-message notice instead of the Limited-Time Offer content. Ask the recipient to open the message in the WhatsApp mobile app.

Q: Can I add a footer to my LTO template?

No. LTO templates do not support a footer component. Including one will cause Meta to reject the template. Remove any footer content before submitting.

Q: What happens when the countdown reaches zero?

The countdown stops, and WhatsApp shows the offer as expired. The message remains visible in the customer's conversation.

Q: Can I use an LTO template as a Utility or Authentication template?

No. LTO templates are only available under the Marketing category. You cannot submit them under any other category.

Q: Will my LTO template be approved faster because of Meta's existing LTO format support?

No. All templates go through the same Meta review process regardless of type. Following the category guidelines and providing accurate sample values gives your template the best chance of approval.

Q: Do LTO messages count against my messaging limits?

Yes. When Limited-Time Offer templates are sent outside the 24-hour customer service window, they count toward Meta messaging limits.

Q: Why can't I remove the Copy code or URL button?

Both buttons are mandatory for Limited-Time Offer templates under Meta's requirements and cannot be removed.

Q: What happens if I send an already-expired offer?

The message may still be accepted, but WhatsApp displays the offer as expired. Check the deadline before sending a campaign.
