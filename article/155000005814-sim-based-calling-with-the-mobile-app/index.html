SIM-based calling with the Mobile App

Mobile App · Calling

SIM-Based Calling with the Mobile App

Place calls from the MxP mobile app using your phone's SIM and native carrier calling service when MxP Phone is not active for the sub-account.
Overview

SIM-based calling lets you start a call from the MxP mobile app using the mobile number and carrier plan already configured on your phone. MxP passes the contact's phone number to your device's native dialer, where you place the call using your SIM.

Important

SIM-based calling is available only when an MxP Phone number is not active for the sub-account. Once MxP Phone is set up, the mobile app uses the MxP Phone System instead of SIM-based calling.

1. What Is SIM-Based Calling?

SIM-based calling is a quick way to start a phone call from the MxP mobile app using your device's normal carrier calling service.

When you tap the call icon next to a contact's number, MxP passes the number to your phone's native dialer. You then place the call using the SIM or mobile line configured on your device.

2. Key Benefits

SIM-based calling provides a simple way to contact customers directly from the mobile app without manually copying their phone number.

Quick Calling

Tap the call icon and open your phone's native dialer with the contact's number already filled in.

Uses Your Existing Mobile Plan

Calls use your mobile carrier and SIM instead of the MxP Phone System.

Mobile-Friendly Workflow

Call contacts directly while working from Contacts, Opportunities, or Conversations in the mobile app.

3. SIM-Based vs MxP Calling

SIM-based calls and MxP Phone System calls use different calling methods and provide different features.

4. Prerequisites and Limitations

Before using SIM-based calling, review the following requirements and limitations.

  • No active MxP Phone number: SIM-based calling is available only when an MxP Phone number has not been purchased and activated for the sub-account. Once MxP Phone is active, calls use the MxP Phone System instead.
  • Carrier charges may apply: Calls use your existing mobile plan. Standard minutes, roaming charges, international calling charges, or other carrier fees may apply.
  • MxP call features do not apply to the SIM call: Because the call is handled through your device's carrier service, MxP call recording, transfer controls, and automatic call analytics are not available for that call.
  • Manual logging is required for MxP tracking: SIM-based calls are not automatically logged in MxP. If you want the call outcome recorded in the contact record, log it manually after the call.
  • Local Presence Dialing does not apply: SIM-based calls use the phone number associated with your mobile carrier line instead of a MxP phone number.

5. Where You Can Place SIM-Based Calls

When SIM-based calling is available, you can start a call from common areas of the MxP mobile app where a contact's phone number appears.

  • Contacts: From the contact profile.
  • Opportunities: From an opportunity card or opportunity details.
  • Conversations: From the call action available in the conversation.

6. How to Place a SIM-Based Call

You do not need to copy and paste the contact's phone number. Start the call directly from the mobile app.

Step 1

Open Contacts, Opportunities, or Conversations and locate the contact you want to call.

Step 2

Tap the phone icon next to the contact's phone number.

Step 3

Your device's native dialer opens with the contact's phone number pre-filled. Use the native dialer to place the call.

Result: The call is placed through your mobile carrier just like a standard phone call made from your device.

7. Manual Call Logging

SIM-based calls are handled outside the MxP Phone System, so they are not automatically logged as MxP calls.

After completing a SIM-based call, record the outcome when needed so the contact record and sales process remain up to date.

  • Add a Note to the contact record with the call outcome.
  • Use an applicable manual or external call logging option when your workflow requires structured call tracking.

For automated or workflow-based call tracking, see the related Log External Call and Manual Call documentation.

8. Troubleshooting

If SIM-based calling does not work as expected, check the device and sub-account calling configuration first.

The native dialer does not open
Confirm that your device has an active calling app and an active mobile line. Then reopen the MxP mobile app and try the call again.
The SIM-based calling option is missing
Check whether an MxP Phone number is already active for the sub-account. SIM-based calling is only available when MxP Phone is not active. If no MxP Phone number is active and the option is still missing, contact MxP Support.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Does SIM-based calling cost anything extra in MxP?
SIM-based calls are not billed through the MxP Phone System. They use your mobile carrier plan, so standard minutes, roaming, international calling, or other applicable carrier charges may apply.
What caller ID will the contact see?
Because the call is placed through your SIM and mobile carrier, the recipient generally sees the caller ID provided by the carrier for that line. Caller ID presentation can vary by carrier, device, and region.
Will MxP automatically log my SIM-based calls?
No. SIM-based calls use the device's carrier network and are not automatically logged as MxP Phone System calls. Record the outcome manually when tracking is required.
Can I use MxP call recording or call transfer during a SIM-based call?
No. The call uses your device's carrier network, so MxP call recording and transfer controls are not available for that SIM-based call.
Does Local Presence Dialing work with SIM-based calls?
No. Local Presence Dialing uses MxP phone numbers, while SIM-based calls use the phone number associated with your mobile carrier line.
Why did the SIM-based calling option disappear after I set up MxP Phone?
SIM-based calling is available only when an MxP Phone number is not active for the sub-account. Once MxP Phone is set up, the mobile app uses the MxP Phone System for calling instead.
