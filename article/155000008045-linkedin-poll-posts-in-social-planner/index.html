LinkedIn Poll Posts in Social Planner

LinkedIn Poll Posts in MxP's Social Planner lets you create, schedule, and publish interactive polls directly to your connected LinkedIn account. This article explains how to configure poll questions, answer options, and duration, along with supported publishing workflows and important limitations.

What are LinkedIn Poll Posts?


LinkedIn Poll Posts allow you to create interactive polls directly from Social Planner without switching to LinkedIn. You can configure poll questions, provide answer options, preview the poll, and publish or schedule it using supported Social Planner workflows.

Supported Poll Settings and Publishing Options


You can configure the following settings when creating LinkedIn polls.


Setting
Supported Options
Poll question
Up to 140 characters
Answer options
2–4 options, up to 30 characters each
Poll duration
1, 3, 7, or 14 days
Preview
Preview before publishing
Publishing methods
Instant publishing, scheduled posts, recurring posts, Category Queue, CSV Bulk Upload, and drafts

How to Create a LinkedIn Poll


Follow these steps to create and publish a LinkedIn poll from Social Planner.


  1. Navigate to Marketing → Social Planner.

  2. Create a new post and select your connected LinkedIn account.



  3. Click Add Poll in the post composer.



  4. Enter your poll question, add 2–4 answer options, and select a poll duration of 1, 3, 7, or 14 days.




  5. Preview your poll, then publish immediately, schedule it, or add it to a queue.

Important: LinkedIn polls cannot be combined with images, videos, PDFs, or other media attachments. When creating posts for multiple platforms, you can still add media to non-LinkedIn platform versions. If media is attached to the LinkedIn version, the poll will not be published.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: How many answer options can I add to a LinkedIn poll?

You can add 2–4 answer options, with a maximum of 30 characters per option.

Q: Can I include images or videos in LinkedIn polls?

No. LinkedIn polls cannot include media attachments. However, media can be added to other platform versions when creating a multi-platform post.

Q: Can I schedule LinkedIn polls or use them in recurring posts?

Yes. LinkedIn polls support scheduled posts, recurring posts, Category Queue, CSV Bulk Upload, and drafts.
