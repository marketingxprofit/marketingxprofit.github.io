Rich Link & Event Previews in Communities

Share content and events with automatic rich previews that keep members engaged without leaving the conversation
What You'll Learn

Rich link and event previews transform how members interact with shared content in MxP Communities. Instead of clicking away to view videos or event details, members can watch videos, see event information, and register for events directly within posts and comments.

This article explains how rich previews work, which content types are supported, and how to use event preview cards to boost engagement and streamline community interactions.

What Are Rich Link & Event Previews?

Rich link and event previews are automatically generated visual cards that appear when you share supported links or mention events in community posts and comments. These previews display relevant information directly in the feed, allowing members to interact with content without navigating away from the conversation.

For links, this means embedded video players and contextual information about the shared content. For events, members see event details and registration options right in the post. Both preview types work seamlessly in posts and comments throughout your MxP Community.

2

Key Benefits

Rich previews create a more engaging and seamless experience for community members by reducing friction and keeping conversations flowing.

Inline Video Playback — Watch YouTube, Loom, and other supported videos directly in the feed without opening new tabs or windows.
One-Click Event Registration — Register for events directly from posts without navigating to the Events page.
Contextual Information — See event details, video thumbnails, and link descriptions before deciding to engage.
Enhanced Engagement — Create richer, more interactive discussions that keep members actively participating in the community.
Reduced Navigation — Keep members in the conversation flow by minimizing the need to click away from posts.
3

Rich Link & Video Previews

When you paste a supported link into a post or comment, MxP automatically generates a rich preview card that displays relevant information about the shared content. For video platforms like YouTube and Loom, the preview includes an embedded player that allows members to watch videos without leaving the feed.

Feature 1
Automatic Preview Generation

Simply paste a supported link into any post or comment. The rich preview generates automatically without requiring any additional setup or formatting.

Feature 2
Inline Video Playback

Supported videos from platforms like YouTube and Loom can be played directly within the feed. Members click the play button on the preview card to watch without leaving the conversation.

Feature 3
Works in Posts and Comments

Rich link previews function consistently across both community posts and comments, ensuring a uniform experience regardless of where content is shared.

Feature 4
Contextual Link Information

Preview cards display titles, descriptions, and thumbnails from the linked content, giving members helpful context before they decide to click through or watch.

Supported Platforms

Rich link previews currently support YouTube and Loom video platforms. When you paste links from these platforms, an embedded video player appears in the preview card.

4

Interactive Event Previews

Event preview cards make it easy to promote and share community events directly within posts. When you mention an event in a post, an interactive preview card displays the event's key details and allows members to register without leaving the conversation.

Feature 1
Automatic Event Card Generation

Mention an event in your post and an event preview card is automatically generated, displaying the event's essential information in a clean, organized format.

Feature 2
Event Details at a Glance

The preview card displays important event information including the event name, scheduled date and time, and current registration status. Members can quickly assess whether the event fits their schedule.

Feature 3
Direct Registration from Posts

Members can register for events directly from the preview card in the post. This eliminates the need to navigate to the Events page just to sign up.

 

Feature 4
Events in Conversations

Event previews make events a natural part of community discussions. You can reference upcoming events in posts about related topics, making it easier for members to discover relevant events organically.

Simplified Event Promotion

Event preview cards reduce the friction between discovering an event and registering for it. By embedding registration directly in posts, you can increase event attendance and engagement within your community.

How to Use Rich Previews in Your Community

Rich link and event previews require no special setup or configuration. Follow these simple steps to start using them in your community posts and comments.

Step 1
Create a Post or Comment

Navigate to your MxP Community and create a new post or add a comment to an existing post.

Step 2
Paste a Supported Link or Mention an Event

For link previews: Paste a URL from a supported platform like YouTube or Loom directly into your post or comment.

Step 3
Preview Generates Automatically

MxP automatically generates the rich preview card as you type. You'll see the preview appear below your text, showing the video player, link information, or event details.

Step 4
Publish Your Post or Comment

Publish your post or comment as usual. The rich preview will display in the feed, allowing members to interact with videos or event registration directly from the preview card.

Best Practices

Add context around your shared links and events to give members a reason to watch or register. A brief explanation of what the video covers or why the event matters can significantly increase engagement.

Use event previews when announcing webinars, workshops, or training sessions in discussion posts related to those topics. This helps members discover events naturally as they browse community content.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which video platforms support rich link previews?
Rich link previews currently support YouTube and Loom video platforms. When you paste a link from these platforms, an embedded video player appears in the preview card, allowing members to watch videos directly in the feed.
Q: Do rich previews work in both posts and comments?
Yes, rich link previews and event preview cards work consistently in both community posts and comments. You can share supported links or mention events anywhere in the conversation and the appropriate preview will generate automatically.
Q: Do I need to configure anything to enable rich previews?
No configuration is required. Rich link and event previews are automatically enabled in MxP Communities. Simply paste a supported link or mention an event in your post or comment, and the preview generates automatically.
Q: Can members register for events directly from the preview card?
Yes, event preview cards include a registration button that allows members to register directly from the post. They don't need to navigate to the Events page to sign up, which reduces friction and can increase event attendance.
Q: What information displays in an event preview card?
Event preview cards display the event name, scheduled date and time, current registration status, and a registration button. This gives members all the essential information they need to decide whether to attend without clicking through to the full event page.
Q: Can members watch videos without leaving the community feed?
Yes, supported videos from YouTube and Loom can be played directly within the feed using the embedded video player in the rich preview card. Members click the play button to watch the video without opening a new tab or leaving the conversation.
Q: Will more video platforms be supported in the future?
YouTube and Loom are currently the supported video platforms for rich link previews. MxP continues to expand platform integrations based on user feedback and demand. Check the MxP release notes for announcements about additional platform support.
Q: Can I remove or disable a rich preview after it's generated?
Rich previews are generated automatically when you paste a supported link or mention an event. To remove a preview, you would need to edit your post or comment and remove the link or event mention that triggered the preview generation.
