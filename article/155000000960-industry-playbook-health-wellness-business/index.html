Industry Playbook - Health & Wellness Business



What Is the Health & Wellness Industry Playbook?


The Health & Wellness Industry Playbook provides a recommended MxP setup for businesses that capture leads, book services, manage appointments, collect payments, request reviews, and encourage repeat visits. Use this framework as a starting point and adapt it to your services, staff, and business requirements.


A typical customer journey is:


Capture → Book → Confirm → Deliver → Pay → Review → Rebook

Key Benefits of the Health & Wellness Playbook


A connected MxP setup can reduce manual appointment management while creating a more consistent experience from the first inquiry through repeat visits.


  • Simplified Booking: Let clients select services, providers, and available appointment times.

  • Reduced No-Shows: Automate confirmations, reminders, and missed-appointment follow-up.

  • Flexible Payments: Collect deposits, prepayments, or other applicable service payments.

  • Faster Follow-Up: Respond to new inquiries using automation and optional Conversation AI.

  • Client Retention: Encourage repeat bookings through reminders and reactivation campaigns.

  • Performance Visibility: Monitor bookings, appointment outcomes, and service revenue.

Recommended Setup Order


Building the core booking experience first gives your automations and follow-up campaigns the information they need to work effectively.

  1. Configure your communication channels.

  2. Create your Services and Calendars.

  3. Connect a payment provider if needed.

  4. Set up lead capture and booking forms.

  5. Configure confirmations and reminders.

  6. Build no-show and cancellation follow-up.

  7. Configure review requests.

  8. Build rebooking and reactivation campaigns.

  9. Create reporting dashboards.

Important: If your organization is subject to HIPAA and intends to store or process protected health information in MxP, review HIPAA Compliance With MxP and your organization's compliance requirements before collecting that information.

Capture and Book Health & Wellness Leads


Give prospective clients simple ways to ask questions and move toward an appointment. Depending on your business, this can include forms, landing pages, chat, inbound calls, booking pages, and Missed Call Text Back.


For businesses with multiple offerings, Services and Service Calendars can organize:

  • Services and categories.

  • Staff assignments.

  • Appointment duration and availability.

  • Service variations and add-ons.

  • Booking forms.

  • Payment requirements.

Conversation AI can also help answer common questions, identify the service a prospect is looking for, and support appointment booking when configured appropriately.


See How to Create Service Calendars and Appointment Booking in Conversation AI.

Confirm Appointments and Reduce No-Shows


Appointment communication keeps clients informed and gives them a clear path when their plans change. Configure confirmations and reminders around the timing that works for your business.


A simple workflow is:


Booked → Confirmation → Reminder → Appointment


For missed or canceled appointments:


No Show/Cancel → Follow-Up → Reschedule


See Calendars & Services: SMS, Email & In-App Notifications.

Collect Payments


Choose a payment setup that matches how your services are sold. MxP can support different payment experiences depending on the booking and service configuration.


Common examples include:

  • Deposit: Collect part of the price when the appointment is booked.

  • Prepayment: Collect the full service amount during booking.

  • Remaining Balance: Collect an applicable outstanding appointment balance.

  • Package or Program: Use an appropriate checkout or Payment Link.

  • Recurring Service: Use recurring appointment/payment options where appropriate.

See Collecting Payments in Calendars.

Generate Reviews and Repeat Bookings


Post-appointment follow-up can help build your reputation while encouraging clients to return when another service is appropriate. Automate these actions around meaningful points in the client journey rather than relying entirely on manual outreach.


Consider:

  • Sending a review request after an appropriate completed service or positive experience.

  • Sending rebooking reminders.

  • Following up with clients who have not returned within a defined period.

  • Promoting applicable packages, memberships, or recurring services.

  • Using newsletters and seasonal campaigns for ongoing engagement.


A simple lifecycle can be:


Completed Service → Review Request → Rebooking/Retention Follow-Up

See How to Send Review Requests.

Track Health & Wellness Performance


Reporting helps you understand whether leads are becoming appointments and whether clients continue returning. Focus on the metrics that reflect your actual business model.


Useful metrics can include:

  • Leads captured.

  • Appointments booked.

  • Completed appointments.

  • Show/no-show rate.

  • Service revenue.

  • Revenue by service.

  • Repeat bookings.

  • Reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Should I collect a deposit when clients book?

That depends on your business model. Calendar Payments can support applicable deposits or upfront payment configurations.


Q: How can I reduce no-shows?

Use confirmations, appointment reminders, clear rescheduling options, and an automated no-show follow-up workflow.


Q: Can Conversation AI book appointments?

Yes. Conversation AI can support appointment booking and can work with applicable Service Calendar configurations.


Q: Can I schedule recurring appointments?

Yes. MxP supports recurring appointments for applicable use cases. Review the dedicated recurring appointment documentation when configuring repeating services and payments.


Q: When should I request a review?

Choose an appropriate point after the client has experienced the service, such as a completed appointment or another positive milestone.


Q: How can I reactivate inactive clients?

Segment clients who have not booked within a period appropriate to your business, then send relevant rebooking or reactivation communication.


Q: What should I consider before collecting health information?

If your organization is subject to HIPAA or other privacy requirements, review the applicable MxP configuration and your organization's compliance requirements before collecting protected or sensitive information.
