How to track leads from an Affiliate Campaign

Affiliate lead tracking in MxP helps you identify which affiliates bring new contacts to your business. Create a campaign, choose where referral links send visitors, and attribute eligible activity to the correct affiliate. You can review leads in an affiliate profile and optionally configure commission rewards. This guide follows the updated three-stage campaign creation workflow.

What Is Affiliate Campaign Lead Tracking?


Affiliate campaign lead tracking associates eligible referred contacts with an affiliate and campaign. Every assigned affiliate can receive a campaign-specific referral link used for attribution. Lead tracking and commission payments are separate: a tracked lead does not automatically mean that a commission was earned.

Key Benefits of Affiliate Campaign Lead Tracking


Accurate attribution helps you measure partner performance, understand where contacts originate, and manage follow-up without confusing lead activity with payable commissions.

  • Clear Attribution: Identify the affiliate and campaign linked to an eligible lead.

  • Flexible Sources: Select the source that affiliates should promote.

  • Centralized Visibility: Review lead details in each affiliate's profile.

  • Configurable Rewards: Apply eligible lead-based or sale-based commissions separately.

  • Better Follow-Up: Use tracked leads in affiliate reporting and workflows.

Supported Campaign Sources


The selected source determines where a referral link leads and which supported activity can be attributed to an affiliate. The campaign setup offers seven source choices, but the availability of a source does not guarantee that every commission model applies to it.

  • Funnels: Promote a funnel and choose its starting page.
  • Websites: Promote a MxP website.
  • Store: Direct visitors to a store.
  • External Website: Direct visitors to an external site.
  • Forms: Track eligible referred form submissions.
  • Surveys: Track eligible referred survey completions.
  • Calendar: Track eligible referred calendar bookings.

Important: Forms, Surveys, and Calendar are documented lead-generation sources for Pay Per Lead commissions. Leads may also display sources such as Funnels and External Website, as shown in the current affiliate Leads screen. A Source value alone does not establish Pay Per Lead commission eligibility.


Referral Links and Commission Models


Affiliate referral links identify who referred a visitor, while commission rules determine whether an eligible lead or sale earns a reward. Reviewing both settings prevents attribution from being mistaken for payment approval.

  • Referral Links: Campaign URLs may preview <<affiliate_id>>; the actual affiliate link uses that affiliate's unique identifier.

  • Unique Tracking: The setup can display the ?am_id tracking parameter. Share the assigned affiliate's tracked URL, not the ordinary page URL.

  • Pay Per Lead (Optional): Rewards eligible form submissions, survey completions, or calendar bookings when configured. Refer to How to Set Up Pay-Per-Lead Commissions for Affiliates.

  • Pay Per Sale (Optional): Rewards a qualifying purchase rather than simply a submitted lead. Pay Per Lead and Pay Per Sale cannot both be active for the same Forms, Surveys, or Calendar campaign setup.

Note: The percentage-based default commission shown in the supplied screenshot is not a Pay Per Lead control. Choose the commission configuration that is available for your selected source.


How to Set Up Affiliate Campaign Lead Tracking


Choosing the correct source, checking the referral URL, and assigning affiliates are essential for accurate attribution. The current campaign setup follows Campaign Details, Set Commission, and Assign Affiliate.


Step 1: Open the Campaign Page

In your MxP sub-account, go to Marketing > Affiliate Manager > Campaign. This page lists existing campaigns and summarizes campaign activity.

Step 2: Click Add to Create a Campaign

Select the blue + Add button in the upper-right corner to begin.




Step 3: Name the Campaign and Choose Its Source

In Campaign Details, enter a descriptive Campaign Name. Under Choose Where Your Affiliate Links Should Lead, select Funnels, Websites, Store, External Website, Forms, Surveys, or Calendar. For a lead-commission campaign, Forms, Surveys, and Calendar are the documented Pay Per Lead source types.



Step 4: Configure the Source Details

Complete the fields available for the selected source. For Funnels, choose Pick the Funnel You Want to Promote and First Page for Visitors to Land On. For Forms, Surveys, or Calendar, select the specific form, survey, or calendar using the relevant source controls.

Review Affiliate Link Shared with Customers and Unique Tracking. If the source supports products, review the Commission-Eligible Products list. These product settings are not necessary for every lead-only campaign.



Tip: For supported funnel and website campaigns, the first landing page sets the starting destination, while tracking can apply across the selected source.


Step 5: Review the Affiliate Tracking URL

Confirm that the preview URL points to the intended destination and uses the expected affiliate identifier format. Once affiliates are assigned, copy each affiliate's unique URL from Active Referral Links in their profile. To customize the domain used for branded affiliate links, review Settings > Business Profile > Branded Domain.

Step 6: Configure Commission Settings

Click Set Up Reward to open Set Commission. Choose the available Default Commission type and value appropriate to your campaign. Expand Advanced Commission Settings if you need product-specific commissions, variable commissions, commission payment duration, affiliate level-up rules, or multiple tiers.

If you want to pay for qualifying leads, use the Pay Per Lead guide to configure the applicable lead-based option when it is available. Do not interpret the percentage-based default commission shown below as a Pay Per Lead setting. Continue to Assign Affiliate when ready.



Step 7: Assign Affiliates

In Assign Affiliate, use Select Affiliate to search for and choose existing affiliates. Use Select All only if every listed affiliate should join the campaign. To create or import affiliates first, see How to Add Affiliates in the Affiliate Manager.



Step 8: Review Additional Settings and Finish

Review the available settings before completing campaign creation:

  • Send Affiliate Email Notification: Choose whether to send an onboarding email and select its template.
  • Cookie Life: Set the referral tracking duration appropriate to the campaign.
  • Current Payout Terms: Review when eligible commissions become payable.
  • Include Setup Fees in Commission Calculations: Use only when relevant to sale-based commission calculations.

Click Finish and, if the dropdown provides multiple completion options, choose the appropriate action. Publish when the campaign is ready or save a draft if that option is offered and more edits are needed. Confirm that the campaign is active before distributing referral links.


Step 9: Track Leads from the Affiliate Profile

Navigate to Marketing > Affiliate Manager > Affiliates, open the relevant affiliate profile, and select the Leads tab. Search by name or email, or review the visible table columns:

  • Name: The attributed contact, with email displayed below the name where available.
  • Created At: The lead's recorded date and time.
  • Source: The source associated with the lead, such as Funnels or External Website.
  • Campaign: The campaign associated with the lead.

The Add Lead button provides a supported manual assignment flow. Above the Leads tab, Active Referral Links lets you copy or customize each affiliate's campaign link. Use the separate Commissions tab to inspect eligible rewards.

Important Lead Tracking Considerations


Lead attribution depends on valid referral links, supported capture components, and consistent campaign configuration. Review these points when validating a new campaign or investigating missing records.

  • Use Real Affiliate Links: Share and test each affiliate's assigned referral URL instead of the normal destination URL.

  • Avoid Unsupported Embeds: For documented Forms, Surveys, and Calendar lead tracking, add these as native components within a funnel or webpage. Custom code embeds and iframes are not supported for this workflow.

  • Keep Attribution Separate From Commissions: A lead record does not automatically mean a commission has been earned, approved, or paid.

  • Review Source Changes: Changing the source in an existing campaign can regenerate the referral URL and stop new tracking through the former link.

  • Use Workflows When Needed: The Add Leads Under an Affiliate workflow action provides additional lead-assignment methods.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Why is a lead missing from an affiliate's Leads tab?

Confirm that the visitor used the assigned affiliate URL, the campaign was active, and an eligible action occurred on the selected source. Check that you opened the correct affiliate and campaign and used a supported capture method.

Q: Does a referral-link click automatically create a lead?

No. A click and an attributed lead are different activities. The supported lead capture or attribution process must create the lead record.

Q: Can I manually add a lead to an affiliate?

Yes. Open the affiliate's Leads tab, select Add Lead, and follow the available assignment process.

Q: Can Funnels or External Website appear in the Source column?

Yes. The current affiliate Leads screen includes both source values. This does not necessarily mean the same sources support every commission model.

Q: Can Pay Per Lead and Pay Per Sale run together on the same Forms, Surveys, or Calendar campaign?

No. MxP documents them as mutually exclusive for that setup. Choose the model that matches the outcome you want to reward.

Q: What if I change the campaign source after sharing links?

The referral URL may be regenerated, and old links can stop tracking new activity. Review the new link and share it with participating affiliates.

Q: Can I trigger follow-up actions when a new affiliate lead appears?

Yes. The Lead Created workflow trigger can start supported actions for affiliate-attributed leads, such as internal notifications.
