Create and Send Invoices in MxP
MxP invoices give you a centralized way to bill customers for products and services directly from a sub-account. You can add customers and products, configure invoice details and payment options, save your work, review the accurate customer-facing preview, and send the completed invoice by email or text. This guide explains how to create a one-time invoice and use the current invoice editor from start to finish.
What You'll Learn

Learn how to create a one-time invoice, add a customer and products, configure payment schedules and additional options, understand the invoice editor preview, review the accurate final preview, and send the invoice to your customer.

Important

The right-side preview inside the invoice editor is not a WYSIWYG customer preview and can contain placeholder sample data. Save the invoice, open the three dots (⋮) next to Save, and select Preview when you need to review the accurate invoice layout before sending.

What Are Invoices in MxP?

Invoices let you bill customers for products or services directly from MxP while keeping customer information, products, pricing, payment options, invoice details, and delivery in one workflow.

A one-time invoice is intended for a single billing event. After adding the customer and invoice items, you can apply eligible discounts, taxes, payment schedules, fees, tips, terms, or attachments before saving and sending the invoice.

For billing that needs to repeat on a schedule, use a recurring invoice instead of repeatedly creating one-time invoices.

2

Key Benefits of Invoices

The invoice editor brings invoice configuration, pricing controls, payment options, and document review into a single billing workflow so you can prepare an invoice before delivering it to the customer.

  • Centralized Billing: Manage customer details, products, pricing, dates, and supported payment options from one invoice.
  • Flexible Product Entry: Select existing products or create a product while building the invoice.
  • Payment Flexibility: Configure supported discounts, taxes, payment schedules, late fees, processing fees, tips, terms, and attachments.
  • Save Before Sending: Store invoice changes as a draft before delivering the invoice to the customer.
  • Accurate Final Preview: Use the dedicated Preview action to review the complete invoice before sending it.
3

Prerequisites and Limitations

Reviewing your invoice type and payment configuration before you begin helps you choose the correct billing workflow and understand which payment features will be available.

  • Use a one-time invoice for a single billing event.
  • Use New Recurring Invoice when invoices need to be generated repeatedly on a defined schedule.
  • Configure invoice defaults when you plan to use features such as partial payments, late fees, tips, reminders, or other invoice settings.
  • Electronic payment availability depends on the payment provider and payment methods configured for the sub-account.
  • One-time invoices can also be used in documented manual-payment workflows, but the payment must be collected and recorded manually when no supported online payment provider is being used.
  • Some invoice payment features depend on the connected provider and account configuration.

How To Create and Send an Invoice

Creating a one-time invoice starts from the Payments area. Build the invoice by selecting a client, reviewing dates, adding products, configuring any required payment options, saving the draft, previewing the completed document, and then sending it.

Step 1: Start a New Invoice

Start from the All Invoices view so you can choose between a one-time invoice, recurring invoice, or other available invoice actions.

  1. From the Sub-Account view, select Payments.
  2. Open Invoices & Estimates.
  3. Select All Invoices.
  4. Click + New.
  5. Select New Invoice.

Step 2: Add the Client and Invoice Details

Customer, business, and scheduling information identifies who receives the invoice and controls the primary dates displayed on the document.

  1. Select the customer who should receive the invoice.
  2. Review or edit the Invoice Number.
  3. Review the Issue Date.
  4. Review the Due Date.
  5. Edit the business information when needed.
  6. Save any business-information changes before continuing.

Step 3: Add Products or Services

Invoice items define what the customer is being billed for. You can select an existing product or create a product while building the invoice.

  1. In the Add Products area, click Add Product or the item selector.
  2. Choose an existing product from the list.
  3. If the product does not exist, select + Create Product and enter the required product information.
  4. Add the item to the invoice.
  5. Update the price or quantity on the invoice line when needed.
Select an existing product or use + Create Product when the item is not already available.

Step 4: Configure Invoice Options

Invoice options let you adjust the balance and apply supported billing rules before the invoice is delivered.

Depending on your account and payment configuration, available controls can include:

  • Add Discount: Apply an eligible flat or percentage discount.
  • Add Tax: Apply the applicable configured tax.
  • Add Payment Schedule: Divide the amount into percentage-based or fixed-amount installments with due dates.
  • Additional Options: Configure supported settings such as terms, late fees, processing fees, tipping, or attachments.

Step 5: Save and Preview the Invoice

Saving stores the current invoice configuration. The dedicated Preview action should be used when you want to review the accurate customer-facing layout.

  1. Review the invoice details and click Save.
  2. Click the three dots (⋮) next to Save.
  3. Select Preview.
  4. Review the accurate invoice in the browser tab that opens.
  5. Return to the original invoice editor tab when you are ready to make changes or send the invoice.

Step 6: Send the Invoice

Sending delivers the completed invoice through the delivery methods available for the selected customer and invoice configuration.

  1. Return to the invoice editor after reviewing the final preview.
  2. Click Send.
  3. Review the invoice name.
  4. Choose the available delivery option, such as Email & Text, Email, or Text.
  5. Review the recipient information and message or email template.
  6. Review the applicable Additional Options, including Test or Live mode when shown.
  7. Complete the send action.

Invoice Options and Payment Features

Invoice options provide additional control over how much the customer pays, when payments are due, and what supporting information accompanies the invoice. Feature availability can depend on invoice settings and the connected payment provider.

OptionWhat It Does
DiscountReduces the invoice by the configured flat amount or percentage.
TaxApplies the applicable configured tax to eligible invoice items.
Payment ScheduleSplits the invoice into percentage-based or fixed-amount installments with defined due dates.
Terms & ConditionsAdds invoice-specific terms or notes for the customer.
Late FeesApplies the configured late-fee behavior when the feature is enabled and supported.
Processing FeesAdds supported processing-fee behavior according to the payment configuration.
TippingAllows eligible customers to add a tip when paying the invoice.
AttachmentsAdds supporting files to the invoice email sent to the customer.

Configuration matters: Features such as partial payments, late fees, and tipping may need to be enabled in the invoice payment settings before they can be used on an individual invoice.

Understand the Invoice Editor and Preview

The editor keeps invoice controls and a visual layout reference side by side, but the right-side editor preview and the dedicated Preview action have different purposes.

ViewPurpose
Invoice EditorManage customer details, dates, products, prices, quantities, taxes, discounts, schedules, and other invoice options.
Right-Side PreviewProvides a structural layout reference while editing. It can contain placeholder sample data and is not the exact final invoice.
Three Dots → PreviewOpens the accurate invoice preview in a new browser tab so you can review the final formatting before sending.

Do not rely on the editor preview as the final customer view. Save the invoice and use the dedicated Preview action when invoice formatting must be verified before sending.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the right-side invoice preview exactly what the customer will see?
No. The right-side editor preview can contain placeholder sample data and is intended to show the general document structure. Save the invoice and use three dots (⋮) → Preview to review the accurate invoice layout.
Q: Can I create a product while building the invoice?
Yes. Open the product selector in the Add Products area and use + Create Product when the item does not already exist.
Q: Does clicking Save send the invoice?
No. Save stores the invoice changes. Sending the invoice is a separate action performed with the Send control.
Q: Can I use invoices when payments are collected manually?
Yes. MxP documents a manual-payment workflow for one-time invoices. When electronic payment processing is not being used, the payment must be collected and recorded manually.
Q: Can an invoice use a payment schedule?
Yes. Payment schedules can split an invoice into percentage-based or fixed-amount installments with their own due dates.
Q: How do I create a recurring invoice?
Go to Payments → Invoices & Estimates → + New → New Recurring Invoice, then configure the customer, products, frequency, and applicable start or end settings.
Q: Why might a payment feature be missing from my invoice?
Availability can depend on your invoice settings, connected payment provider, and the specific payment feature. Review the invoice payment settings and provider configuration before troubleshooting the invoice itself.
