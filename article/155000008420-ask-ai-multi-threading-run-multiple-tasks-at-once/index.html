Ask AI Multi-Threading & Background Sessions

Run up to 10 AI conversations in parallel, keep work running in the background, and get notified when sessions complete or need your input — all while switching freely between accounts.

You'll discover how to run parallel tasks, monitor progress through visual indicators, respond to input requests, and seamlessly switch between accounts while maintaining complete conversation separation.

What is Ask AI Multi-Threading?

Ask AI multi-threading transforms how you interact with MxP's AI assistant by enabling parallel conversation processing. Instead of waiting for one task to complete before starting another, you can initiate up to 10 simultaneous conversations that run independently.

Each conversation maintains its own context, scroll position, and history. You can draft a blog post in one conversation, generate a report in another, and schedule appointments in a third — all running concurrently. When you close the Ask AI panel or switch to different tools, your conversations continue processing in the background.

The system automatically saves completed conversations and notifies you when they finish or require your input, ensuring no work is lost and nothing fails silently.

2

Key Capabilities

Multi-threading delivers several powerful capabilities that streamline your AI-assisted workflow:

Parallel Conversation Execution — Run up to 10 conversations simultaneously without waiting for sequential completion. Each conversation processes independently with its own context and state.
Background Processing — Close the Ask AI panel, navigate to other tools, or switch accounts while conversations continue running. Completed work is automatically saved and ready when you return.
Completion Notifications — Receive toast notifications when background conversations finish, displaying the conversation title and a clickable "Open to view" action that takes you directly to the completed chat.
Error Transparency — Failed sessions trigger clear "Background session failed" notifications instead of failing silently, ensuring you're always aware of processing issues.
Persistent State Management — Each conversation retains its scroll position, history, and context even when running in the background or after account switches.
3

Understanding Background Notifications

Ask AI's background notifications are conversation-specific alerts that differ from standard platform notifications in several important ways:

Feature 1
Direct Conversation Navigation

Notifications are clickable and navigate directly to the specific conversation that triggered them. A notification reading "Done • Create launch blog post" takes you straight to that completed conversation when clicked.

Feature 2
Account-Specific Targeting

Notifications are tied to the account where the work was initiated. A conversation running in one sub-account will not trigger notifications while you're viewing a different sub-account.

Feature 3
Persistent Replay

If a conversation completes while you're away from Ask AI, the notification waits and replays when you return instead of disappearing. This ensures you never miss completed work.

Feature 4
Context-Aware Display

Notifications only appear when you're working outside Ask AI. If you're already viewing the Ask AI panel, notifications are suppressed to avoid redundant alerts.

How It Works

When a background conversation completes successfully, you'll see a toast notification like "Done • Create launch blog post" with an "Open to view" link. If a session encounters an error, you'll receive a "Background session failed" message instead, ensuring complete transparency about conversation status.

4

How Input-Needed Notifications Work

Sometimes Ask AI reaches a point in a conversation where it requires additional information or a decision from you before it can proceed. Rather than stalling silently, the system surfaces an "Input needed" notification.

For example, if Ask AI is updating pipeline stages and needs clarification on stage names or settings, you'll receive a notification like "Update pipeline stages needs your response. Open it." Clicking the notification takes you directly to the waiting conversation where you can provide the required input.

Priority Handling

Input-needed notifications take priority over standard completion notifications. This ensures conversations requiring your attention are always surfaced before those that completed successfully on their own.

Cross-Account Alerts

Input-needed notifications work even when you're viewing a different sub-account than where the conversation originated. This ensures you're always alerted when Ask AI needs your input, regardless of which account you're currently working in.

Once you provide the requested information, Ask AI resumes processing the conversation automatically, continuing from exactly where it paused.

5

Live Status Indicators

MxP provides visual status indicators throughout the interface so you can monitor Ask AI activity without keeping the panel open:

Indicator 1
Top Navigation Bar Pulse

The Ask AI button in the top navigation bar pulses gently while at least one conversation is actively processing. This provides immediate visual feedback that work is in progress.

Indicator 2
Red Completion Dot

When background conversations finish while you're working elsewhere in MxP, a red dot appears on the Ask AI button in the top navigation, signaling there's completed work ready to review.

Indicator 3
Sidebar Blue Icon

The Ask AI icon in the sidebar turns blue while conversations are actively running. This provides a secondary visual cue visible regardless of which navigation you're using.

Indicator 4
Sidebar Blue Dot

A small blue dot appears next to the sidebar Ask AI icon when background sessions complete and you haven't reviewed them yet. The dot disappears once you open Ask AI.



Indicator 5

Mobile Chat History Indicators

Chat History on mobile shows which Ask AI conversations are still processing and which finished while you were viewing another chat.

  • Spinner: The chat is still working on a response.
  • Dot: The response finished while you were elsewhere and is ready to review.
Account-Specific Status

All status indicators are account-specific. The badges and dots you see reflect only the sub-account or agency view you're currently in. Switching accounts instantly updates the indicators to show the correct status for that account.

Note

When you're already viewing the Ask AI screen, status dots are hidden since you're already in context. They reappear when you navigate away to signal when new activity occurs.

6

Account Switching & Conversation Isolation

Ask AI maintains complete conversation isolation across accounts, allowing you to switch freely between sub-accounts and agency views without mixing up work or losing context.

Each account maintains its own independent set of conversations, history, scroll positions, and error states. When you switch accounts, all active conversations in the previous account continue running in the background and finish there.

Separate Conversation Histories — Each account maintains its own conversation list. Switching to a different sub-account shows only that account's conversations.
Persistent Scroll Positions — When you return to an account, each conversation resumes at the exact scroll position where you left it, even if you've been working in other accounts.
Account-Specific Errors — Error messages and failed session notifications are tied to the account where the conversation originated. Errors in one account don't appear when viewing another.
Independent Background Processing — Conversations initiated in Sub-Account A continue running even when you switch to Sub-Account B. Completion notifications appear only when you return to the originating account.
Result

You can confidently manage multiple client accounts simultaneously, knowing that conversations and their results will never bleed across account boundaries. Each account operates as a completely independent workspace.

7

Voice Mode Enhancements

Voice mode receives several improvements that make the experience more consistent and flexible when mixing typed and spoken input.

Enhancement 1
Typed Messages Read Aloud

When you type a message in voice mode instead of speaking it, Ask AI now reads its reply out loud. This maintains audio consistency throughout the conversation regardless of input method.

Enhancement 2
Graceful Response Completion

If you switch to another conversation while Ask AI is speaking a voice response, the system waits for the response to finish naturally instead of cutting it off mid-sentence.

Enhancement 3
Session Exit Confirmation

Ask AI now confirms before ending a live voice session, preventing accidental disconnections and giving you a chance to continue the conversation if needed.

Text Mode Behavior

Plain text conversations outside voice mode remain silent as before. Audio responses only occur when voice mode is active, ensuring quiet operation when you're working in text-only mode.

How to Use Multi-Threading

Follow these steps to take advantage of Ask AI's multi-threading capabilities:

Step 1
Open Ask AI


Click the Ask AI button in your top navigation bar or sidebar to open the Ask AI panel.


Step 2
Start Your First Conversation


Type or speak your first request — for example, "Draft a blog post about email marketing best practices." Ask AI begins processing immediately.


Step 3
Create Additional Conversations


Start new conversations by clicking the "New conversation" button or using the keyboard shortcut. Each conversation runs independently. You can create up to 10 parallel conversations.


Step 4
Continue Your Workflow


Close the Ask AI panel, navigate to other MxP tools, or switch to different accounts. Your conversations continue processing in the background.



Step 5
Monitor Status Indicators


Watch for the pulsing Ask AI button (active processing) and red dot (completed work) in your navigation bar. The sidebar icon turns blue during active processing and shows a blue dot when conversations finish.


Step 6
Respond to Notifications


When you see a "Done" or "Input needed" notification, click "Open to view" to jump directly to the relevant conversation. For input-needed notifications, provide the requested information to allow Ask AI to continue.


Step 7
Review Completed Work


Open Ask AI to review finished conversations. Each conversation preserves its full history and scroll position, so you can immediately see results without scrolling or searching.


Parallel Sessions in Ask AI Mobile


Parallel sessions let you start another Ask AI request without waiting for the current response to finish. Each chat runs independently, so switching conversations does not cancel work that is already processing.


The mobile parallel-session experience is available in version 4.23.0 and above for the MxP, MxP, and WL App.


Run Multiple Ask AI Chats on Mobile


Starting another conversation lets you keep asking questions while earlier requests continue processing.

  1. Open the mobile app.
  2. Open Ask AI from the App Drawer.
  3. Send your first question.
  4. Open Chat History.
  5. Tap New Chat.
  6. Send another question. The first chat continues processing independently.
  7. Open Chat History whenever you want to move between conversations.
  8. Look for a spinner to identify chats still processing and a dot to identify responses that finished while you were elsewhere.
Best Practice

Use descriptive conversation titles or opening messages to make it easy to identify conversations later. When you receive a notification like "Done • Create launch blog post," you'll immediately know which task completed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many conversations can I run simultaneously?
Ask AI supports up to 10 parallel conversations at once. Each conversation runs independently with its own context, history, and state. You can freely switch between conversations, close the Ask AI panel, or navigate elsewhere while all active conversations continue processing.
Q: What happens to my conversations if I close the Ask AI panel?
All active conversations continue running in the background when you close the panel. Completed work is automatically saved and waiting for you when you return. You'll receive toast notifications when conversations finish, and status indicators in your navigation bar and sidebar show ongoing activity and completed work.
Q: How do I know when a conversation needs my input?
When Ask AI requires additional information to proceed, you'll receive an "Input needed" notification with the conversation title and a prompt to open it. These notifications take priority over completion notifications and work even when you're viewing a different sub-account, ensuring you're always alerted when Ask AI needs your input. Click the notification to jump directly to the conversation and provide the requested information.
Q: Do conversations mix up when I switch between sub-accounts?
No. Conversations are completely isolated per account. Each sub-account and the agency view maintain separate conversation histories, scroll positions, and background sessions. Work started in one account continues running there and finishes there, with notifications appearing only when you're viewing the originating account.
Q: What do the different status indicators mean?
The pulsing Ask AI button in the top navigation indicates active processing. A red dot on the top navigation button signals completed background work awaiting review. The sidebar Ask AI icon turns blue during active processing and displays a blue dot when sessions finish in the background. All indicators are account-specific and update instantly when you switch accounts.
Q: How does voice mode work with typed messages?
When voice mode is active and you type a message instead of speaking it, Ask AI reads its reply out loud to maintain audio consistency. Voice mode also waits for responses to finish before switching conversations and confirms before ending live voice sessions. Text-only conversations outside voice mode remain silent.
Q: What happens if a background conversation fails?
You'll receive a clear "Background session failed" notification instead of a standard completion notification. This ensures errors never fail silently. Click the notification to view the conversation and see error details, then retry the request or modify your approach as needed.
Q: Will I receive notifications if I'm already viewing Ask AI?
No. Notifications are suppressed when you're actively viewing the Ask AI panel to avoid redundant alerts. Status indicators also hide while you're on the Ask AI screen since you're already in context. Notifications and indicators reappear when you navigate away to signal new activity.
