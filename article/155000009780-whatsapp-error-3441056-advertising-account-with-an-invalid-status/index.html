WhatsApp Error #3441056 — Advertising Account With an Invalid Status

WhatsApp Error #3441056 — Advertising Account With an Invalid Status

Why Meta blocks the WhatsApp connection when a business has an advertising account in a bad state, and how to resolve it in Business Support Home.

Error #3441056: The business you selected has one or more advertising accounts with an invalid status. Go to Business Support Home to resolve any issues with your advertising accounts.

Table of Contents

What This Error Means How to Resolve It Related Meta Errors Troubleshooting Frequently Asked Questions

What This Error Means

This error comes from Meta, not from the CRM. It appears in Meta's sign-up window when you connect a WhatsApp number and select a business portfolio that has one or more advertising accounts in a restricted or invalid state.

Meta requires the advertising accounts in the selected business to be in good standing before it lets the WhatsApp connection continue. Until the issue is resolved, Meta stops the connection and the number cannot be added.

It is about advertising accounts, not the WhatsApp number

The problem is the status of an ad account in the business portfolio. It does not by itself mean your WhatsApp number or WhatsApp Business Account has been restricted.

It can be caused by an ad account you no longer use

The message refers to any advertising account in the selected business, so an old or inactive ad account that Meta has restricted can trigger it, even if you do not run ads.

This can only be fixed in Meta. The status of an ad account is controlled by Meta. Neither you nor the CRM can change it from the CRM. You need to resolve it in Meta's Business Support Home.

How to Resolve It

1

Sign In With an Admin Account

Sign in to Meta with a Facebook account that has admin access to the business portfolio you selected. Accounts with lower roles may not be able to see or fix the issue.

2

Open Business Support Home

Go to Business Support Home in Meta. You can reach it from Meta Business Settings in the left navigation, or by opening business.facebook.com/business-support-home.

3

Select Your Business

Select the same business portfolio you chose when connecting WhatsApp.

4

Open Each Ad Account With an Issue

In the Ad accounts section, select every ad account that shows a warning. Meta displays the issue and the options available to resolve it.

5

Resolve or Appeal the Issue

Follow the resolution option Meta offers for that ad account, such as requesting a review or submitting an appeal.

If Meta shows no resolution option, for example because the ad account has been permanently disabled, contact Meta directly through the Business Help Center.

6

Retry Connecting WhatsApp

Once Meta shows all ad accounts in good standing, return to the CRM and connect your WhatsApp number again.

Do not create a new business portfolio to get around a restriction. Doing so can lead to further enforcement by Meta. Resolve or appeal the issue on the existing portfolio. Select a different portfolio only if your WhatsApp account legitimately belongs to it.

Troubleshooting

Issue
What to do
I cannot find Business Support Home
Make sure you are signed in with a Facebook account that has admin access to the business portfolio. Ask an existing admin to open it or to add you as an admin.
No issue is shown for my business
Confirm you selected the same business portfolio used in the connection, and open each ad account individually. Also check with other admins of the portfolio.
The ad account is permanently disabled and has no resolution option
Contact Meta directly through the Business Help Center. The CRM cannot reinstate or remove the ad account.
An appeal or review is still pending
Wait for Meta's decision in Business Support Home before retrying. The status must be resolved before the connection can continue.
Business Support Home shows everything resolved, but the error continues
Close the Meta sign-up window, sign out and back in, and start the connection again. If it still fails, contact Support with the details below.
When contacting Support, include: your Location ID, the WhatsApp number you are connecting, the exact error code and message, the name of the business portfolio you selected, a screenshot of the error, and a screenshot of what Business Support Home shows for your ad accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can the CRM fix this error for me?

No. The error is returned by Meta and depends on the status of your advertising accounts. It has to be resolved in Meta's Business Support Home.

Q: Does this mean my WhatsApp number is banned or restricted?

Not necessarily. This error is about the status of an advertising account in the business portfolio. A restriction on the WhatsApp Business Account itself produces a different error, such as 2655089 or 3441040.

Q: I do not run ads. Why am I seeing an advertising error?

The check covers the advertising accounts in the business portfolio you selected, so an old or inactive ad account that Meta has restricted can cause it. Open Business Support Home and look at every ad account listed.

Q: How long does Meta take to resolve an ad account issue?

Meta decides this, and the time depends on the issue and on Meta's review. Check the status in Business Support Home and retry the connection once it shows as resolved.

Q: Can I connect my number using a different business portfolio instead?

Only if your WhatsApp account legitimately belongs to that portfolio. Do not create a new portfolio to bypass a restriction, as this can lead to further enforcement.
