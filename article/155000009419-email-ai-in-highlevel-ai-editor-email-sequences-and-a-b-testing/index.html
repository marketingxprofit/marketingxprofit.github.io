Email AI in MxP: AI Editor, Email Sequences, and A/B Testing

MxP makes email creation smarter, faster, and more effective with three AI-powered capabilities: Email AI Editor, Email Sequence Creation via AI, and AI-Powered A/B Testing. These features help you generate branded emails, build multi-step email journeys, and optimize campaign performance with less manual effort.

What is Email AI in MxP?


Email AI brings AI-powered content generation, editing, sequencing, and optimization into the email marketing experience. By using natural-language prompts, you can create and refine emails, generate connected follow-up messages, and experiment with different content variations to improve customer engagement.


FeatureDescription
Email AI EditorCreate and refine email content and designs using conversational AI prompts.
Email Sequence Creation via AIGenerate a series of connected emails based on a single prompt and communication goal.
AI-Powered A/B TestingGenerate email variations and compare their performance to identify more effective messaging.

Email AI Editor




The Email AI Editor simplifies email creation by allowing you to generate and refine emails through conversational prompts. It uses your instructions and Brand Board information to create emails aligned with your messaging and visual identity, while helping you improve content and design without manually editing every element.


CapabilityDescription
Conversational Email EditingCreate and modify email content using natural-language prompts.
Brand-Aware ContentLeverage Brand Board colors, fonts, and styling to create consistent email designs.
AI Design SuggestionsGenerate and refine layouts, messaging, and visual elements.
Content RefinementAdjust subject lines, headlines, email copy, and calls to action through follow-up prompts.
Faster IterationsMake multiple content and design changes without rebuilding the email from scratch.


For example, you can ask Email AI to create a promotional email, shorten its content, adjust the tone, or highlight a specific offer. The generated email remains editable, allowing you to further customize the final design.

Email Sequence Creation via AI




AI Email Sequences allow you to generate multiple connected emails using a single prompt that describes your communication objective. AI creates context-aware messaging across the sequence, making it easier to develop consistent follow-up journeys for lead nurturing, onboarding, and customer engagement.


CapabilityDescription
AI-Generated Email SequencesCreate multiple related emails from one prompt describing your goal.
Context-Aware MessagingMaintain a consistent theme and logical message progression throughout the sequence.
AI-Assisted Content CreationReduce the need to manually draft individual follow-up emails.
Email RefinementReview and customize generated email content to align with your campaign objectives.
Personalized CommunicationGenerate sequences for different audiences, communication goals, and customer journeys.

AI-generated sequences can support different stages of customer communication, from the first interaction to ongoing engagement.

AI-Powered A/B Testing




AI-Powered A/B Testing helps you create alternative email variations and compare their performance with a sample of your audience. By combining AI-generated content with performance-based testing, you can identify messaging that resonates more effectively with recipients and reduce the effort required to create multiple test versions.


CapabilityDescription
AI-Generated VariationsGenerate multiple subject-line or email-content alternatives.
Subject Line TestingCompare different subject lines to determine which generates stronger engagement.
Email Content TestingCompare different email messages, layouts, or calls to action.
Audience Split TestingDistribute variations across a sample audience to measure performance.
Automatic Winner SelectionSelect the winning variation using the configured engagement metric.


A/B Testing Options


MxP's email campaign A/B testing provides controls for the number of variations, audience allocation, test duration, and winner selection. These settings help determine how variations are compared and which version is delivered to the remaining audience.


SettingAvailable Options
Test TypeEmail Subject or Email Content
Number of VariationsUp to 6
Test Duration30 minutes to 24 hours
Audience DistributionAdjustable test audience percentage
Winning CriteriaUnique Open Rate or Unique Click Rate
Delivery OptionsSend Now or Schedule


AI can generate alternatives with different messaging approaches, such as emphasizing urgency, curiosity, or specific benefits. The variations can then be evaluated using the selected A/B testing criteria.


Important: Standard email campaign A/B testing requires at least 40 contacts overall and 10 contacts per variation. Subject lines and email content are tested separately. If no engagement is recorded for the selected winning metric, MxP selects the first variation as the default winner.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can Email AI modify an email that has already been created?

Yes. Supported Email AI editing experiences allow you to refine existing emails using conversational prompts, including changes to messaging and design.


Q: Will changes to my Brand Board automatically update previously generated emails?

No. Previously generated emails retain their existing styling unless you modify them. Updated Brand Board information can be used when generating new content.


Q: Can AI generate an entire email sequence from a single prompt?

Yes. AI can generate multiple connected email messages based on a single prompt describing your objective, audience, and desired communication journey.


Q: Are AI Email Sequences the same as workflow automations?

No. Email Sequences focus on connected email follow-ups, while workflows support broader automation involving triggers, actions, and multiple communication channels.


Q: Can I test both subject lines and email content in one A/B test?

No. The documented email campaign A/B testing experience supports either subject-line testing or email-content testing within a single test.


Q: Does AI-Powered A/B Testing guarantee higher open or click rates?

No. AI helps generate variations and simplify experimentation, but campaign performance depends on factors such as audience relevance, email content, deliverability, and engagement.
