Communities UI Refresh in GoKollab (Beta)

Communities in GoKollab have been redesigned to deliver a cleaner, more intuitive experience for members and creators. The updated interface introduces improved navigation, larger content areas, easier access to conversations, and new engagement features like emoji reactions. Built on HighRise, MxP's new design system, this refresh lays the foundation for a unified Client Portal experience while making it easier to discover content, switch between groups, and stay connected.

What is the Redesigned Communities Experience?


The redesigned Communities experience in GoKollab introduces a modern interface that makes it easier to navigate communities, interact with members, and access courses. Built using HighRise, MxP's new design system, the update improves usability while creating a more consistent experience across the platform. These enhancements are currently available for GoKollab creators participating in the Beta program.

Key Benefits


The redesigned experience improves navigation, simplifies member interactions, and makes community content easier to discover and engage with.

  • Faster Navigation: Quickly switch between communities without losing your place.

  • Improved Content Visibility: Larger post layouts make discussions easier to read and engage with.

  • Unified Experience: Move seamlessly between Communities and Courses.

  • Enhanced Course Discovery: Smarter course cards display more useful information at a glance.

  • Simplified Member Engagement: Start chats or view member profiles directly from the Members tab.

  • Improved Organization: Cleaner navigation filters help you locate content more efficiently.

  • Better Engagement: React to posts using emoji reactions.

  • Future Ready Platform: Experience the first phase of MxP's unified Client Portal built on HighRise.

Group Switcher


Switching between multiple communities is now faster and more intuitive. The Group Switcher is available across the experience, allowing you to move between communities while maintaining your current location whenever possible.


Benefits

  • Access all of your communities from a single switcher.

  • Reduce unnecessary navigation.

  • Maintain workflow while changing groups.

Home (All Posts)


The Home page now serves as your default All Posts feed, making it easier to discover conversations across your 

community without selecting individual channels.


Benefits

  • View all community activity from one location.

  • Stay updated on recent discussions.

  • Spend less time switching between channels.

Larger Post Layout


Posts now have a larger, cleaner layout that places greater emphasis on community content and discussions.


Benefits

  • Improved readability.

  • Better visual hierarchy.

  • Easier interaction with comments and reactions.

Community and Course Switching


Moving between Communities and Courses is now part of one seamless experience, making it easier to continue learning while staying engaged with your community.


Benefits

  • Navigate naturally between learning content and discussions.

  • Reduce unnecessary clicks.

  • Maintain context while switching experiences.

Smarter Course Tiles


Course cards now display more useful information, helping members quickly identify and access relevant learning content.


Benefits

  • Better visibility into available courses.

  • Easier course selection.

  • Improved browsing experience.

Members Tab Improvements


The Members tab now provides direct shortcuts for viewing member profiles and starting conversations.


Chat with Members: Start a direct conversation with another member directly from the Members tab.


View Member Profiles: Access member profiles with a single click to learn more about community participants.


Simplified Left Navigation Filters: The left navigation has been streamlined with cleaner filters that help you locate content more efficiently.


Benefits

  • Reduced visual clutter.

  • Faster navigation.

  • Easier content discovery.

Emoji Reactions


Emoji reactions allow members to quickly engage with posts without writing a comment.


Benefits

  • Encourage participation.

  • Increase community engagement.

  • Provide lightweight feedback on posts.

How to Access the Redesigned Communities Experience


The redesigned Communities experience is currently available for GoKollab creators participating in the Beta program.

  1. Sign in to your MxP account.

  2. Open GoKollab.

  3. Access a community where the Beta experience is enabled.

  4. Explore the updated navigation, Home feed, Members tab, Courses, and emoji reactions.


Note: No additional configuration is required for eligible Beta users. The updated interface is automatically available once enabled for your account.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Who can access the redesigned Communities experience?
A: The experience is currently available to GoKollab creators participating in the Beta program.


Q: Do I need to enable the new interface?
A: No. Eligible Beta users receive the updated experience automatically.


Q: Can I switch between Communities and Courses?
A: Yes. The redesigned navigation allows you to move seamlessly between community discussions and course content.


Q: What is the default Home page now?
A: Home now displays an All Posts feed by default.


Q: Can I react to posts without leaving a comment?
A: Yes. Emoji reactions allow you to quickly engage with posts.


Q: Can I message members directly?
A: Yes. You can start a direct chat from the Members tab.

Next Steps


Continue exploring the redesigned Communities experience and watch for additional Client Portal updates as more HighRise enhancements become available.
