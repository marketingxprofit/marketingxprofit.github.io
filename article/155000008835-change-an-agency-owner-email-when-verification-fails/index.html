Change an Agency Owner Email When Verification Fails

Agency Owner • Login Email • Ownership Transfer
Change an Agency Owner Email When Verification Fails
MxP normally lets users change their login email using an available verified authentication method, such as email, SMS, or an Authenticator App. If the Agency Owner cannot complete that standard process, a replacement Agency Admin can be created with the preferred email and Agency Ownership can be transferred to that user. This guide explains when to use the fallback workflow, how to prepare the replacement user, and how to complete the ownership transfer safely.
What You'll Learn

Learn when an ownership-transfer fallback is appropriate, how to create an eligible replacement Agency Admin, how to transfer the Agency Owner role, and when to contact MxP Support for account-access assistance.

Important

Do not create a replacement user simply because the current email address cannot receive verification messages. If the existing Agency Owner can verify the login-email change through another available method, such as SMS or an Authenticator App, use the standard email-change process instead.

What Is the Agency Owner Email Change Fallback Process?

The Agency Owner email change fallback process provides an alternate way to establish the correct Agency Owner login when the standard verified email-change workflow cannot be completed. Instead of modifying the existing user profile, you create another Agency-level Admin using the preferred email address and transfer Agency Ownership to that user.

MxP's standard login-email change process can use available verified authentication methods, including email, SMS, or an Authenticator App. If one of those methods lets the current Agency Owner complete the email update successfully, a replacement profile is not necessary.

Use the ownership-transfer workflow only when the standard email change still cannot be completed, account-access issues prevent the normal process, or MxP Support directs you to use the replacement-user method.

2

Key Benefits of the Agency Owner Email Change Fallback Process

Using a replacement Agency Admin provides a controlled alternative when the current Agency Owner profile cannot complete the normal login-email update. The existing Owner remains in control until the replacement account is ready and ownership is intentionally transferred.

  • Preserves Agency Access: Keep the existing Agency Owner active until the ownership transfer is complete.
  • Corrects Login Credentials: Move Agency Ownership to a user profile that contains the correct and accessible email address.
  • Supports Credential Validation: Test the replacement user's credentials before transferring ownership to reduce the risk of another access issue.
  • Maintains Controlled Ownership Transfer: Transfer ownership to an eligible Agency Admin through the documented Agency Owner controls.
  • Provides a Support Path: Escalate login or verification problems to MxP Support when the self-service process cannot be completed.
3

When to Use This Process

Choosing the standard email-change workflow whenever possible avoids unnecessary user creation and role changes. The replacement-user method is intended for situations where verified login-email changes remain blocked.

Use this fallback workflow when:

  • The Agency Owner cannot complete the standard verified login-email change using the available authentication methods.
  • The current login email is incorrect or inaccessible and another verification method does not resolve the email-change issue.
  • Account-access or verification problems prevent the normal email update from being completed.
  • MxP Support directs you to create another Agency Admin and transfer Agency Ownership.

Use the standard process first: If the existing Agency Owner can verify the login-email change through email, SMS, an Authenticator App, or another available verified method, update the existing profile instead of creating a replacement user.

Important: The Agency Owner login email belongs to the individual user profile used to sign in. It is different from the company email configured under Agency Company Settings.

4

Before You Begin

Maintaining access to the current Agency Owner is essential because the ownership transfer must be initiated from the current Owner profile. Verify access, the replacement email, and the new user's role before starting the transfer.

  • Confirm that you can access the current Agency Owner account.
  • Confirm that the preferred replacement email address is correct and accessible.
  • Use an available verified authentication method when MxP requests identity verification.
  • Prepare the replacement user as an Agency-level Admin.
  • Contact MxP Support before continuing if you cannot access the current Agency Owner account or complete required verification.

5

Requirements for the Replacement User

Agency Ownership can only be transferred to an eligible Agency Admin. Creating the replacement user with the correct user type and role ensures that the user can be selected during the ownership-transfer process.

Configure the replacement profile with:

  • The Agency Owner's correct name.
  • The correct or preferred login email.
  • A valid phone number when applicable.
  • User Type: Agency
  • Role: Admin

Recommended Credential Check

Before transferring ownership, it is a good practice to sign in to the replacement profile and confirm that its credentials and verification methods work. This reduces the risk of transferring ownership to a user who cannot successfully access the agency.

6

When to Contact MxP Support

Login and verification problems can prevent the current Agency Owner from completing either the standard email update or the ownership-transfer fallback. Escalate the issue before changing roles when secure access to the current Owner profile cannot be maintained.

Contact MxP Support when:

  • You cannot sign in to the current Agency Owner profile.
  • You cannot receive or complete the required verification method.
  • The available authentication methods continue to fail.
  • The Transfer Owner control is unavailable.
  • The expected replacement Agency Admin does not appear as an ownership-transfer option after the user has been configured correctly.

If you can access MxP, use the Support options available from the Help menu. If you cannot log in, use the external contact options documented in  24/7 Customer Support Options .

How To Change an Agency Owner Email When Verification Fails

Create the replacement Agency Admin before changing ownership, verify that the replacement credentials are usable, and then return to the current Agency Owner profile to complete the transfer. Keeping the current Owner active until the final transfer helps preserve continuous agency access.

Step 1: Sign In to the Current Agency Owner Account

Start from the current Agency Owner profile because only the current Owner can complete the final ownership transfer.

  1. Go to the MxP login page.
  2. Enter the current Agency Owner login email.
  3. Enter the current password.
  4. Complete verification using an available verified authentication method.
  5. Open Agency View.

Cannot access the current Owner? Contact MxP Support before continuing. Do not attempt to work around an inaccessible Agency Owner account by changing other users or permissions.

Step 2: Create the Replacement Agency Admin

The ownership target must be an Agency-level Admin. Configure the replacement user with the preferred login email before starting the ownership transfer.

  1. From Agency View, go to Settings → Team.
  2. Click + Add User.
  3. Enter the Agency Owner's name.
  4. Enter the correct or preferred login email.
  5. Complete the other required user information.
  6. Under the user's roles and permissions, set User Type to Agency.
  7. Set the user's Role to Admin.
  8. Save the new user.


Step 3: Validate the Replacement User

Testing the replacement credentials before transferring ownership helps confirm that the preferred email and authentication setup work correctly.

  1. Log out of the current Agency Owner profile.
  2. Sign in using the newly created Agency Admin's email and credentials.
  3. Complete any required authentication or verification.
  4. Confirm that the replacement user can successfully access MxP.
  5. Log out of the replacement profile.

Recommended: Testing the replacement login is a precaution that confirms the new credentials work before Agency Ownership is moved to that profile.

Step 4: Transfer Agency Ownership

Return to the current Agency Owner profile to transfer the Agency Owner role to the replacement Agency Admin. An agency can have only one Agency Owner at a time.

  1. Sign back in using the current Agency Owner profile.
  2. Complete the required verification.
  3. Go to Agency View → Settings → Team.
  4. Edit the current Agency Owner.
  5. Open User Roles.
  6. Select Transfer Owner.
  7. Choose the replacement Agency Admin.
  8. Confirm the ownership transfer.

After the transfer: The selected Agency Admin becomes the Agency Owner. The previous Agency Owner automatically becomes an Agency Admin.

Step 5: Verify the New Agency Owner

A final login confirms that the replacement account has the expected Agency Owner access and that the preferred login email is working correctly.

  1. Log out of the previous Agency Owner profile.
  2. Sign in using the new Agency Owner email and credentials.
  3. Open Agency View.
  4. Go to Settings → Team.
  5. Confirm that the replacement user is identified as the Agency Owner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Should I always create another user when changing an Agency Owner email?
No. Use the normal login-email change process first. If the Agency Owner can verify the change through an available method such as email, SMS, or an Authenticator App, a replacement user is not necessary.
Q: What if the Agency Owner cannot receive a verification email?
Use another available verified authentication method when possible. If no usable verification method is available or verification continues to fail, contact MxP Support.
Q: Why can't I select the replacement user when transferring ownership?
Confirm that the replacement user is configured with User Type: Agency and Role: Admin. If the correctly configured Agency Admin still does not appear, contact MxP Support.
Q: Do I have to sign in to the replacement user before transferring ownership?
Testing the replacement login is recommended because it confirms that the new credentials work before ownership is transferred. The replacement user must still meet the Agency Admin requirements to be an eligible ownership target.
Q: What happens to the previous Agency Owner after ownership is transferred?
The previous Agency Owner automatically becomes an Agency Admin after the ownership transfer is completed.
Q: Can an agency have more than one Agency Owner?
No. An agency can have only one Agency Owner at a time.
Q: Is changing the Agency Company email the same as changing the Agency Owner login email?
No. The Agency Company email is part of the agency's company information. The Agency Owner login email belongs to the individual user profile used to sign in.
Q: What should I do if I cannot log in to the current Agency Owner account at all?
Contact MxP Support for account-access assistance before attempting the replacement-user or ownership-transfer workflow.
