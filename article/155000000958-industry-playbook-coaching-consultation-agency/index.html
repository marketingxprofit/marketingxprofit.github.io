What Is the Coaching & Consulting Industry Playbook?


The Coaching & Consulting Industry Playbook provides a recommended MxP setup for businesses that generate leads, qualify prospects, book consultations, convert opportunities into clients, and maintain relationships after the sale. It can be used by coaching and consulting businesses directly or by agencies building MxP systems for clients in these industries. The playbook connects lead capture, Conversation AI, calendars, pipelines, payments, onboarding, reputation management, retention, and reporting into one customer journey.


A typical coaching or consulting customer journey can follow:

Capture → Qualify → Book → Track → Convert → Onboard → Deliver → Review → Retain/Reactivate → Measure


Your exact process may differ based on whether you offer free discovery calls, paid consultations, recurring coaching, project-based consulting, courses, or other services. Use this playbook as a starting framework and adapt each stage to your business model.

Key Benefits of the Coaching & Consulting Playbook


A connected MxP setup helps move prospects from their first inquiry through consultation, purchase, onboarding, and ongoing client communication. Building around the complete customer journey also makes it easier to automate repetitive tasks without losing visibility into where each prospect or client stands.

  • Centralized Lead Management: Capture inquiries from multiple sources and manage prospect information in MxP.

  • Faster Lead Follow-Up: Use automated responses, workflows, and optional Conversation AI to engage new inquiries.

  • Structured Qualification: Collect the information needed to determine whether a prospect is a good fit before or during the consultation process.

  • Simplified Consultation Booking: Give qualified prospects a clear path to book free or paid consultations.

  • Visible Sales Pipeline: Track prospects from initial inquiry through consultation and closed business.

  • Automated Client Onboarding: Trigger welcome communication and client-access steps after a successful sale.

  • Ongoing Client Engagement: Use review requests, nurture campaigns, renewals, upsells, and reactivation workflows to continue the relationship.

  • Performance Visibility: Monitor lead, appointment, opportunity, revenue, and other relevant business metrics.

Recommended Setup Order


Building the system in the right sequence helps ensure each stage has the information and configuration required by the next one. Start with the core communication and sales infrastructure before adding advanced automation, AI, or client-delivery features.


A recommended implementation order is:

  1. Configure your primary communication channels.

  2. Create your consultation calendar.

  3. Create your sales pipeline.

  4. Connect a supported payment provider if you will collect payments through MxP.

  5. Build your lead-capture forms, applications, pages, or other entry points.

  6. Configure lead qualification.

  7. Build appointment reminders and no-show follow-up.

  8. Configure the sales and payment process.

  9. Build client onboarding.

  10. Configure review requests.

  11. Create retention and reactivation campaigns.

  12. Build dashboards or reports for the metrics you want to monitor.


Add optional features such as Conversation AI, Client Portal, Courses, or Communities when they support your 

specific coaching or consulting model.

Capture Coaching and Consulting Leads


Lead capture gives prospects a clear way to express interest and provides MxP with the contact information needed for follow-up. Coaching and consulting businesses can use different entry points depending on whether prospects come from websites, advertisements, referrals, phone calls, social channels, or other campaigns.


Consider using:

  • Forms: Capture contact information and basic qualification details.

  • Surveys or Applications: Collect more detailed information when prospects need to qualify before a consultation.

  • Landing Pages: Create campaign-specific experiences for lead magnets, consultations, webinars, or program offers.

  • Chat: Give website visitors a conversational way to ask questions or start an inquiry.

  • Missed Call Text Back: Automatically respond when a prospective client calls but the call is missed.

  • Inbound Calls: Route phone inquiries into your normal lead-management process.

  • Text Snippets: Save frequently used responses for team members handling conversations manually.

Use Missed Call Text Back to Recover Phone Leads


A missed phone call can represent a prospective client who is actively looking for help. Missed Call Text Back can automatically acknowledge the inquiry and give the prospect a way to continue the conversation by SMS.


A simple flow can be:


Missed call → Automatic SMS → Prospect responds → Qualification → Consultation booking


Use the automated message to acknowledge the missed call and guide the prospect toward the appropriate next action.


Note: Google Business Profile Chat is not included in this playbook because Google discontinued Business Profile Chat functionality in July 2024.

Qualify New Leads


Qualification helps determine which prospects are appropriate for your coaching or consulting offer before your team invests significant time in the sales process. The right qualification process depends on the price, complexity, and eligibility requirements of your service.


You can collect information such as:

  • The prospect's primary goal or challenge.

  • The service or program they are interested in.

  • Their preferred timeline.

  • Relevant business or personal context.

  • Budget or investment expectations when appropriate.

  • Other criteria that determine whether your offer is a good fit.


Qualification can be handled through:

  • Forms or applications.

  • Surveys.

  • Workflow conditions.

  • Tags or custom fields.

  • Manual conversations.

  • Conversation AI.

Use Conversation AI for Qualification


Conversation AI can help respond to common questions, collect prospect information, and guide qualified leads toward the appropriate consultation calendar. This can be especially useful when leads arrive outside normal business hours or when your team receives a high volume of repetitive inquiries.


Configure the bot around your actual sales process so it knows:

  • Common questions about your coaching or consulting services.

  • Information it should collect from prospects.

  • Qualification criteria relevant to the offer.

  • Which calendar should be offered to an appropriate prospect.

  • When the conversation should be handed to a team member.


For setup instructions, see How to Set Up a Conversation AI Bot.

Book Consultations


Consultations create a structured transition from lead qualification into a sales conversation. Depending on your business model, you may offer a free discovery call, a paid strategy session, or different consultation types for different services.


Create the appropriate calendar for your offer and configure:

  • Availability.

  • Appointment duration.

  • Required booking information.

  • Confirmation communication.

  • Appointment reminders.

  • Rescheduling and cancellation options.

  • No-show follow-up.


Free Discovery Calls


Use a free consultation when the primary goal is to determine fit, understand the prospect's needs, and recommend an appropriate service.


Paid Consultations


Use Calendar Payments when the consultation itself is a paid service, such as a strategy session, assessment, audit, or advisory appointment.


Book Appointments with Conversation AI


When appropriate, Conversation AI can guide a qualified prospect toward an available calendar and help complete the booking during the conversation.


For details, see Appointment Booking in Conversation AI.


Follow Up with No-Shows


A missed consultation should have a defined follow-up path rather than being treated as a lost lead immediately.


Consider a workflow that:

  1. Identifies the missed appointment.

  2. Sends a follow-up message.

  3. Provides a rescheduling option.

  4. Creates an internal follow-up task when appropriate.

  5. Moves the opportunity to the applicable pipeline stage.

Manage Leads through the Sales Pipeline


A pipeline gives your team visibility into where each prospective client stands in the sales process. Stages should reflect meaningful steps in your actual coaching or consulting sales journey rather than being treated as a fixed template.


An example pipeline is:


New Lead → Qualified → Consultation Booked → Showed → Offer/Proposal → Won / Lost


Depending on your sales process, you may also add stages such as:

  • No Show

  • Follow-Up

  • Decision Pending

  • Long-Term Nurture

Use workflows where appropriate to create or update Opportunities when important events occur, such as:

  • A new qualified lead is captured.

  • A consultation is booked.

  • An appointment is completed or missed.

  • A payment is received.

  • The prospect becomes a client.


For foundational setup, see Getting Started - Setup Pipelines and Opportunities.

Convert Consultations into Clients


The payment experience should match how your coaching or consulting service is sold. A paid strategy session, multi-month coaching program, and project-based consulting engagement may require different payment methods.


Consider the following options:

  • Paid Consultation: Collect payment during calendar booking.

  • Program or Package Purchase: Use an appropriate checkout or Payment Link.

  • Invoice-Based Consulting: Send an invoice when the engagement requires a formal amount due.

  • Recurring Coaching: Use an appropriate recurring payment or subscription configuration when the service is billed repeatedly.

  • Text-Based Payment Collection: Use supported payment-request tools when collecting payment during an active client conversation is appropriate.

Configure a supported payment provider before using payment-dependent features.


For paid appointments, see Collecting Payments in Calendars.

Onboard New Clients


A structured onboarding process helps new clients understand what happens after they purchase and gives your team the information needed to begin the engagement. Automating routine onboarding steps can also reduce the delay between payment and service delivery.


After a prospect becomes a client, consider automatically triggering:

  • A welcome email or SMS.

  • An intake questionnaire or form.

  • A kickoff-call booking link.

  • Internal notifications or tasks.

  • Client Portal access.

  • Course or training access where applicable.

  • Community access where applicable.

  • Instructions for submitting files or other required information.


Use Client Portal for Ongoing Client Access


Client Portal can provide clients with a centralized place to access supported resources such as courses, communities, billing information, shared files, and account settings.


Use Client Portal when your coaching or consulting engagement requires an ongoing client-facing experience rather than only appointments and messages.


For setup instructions, see How to Set Up the Client Portal.

Use Courses for Training-Based Coaching Programs


If your coaching program includes lessons, modules, recordings, worksheets, or other structured training material, MxP Courses can provide an optional delivery method.


Courses are not required for every coaching or consulting business. Use them when structured digital content forms part of the client experience.

Generate Reviews and Referrals


Reviews can help prospective clients evaluate the experiences of previous customers, but timing matters. Request feedback after a meaningful success point rather than automatically asking immediately after every purchase.


Potential review-request moments include:

  • Completion of a successful consulting engagement.

  • Achievement of an agreed milestone.

  • Completion of a coaching program.

  • Positive feedback from the client.

  • Another appropriate success event in your customer journey.


MxP review requests can be sent manually or automated through workflows using supported communication channels.


A simple workflow can be:


Success milestone → Review request → Reminder if appropriate → Continue client nurture


For setup instructions, see How to Send Review Requests.

Retain, Upsell, and Reactivate Clients


The relationship does not have to end when an initial coaching program or consulting engagement is completed. Continued communication can help identify clients who need additional services while also giving inactive prospects another opportunity to engage.


Consider campaigns for:

  • Coaching renewals.

  • Follow-up consulting sessions.

  • Higher-tier programs.

  • Additional services.

  • Alumni or past-client communication.

  • Community engagement.

  • Educational newsletters.

  • Dormant-lead reactivation.

  • Past-client reactivation.

  • New program or offer announcements.


Segment audiences so clients and prospects receive communication relevant to their history and current relationship with the business.

Tip: Treat newsletters as one part of the retention strategy rather than the entire repeat-business plan.

Track Coaching & Consulting Performance


Reporting helps you identify where prospects move successfully through the customer journey and where additional optimization may be needed. Choose metrics that reflect your actual sales model rather than tracking activity without a clear business purpose.


Consider monitoring:

  • Leads Captured: Number of new inquiries entering the system.

  • Qualified Leads: Prospects who meet your defined qualification criteria.

  • Consultations Booked: Number of sales or strategy appointments scheduled.

  • Consultation Show Rate: Percentage of booked consultations that occur.

  • Open Opportunities: Active prospects currently in the sales pipeline.

  • Won Opportunities: Opportunities converted into clients.

  • Pipeline Value: Value associated with active Opportunities.

  • Collected Revenue: Payments received through applicable MxP payment tools.

  • Review Performance: Review requests and resulting client feedback where applicable.


Use MxP dashboards and reporting tools to create views appropriate to your business.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Should I offer a free or paid consultation?

That depends on your business model. A free discovery call can help determine fit before presenting an offer, while a paid consultation may be appropriate when the session itself provides standalone strategic or advisory value. MxP supports both free appointment booking and paid calendar appointments.


Q: How can I qualify leads before they book a consultation?

Use forms, surveys/applications, custom fields, workflows, manual conversations, or Conversation AI to collect the information required by your qualification process. The criteria should reflect your specific coaching or consulting offer.


Q: Can Conversation AI book consultations?

Yes. Conversation AI can support appointment booking when the bot and applicable calendars are configured for that workflow. It can also collect information and answer questions before moving the prospect toward booking.


Q: How should I follow up with a consultation no-show?

Use a workflow to acknowledge the missed appointment, provide a rescheduling path, and create additional follow-up when appropriate. You can also update the Opportunity so the sales team can see that the prospect did not attend.


Q: When should I request a client review?

Choose a meaningful success point, such as completion of an engagement, achievement of a milestone, or positive client feedback. The appropriate timing depends on the service and client journey.


Q: How can I reactivate old coaching or consulting leads?

Segment leads that did not purchase or stopped engaging and send a relevant reactivation campaign. This might include a new consultation opportunity, educational content, a new program, or another reason to restart the conversation.


Q: Do I need Client Portal or Courses for a coaching program?

No. Both are optional. Client Portal is useful when clients need ongoing access to resources, billing, files, courses, or communities. Courses are useful when your program includes structured digital training content.
