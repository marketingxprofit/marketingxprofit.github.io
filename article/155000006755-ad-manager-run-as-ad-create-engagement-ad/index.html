Ad Manager Run As Ad & Create Engagement Ad

New buttons bring one-click ad creation links (“breadcrumbs”) straight into two of MxP’s most-used tools—WhatsApp and Social Planner—so you can turn conversations and social posts into paid ads without ever hunting for Ad Manager.

What are the New Buttons (breadcrumbs) for Ad Manager in WhatsApp & Social Planner?


There are now buttons in WhatsApp and Social Planner that open Ad Manager in one-click preconfigured to run an ad. These context-aware “Create Engagement Ad” (WhatsApp) and “Run as Ad” (Social Planner) buttons deep-link to Ad Manager. Each link pre-loads the relevant campaign objective and post content, trimming several manual steps from the usual ad-creation flow.

Key Benefits of Breadcrumbing


• Accelerates campaign setup—pre-selected objective, destination, media, and copy cut down form-filling time.


• Boosts Ad Manager adoption by surfacing upgrade prompts exactly where non-Ad-Manager users work.


• Improves workflow continuity—marketers move from organic content to paid promotion in a single click.


• Reduces user error; the correct objective (Engagement) and channel (WhatsApp/Facebook/Instagram) are automatically applied.

WhatsApp > Create Engagement Ad


Quickly convert a WhatsApp phone number into an Engagement campaign.


• Button location: Settings > WhatsApp > Numbers tab, next to “Add Number”.


• Auto-filled in Ad Manager:


    – Objective: Engagement


    – Destination: WhatsApp


    – Budget, audience, and creative remain editable.


• Users without Ad Manager see a subscription-benefits modal with “Connect Ad Manager” CTA.


• Users with Ad Manager but without a connected Ad Account see a button "Connect Account".


Social Planner > Run As Ad


Turn Social Planner posts into ad campaigns without manually recreating the content in Ad Manager. Run as Ad supports campaign creation for Meta, Google, and TikTok.


How to Run a Social Post as a TikTok Ad


  1. Navigate to Marketing > Social Planner > Planner.

  2. Find the social post you want to use for your ad campaign.

  3. Click the three-dot menu (⋮) next to the post.

  4. Select Run as Ad.

  5. In the confirmation window, click Continue to open Ad Manager.

  6. In the Create campaign window, select TikTok as your advertising platform.

  7. Click Next to continue to the campaign builder.

  8. Review the prefilled ad text and media. Make any necessary changes.

  9. Complete the remaining campaign settings.

  10. Review your campaign and publish it.


Tip: Connect your TikTok Ad Account in Ad Manager before starting your campaign.






How Run as Ad Uses Your Social Post Content


When you create a TikTok ad campaign using Run as Ad:


  • Ad Creative: Images and videos from the selected social post are carried into the campaign builder.

  • Ad Copy: AI generates ad text based on the selected post, following TikTok's advertising guidelines.

  • Posts Without Media: The builder provides ad text. You can add images or a video before publishing.

  • Multiple Images: A social post containing multiple images becomes a TikTok Carousel Ad. You must upload music before publishing the carousel ad.

Important Considerations


  • Ad content is prefilled only when you first create the campaign.

  • Editing an existing TikTok campaign does not automatically pull content from the original social post again.

  • Review your ad text, media, and campaign settings before publishing.

  • TikTok campaigns must meet TikTok's applicable advertising requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q. Does breadcrumbing cost extra?

No. The feature is free, but the sub-account must have an active Ad Manager subscription or pay-as-you-go plan.



Q. Why don’t I see the “Create Engagement Ad” button in WhatsApp?

Confirm you’re on the Numbers tab and that your location has WhatsApp enabled. Standard user permissions also apply.



Q: Can I create TikTok ads using Run as Ad?

Yes. Select a social post in Social Planner, click Run as Ad, and choose TikTok in Ad Manager. The campaign builder uses content from the selected post to help you create your TikTok ad.



Q: Does editing the original social post update my TikTok ad campaign?

No. Run as Ad prefills content when you first create the campaign. Changes made later to the original social post do not automatically update the existing campaign. Edit the ad directly in Ad Manager.



Q. Can I bypass the confirmation modal?

No. The modal prevents accidental navigation and clarifies you’re entering the Ad Manager flow.
