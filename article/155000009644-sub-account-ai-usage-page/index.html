Sub-Account AI Usage Page

Each of your sub-accounts has an AI usage page. It shows the sub-account's AI plan, how much of its monthly AI limit it has used, what its plan includes, and what happens when it reaches the limit. From the same page, the sub-account can ask you for more usage or upgrade its own plan. Sub-accounts you rebill for AI can also set their own monthly AI limit, on top of yours, once you allow it.


This article shows you, as the agency, exactly what your sub-accounts see and can do, so you can set things up the way you want and answer their questions. What a sub-account sees depends on two things you control: how you bill it for AI, and who you give access to.


Note: The sub-account AI usage page is currently available in Labs. Features and screens may change as we roll it out more widely.

Letting Sub-Accounts Set Their Own AI Limit

You can let the sub-accounts you rebill for AI set a monthly limit of their own. It caps what they are billed for AI each month, so a busy month never turns into a surprise bill. Your own limit for the sub-account stays exactly as it is, and stays yours.

Turn it on

It is off until you turn it on, and it applies to your whole agency.

  1. Go to AI Suite → Plans & Limits and click Edit defaults.
  2. Under Sub-account own limits, turn on Let rebilled sub-accounts set their own AI limit.

The switch in Plans & Limits → Edit defaults

Which limit is for what

Both are monthly AI caps for one sub-account, but they measure different money:

  • Your limit (Agency) caps what the sub-account's AI costs you, before any markup. It works whether you rebill or not. Use it when you think: "I don't want this client's AI to cost me more than $60 a month."
  • Its own limit (Sub-account) caps what the sub-account is billed, after your markup. Use it when they think: "We don't want to be billed more than $100 for AI this month."

Which limit wins?

Whichever is reached first. Example, with every AI product rebilled at a 50% markup, so each $1 of AI costs you $1 and bills them $1.50:

  • Your limit is $60 and theirs is $100. When AI has cost you $60, they have been billed $90. Your limit is reached first, so AI stops even though theirs isn't reached.
  • Your limit is $60 and theirs is $75. When they have been billed $75, it has cost you $50. Their limit is reached first, so their billed AI stops.

Which products each limit covers

Your limit always covers every AI product in the sub-account. Its own limit covers only the products you rebill to it, because those are the only ones it is billed for.

  • Say you rebill Conversation AI and Voice AI, but pay for Ask AI and AI Studio yourself. Your limit counts all four. Its own limit counts only Conversation AI and Voice AI.
  • When its own limit is reached, only Conversation AI and Voice AI pause. Ask AI and AI Studio keep running, because you pay for them.
  • Start rebilling another product later? It joins the same own limit straight away. The own limit is one monthly amount shared by every rebilled product, not one per product.

Anything the sub-account isn't charged for never counts toward its own limit and is never paused by it: usage included in its AI Employee plan, promos, trials and free usage, and products you don't rebill to it.

Setting the own limit

Sub-account admins open AI → AI Usage and click Set a limit on their own-limit card. You can do the same from the sub-account's AI Usage page.

  1. Turn on Limit monthly spending and enter an amount, or pick one of the suggested amounts.
  2. Under Advanced settings, choose Stop AI (recommended) or Keep running and alert me.
  3. Click Save.

When you set or change a sub-account's own limit, its admins are emailed. Sub-account users can't change it; they ask an admin.

When the own limit is reached

  • Stop AI: new AI work the sub-account pays for pauses, and anything already running finishes. AI you pay for, or that its plan includes, keeps running. Its admins see that AI is paused, with Raise limit.
  • Keep running and alert me: AI keeps running, and usage past the limit is billed as usual.

Its admins are emailed as usage nears the limit and when it is reached. AI runs again as soon as the limit is raised or removed, or on the 1st of next month.

Turning the switch off later

Before it turns off, you'll see how many sub-accounts have set their own limit. Once it's off, those limits stop applying and their AI Usage page shows only your limit. Nothing is deleted: turn the switch back on and the same limits apply again.

Note: Per-user limits have been retired. Each sub-account has one monthly limit for everyone in it.

How Sub-Accounts Ask You for More Usage


When you manage a sub-account's limit, anyone in it can ask you for more, from a banner (Request more usage) or from the AI usage page (Ask your agency). They can add a reason, then click Submit Request.


What the sub-account sees when it asks for more usage



The confirmation after a request is sent


On the sub-account's AI usage page, the request shows under Higher limit requested as Waiting for review, with Withdraw to cancel it. Once you decide, it changes to approved or Declined.


A request waiting for your review, as the sub-account sees it


What you see and do


The request appears above your AI Suite → Plans & Limits table and in the Updates panel. Click Raise limit to approve it, at the amount you choose, or Deny to decline it. When you raise the limit, the person who asked gets an email, and if the sub-account's AI was paused it runs again right away. For the full steps, see AI Usage Limits.


Note: Each person in a sub-account can have one open request at a time. If you rebill all of a sub-account's AI at one rate, requests from its users go to its own admins instead, under Requests from your team, and you are not asked.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q. Can sub-account users see the AI usage page?

A. No. Only sub-account admins see it, along with you and your agency team. Sub-account users see usage banners on the AI product pages and can ask for more usage from them.


Q. Why does my sub-account see its limit as a percentage instead of dollars?

A. Sub-accounts see their limit in dollars only when you rebill all their AI at one rate. In every other setup you manage the limit, and they see how much of it is used as a percentage.


Q. How do I let a sub-account change its spending limit?

A. Rebill every AI product for that sub-account at one rate in Configure Rebilling. From the next month in which all its charges are at that rate, its admins can change your spending limit for it (in its dollars) and approve requests from their team.


Q. Can my sub-account set its own limit?

A. Yes, once you turn on Let rebilled sub-accounts set their own AI limit in Plans & Limits → Edit defaults and you rebill at least one AI product to it. Its admins set it on their AI Usage page. It counts only what they are billed and sits alongside your limit, which stays yours.

Q. Which limit applies if both are set?

A. Both. AI stops at whichever is reached first.

Q. Can a sub-account see my costs or markup?

A. No. Once you let it set its own limit, it sees its own limit in its own dollars and your limit only as a percentage. Before that, if you rebill everything at one rate, it sees your limit converted into its own dollars, never your cost or markup.

Q. Can I change or remove a sub-account’s own limit?

A. Yes, from its AI Usage page. Its admins are emailed when you do.

Q. What happens to own limits if I turn the switch off?

A. They stop applying, but they are kept. Turn the switch back on and the same limits apply again.


Q. A sub-account's AI stopped. What can it do?

A. It can click Request more usage or Ask your agency to send you a request, or its admins can upgrade its plan. If its admins manage their own limit, they can raise it themselves. You can raise the limit at any time in Plans & Limits, and AI runs again right away.


Q. Why do my sub-accounts see different plan prices from the ones I see?

A. Sub-accounts see the prices you set under Edit defaults → Pricing → Subscription prices. In your agency view you see MxP's list prices, which is what your agency pays.


Q. Can a sub-account downgrade or cancel its own plan?

A. No. Only you can downgrade or cancel a sub-account's plan.


Q. Does usage included in a plan count toward the limit?

A. No. Included usage is used first and never counts. Only billable usage counts toward the limit.


Q. When does a limit reset?

A. On the 1st of every month at 00:00 UTC.


Q. Can sub-accounts set a limit for each of their users?

A. Not at the moment. Per-user limits have been retired, and each sub-account has one monthly limit. We plan to bring user-level controls back in a future update.
