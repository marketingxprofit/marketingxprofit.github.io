What is Manual Attendee Registration?

Manual attendee registration allows event organizers to create event registrations directly from the Registrations page in MxP. This feature bypasses the public registration form, enabling you to register attendees on their behalf.

This capability is designed for scenarios where attendees cannot or prefer not to register online, such as walk-in registrations at the event venue, phone bookings, offline sales through partners, complimentary ticket allocations, or corporate group registrations.

All manually created registrations are fully integrated with your event management system—tickets are issued immediately, confirmation emails are sent automatically, and registration data syncs with your attendee dashboard and ticket inventory in real time.

Flexible Registration Channels — Accept registrations through any channel including walk-ins, phone calls, email requests, partner referrals, and offline sales without requiring attendees to use the online form.
Capacity Override — Register attendees even after ticket capacity has been reached, giving you control over last-minute accommodations and VIP allocations.
Streamlined Workflow — Create registrations, assign tickets, and process payments from a single interface without switching between multiple tools or systems.
Contact Integration — Select existing contacts from your CRM or create new contact records during registration, maintaining a unified attendee database.

Real-Time Synchronization — All manually created registrations appear instantly in your Registrations dashboard with complete audit trails and payment records.

How to Manually Add Attendees

Follow these steps to create a manual registration for your event attendees:

Step 1
Navigate to the Registrations Page

Go to Events → Registrations. Create or edit an event. Click the Add Attendee button to open the manual registration workflow.

Step 2
Select Ticket Types and Quantities

Choose one or multiple ticket types from the available options for your event. Specify the quantity for each ticket type. Click on Continue.

Step 3
Assign Attendee Information

For each ticket, either select an existing contact from your CRM or create a new contact record. Complete the required attendee information fields.

If you're registering multiple tickets, you can copy attendee details across tickets to speed up the process when one person is purchasing multiple tickets.


Note

When creating a new contact during registration, the contact is added to your CRM and immediately associated with the event registration. All contact information becomes available for future campaigns and event invitations.

Step 4
Complete Payment Processing

Choose your payment collection method and complete the transaction. Options include charging a card through your connected payment provider or recording an offline payment. Once done, click on Add & Record Payment.


Step 5
Confirm Registration

Tickets are issued immediately and confirmation emails are sent automatically to all registered attendees.


Success

The registration appears instantly in your Registrations dashboard with complete details including attendee information, ticket assignments, and payment records.

Payment Collection Options

Manual attendee registration supports two payment collection methods, giving you flexibility to accommodate different transaction scenarios:

Option 1
Charge a Card

Process the payment immediately using your connected payment provider (Stripe, Authorize.net, PayPal, or NMI). This option charges the attendee's credit or debit card in real time and generates an instant payment confirmation.

Option 2
Record an Offline Payment

Document a payment that was collected outside the system using methods such as Cash, Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or Other. This option creates a payment record without processing a transaction through your payment provider.

Payment Records

Both payment methods create a complete audit trail in your Registrations dashboard. All transactions—whether processed online or recorded offline—are documented with timestamps, payment methods, and amounts for accurate financial reporting.

Ticket Issuance and Confirmations

When you complete a manual registration, MxP automatically handles ticket issuance and attendee communications:

Instant Ticket Generation — Tickets are created immediately after registration completion with unique ticket IDs and QR codes for event check-in.
Automatic Email Confirmations — Confirmation emails containing ticket details, event information, and calendar invites are sent automatically to all registered attendees.
Dashboard Synchronization — Registrations appear instantly in your Registrations dashboard with complete attendee data, ticket assignments, and payment information.
Inventory Updates — Ticket inventory is updated in real time, ensuring accurate availability counts for all registration channels.
Email Customization

Confirmation emails use your event's configured email templates. You can customize these templates in your event settings to match your branding and include custom messaging for attendees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I register attendees after ticket capacity has been reached?
Yes. Manual registration allows you to create registrations even when tickets show as sold out. This gives you flexibility to accommodate VIP guests, last-minute requests, or capacity adjustments without modifying ticket settings.
Q: Do manually registered attendees receive the same confirmation emails as online registrations?
Yes. All manually registered attendees receive automatic confirmation emails with ticket details, event information, and calendar invites, identical to attendees who register through the public form.
Q: Can I register multiple attendees in a single transaction?
Yes. You can select multiple ticket types and quantities in one registration. Each ticket requires attendee information, but you can copy details across tickets to speed up the process when one person is purchasing multiple tickets.
Q: What payment methods can I use for offline payments?
You can record offline payments using Cash, Card (when processed outside MxP), Check, Bank Transfer, or Other. These options create payment records without processing transactions through your connected payment provider.
Q: Can I create a manual registration for an existing contact in my CRM?
Yes. During the registration process, you can select any existing contact from your CRM database. The event registration and ticket information are automatically added to that contact's record.
Q: Can I issue complimentary tickets using manual registration?
Yes. If your event includes free ticket types, you can select those tickets during manual registration. For paid tickets, you can record an offline payment with a zero amount or use a custom payment method to document complimentary allocations.
