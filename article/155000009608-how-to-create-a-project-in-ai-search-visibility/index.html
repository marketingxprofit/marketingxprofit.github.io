How to Create a Project in AI & Search Visibility

Creating a project is the first step to using AI & Search Visibility in MxP. Each project connects a business website with its SEO & AEO audit results, AI search tracking, and recommendations. Setting up the correct business information helps ensure that your brand is recognized accurately in AI-generated answers and reports.

What is a Project in AI & Search Visibility?


A project is a dedicated workspace for monitoring and improving the search visibility of a single website domain. It brings together website SEO audits, AI search performance, competitor tracking, and recommendations for that business.


Each project has its own business information, tracking configuration, and reports. During the beta, each MxP location (sub-account) can have up to 4 projects per monthly cycle.

Understanding Project Details


Your project's business information plays an important role in how AI & Search Visibility identifies your brand and tracks results. Using the correct business name and website from the beginning helps make reports more meaningful.


The main project details include:


FieldDescription
Project NameA name used to identify and organize your project within MxP.
Business NameThe official customer-facing name of the business. The tool uses this information to identify the brand in search results, AI-generated answers, reports, and recommendations.
Business WebsiteThe website domain that the project will monitor and audit.


Important: Enter the Business Name exactly as customers commonly recognize and refer to the business. For example, if a business is publicly known as "Acme Plumbing," entering a different internal or abbreviated name may cause some AI-generated mentions to go unrecognized.


AI Search Prompts and Competitors


AI search tracking depends on the questions customers ask and the competing businesses they encounter. Configuring relevant prompts and competitors helps produce results that reflect your business's actual market.


During project setup, you can configure:


  • Prompts: Questions that reflect what potential customers might ask an AI search engine. The beta supports up to 10 active prompts per project.

  • Competitors: Businesses you want to compare against your brand. Competitors may be identified automatically or added manually, up to 5 per project.


For example, a plumbing business could track a branded prompt such as "Does Acme Plumbing offer emergency plumbing services?" alongside an unbranded prompt such as "Who offers emergency plumbing services in Denver?"


Tip: Choose prompts that reflect your customers' real questions and competitors that serve the same audience. Irrelevant prompts or unrelated competitors can make visibility reports less useful.


How to Create a Project in AI & Search Visibility


Creating your first project connects the business website to its visibility workspace and automatically starts the first SEO audit. Completing the setup with accurate information helps establish a reliable starting point for future reports.


  1. Open AI & Search Visibility from the main menu in old UI and Marketing > AI & Search Visibility in the new UI and begin creating a new project.

  2. In Project Details, enter the required business information:

    • Project Name: Enter a recognizable name to identify the project.

    • Business Name: Enter the official customer-facing business name.

    • Business Website: Enter the website domain you want to monitor.

    • Complete any additional required business or location fields displayed during setup.

  3. Continue to Prompts and review or configure the questions you want to track for AI search visibility. Keep the beta limit of 10 active prompts in mind.

  4. Continue to Competitors. Review automatically identified competitors, if available, or add relevant businesses manually. You can track up to 5 competitors per project.

  5. Review your information and complete project creation.

  6. Once the project is created, the first SEO & AEO audit starts automatically. Allow a few minutes for the scan to complete before reviewing its findings.
