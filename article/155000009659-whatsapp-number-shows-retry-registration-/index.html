WhatsApp Number Shows "Retry Registration"

WhatsApp Number Shows "Retry Registration"

Why the Retry Registration button appears next to your WhatsApp number, and how to complete the registration.

Table of Contents

What You Will See Why This Happens Before You Fix It How to Fix It Troubleshooting Frequently Asked Questions

What You Will See

Go to Settings → Communications → WhatsApp → Numbers. If registration for a number was not completed, a Retry Registration button appears in the Actions column on that number's row.

Why This Happens

The most common cause is that the number is still registered in the WhatsApp app or the WhatsApp Business app on a phone.

When you connect a number through the standard sign-up method, Meta requires that the number does not have an active WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app account. While the app account exists, the registration cannot finish, so the CRM shows Retry Registration.

Want to keep using the WhatsApp Business app? You do not have to delete it. Connect the number through the Coexistence method instead, which lets the WhatsApp Business app and the CRM share the same number and keeps your chat history.
↗ WhatsApp Coexistence Method — full setup guide

Before You Fix It

Deleting the WhatsApp account is permanent. The steps below delete the account for that number in the app, including its chat history. Once the number is registered for the CRM, it cannot be used in the WhatsApp Business app again unless it is first removed from the platform.
✓Back up your chats in the WhatsApp Business app first, if you want to keep them.
✓Make sure you have the phone that uses the number, and that it can receive an SMS or voice call for the verification code.
✓Confirm the customer no longer needs the number in the app. If they do, use Coexistence instead.

How to Fix It

1

Open the App That Holds the Number

On the phone that uses the number, open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.

2

Delete the Account

Go to Settings → Account and select Delete my account (some app versions say Delete account). Enter the phone number and follow the on-screen steps to confirm.

If the number is used in both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, or on more than one phone, make sure no active account remains on any of them.

3

Wait a Few Minutes

Meta notes that it can take up to about 3 minutes after deletion for the number to become available again.

4

Click Retry Registration

In the CRM, go to Settings → Communications → WhatsApp → Numbers and click Retry Registration in the Actions column.

5

Verify the Number With the Code

Meta sends a one-time passcode (OTP) to the number by SMS or voice call. Enter it to finish the registration. Once it completes, the Retry Registration button no longer appears for that number.

Tip: After registration completes, send a test message to an internal contact and reply to it, to confirm messages flow both ways.

Troubleshooting

Issue
What to do
Retry Registration fails again right away
Wait a few more minutes and retry. Also check that no WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business account is still active for the number on any phone.
The verification code does not arrive
Confirm the line can receive SMS and calls, then request the code by voice call instead of SMS.
The number is connected through another provider
The number must first be moved away from that provider. See the guide on migrating a number from another partner.
The button is still there after all of the above
Contact Support with the details below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does Retry Registration appear?

Most often because the number still has an active WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app account. Numbers connected through the standard sign-up method must not have one.

Q: Will I lose my chats if I delete the account?

Yes. Deleting the account removes its chat history from the app. Back up your chats first, or use the Coexistence method if you want to keep the WhatsApp Business app and its history.

Q: Can I use the same number in the WhatsApp Business app and in the CRM?

Yes, but only through the Coexistence method with the WhatsApp Business app. With the standard connection, the number cannot also be active in the app.

Q: Does this also apply to the regular WhatsApp app?

Yes. A number registered in the regular WhatsApp app must also have that account deleted before it can be registered through the standard method.

Q: Is "deactivating" the account enough?

WhatsApp does not offer a temporary deactivation for this purpose. Use Delete my account in the app, which removes the app registration from the number.
