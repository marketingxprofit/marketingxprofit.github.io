In App Calendar view

The In-App Calendar View in MxP provides a centralized way to view and manage scheduled appointments and calendar events. You can switch between daily and monthly views, compare schedules across multiple users, calendars, or groups, and identify events using colors and icons. These options make it easier to monitor appointments and navigate your schedule.

What is In-App Calendar View?


The In-App Calendar View is a scheduling interface within MxP that displays appointments and supported synced calendar events. It allows you to view schedules by user, calendar, or group, making it easier to manage appointments without switching between individual calendars.

Key Benefits of In-App Calendar View


The In-App Calendar View provides flexible viewing options and visual indicators to help you organize appointments and compare schedules efficiently.


  • Flexible Calendar Views: Switch between Day and Month views to review appointments across different time periods.

  • Multiple Schedule Selection: View appointments for multiple users, calendars, or groups together.

  • Color-Coded Appointments: Distinguish appointments using staff and calendar colors.

  • Event Identification: Use icons and the annotation key to identify event types.

  • Saved Selections: Retain selected users and calendars when returning to the calendar.

  • Quick Appointment Details: Hover over appointments to view essential information without opening them.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Why can't I see the Meetings scheduling type dropdown?

The dropdown appears only when multiple scheduling types are enabled. If only one scheduling type is active, MxP displays it automatically.



Q: Can I hide the calendar filters to create more viewing space?

Yes. Click Manage View to collapse or expand the filter panel.



Q: Can I change the colors displayed on calendar appointments?

Yes. Calendar View Settings provides event color display options. Admins can also configure staff colors.



Q: Do calendar display preferences change appointment availability?

No. Calendar display settings only affect how appointments appear. They do not change scheduling rules or booking availability.
