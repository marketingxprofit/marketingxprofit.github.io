WordPress Hosting: Specs, Market Comparison, and New Pricing Plans

At the heart of our CRM, we've integrated a comprehensive WordPress hosting solution designed to simplify website management while delivering top-notch performance. Whether you're setting up your first site or managing multiple clients, our hosting service has you covered with robust features, fast speeds, and reliable support.

? What Our WordPress Hosting Offers

  • Blazing Fast Speeds: Powered by Cloudflare Enterprise CDN for lightning-fast global delivery.

  • Unmatched Uptime and Security: Rest easy with daily backups, one-click restores, and strict security protocols that keep your site safe and running smoothly.

  • Free SSL + Global CDN: Enterprise-grade reliability via Cloudflare comes standard.

  • 24/7 Dedicated Support Channel: Direct access to our advanced support team around the clock.

Pricing Note: All WordPress Hosting plans are in addition to your MxP plan.

Example: MxP Starter ($97/mo) + WordPress Standard ($10/mo) = $107/mo. For this, you get all the marketing automation features of MxP plus 1 fully supported WordPress site.

? WordPress Hosting Plans

Once you are a MxP subscriber, we offer three powerful WordPress hosting plans to choose from. You can find and activate these in the Agency > Reselling section of your account.

No contracts – cancel anytime. Prices are in addition to the cost of a MxP subscription.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Do I need to do anything to access these plans?

A: No! Simply navigate to Agency > Reselling in your MxP account to get started. All plan benefits apply immediately upon subscription.

Q: What does "unmetered bandwidth" mean?

A: It means there are no strict bandwidth limits — your sites won't slow down due to data caps. However, standard fair usage policies still apply.

Q: Is there a discount for paying annually?

A: Yes! All three plans offer a discounted rate when billed annually: Standard at $9/mo, Utility at $208/mo, and Unlimited at $414/mo.

Q: Where can I check my storage allocation?

A: You can easily view your current storage usage in the Hosting Dashboard under Site Details.
