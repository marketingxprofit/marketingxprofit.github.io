1. Why is MxP charging me tax?

Like most software companies selling internationally, MxP is required by law to collect VAT, GST, or HST in many countries. These are consumption taxes that local governments require businesses to collect on digital services — even when the seller is based overseas.


Whether you're charged tax, and how much, depends on where your business is located.


Registered business? In some countries, you may be able to account for the  tax exemption on future purchases, instead of paying it to MxP.This is called  "reverse charge." To apply, add your Tax ID to your billing profile. This only affects invoices going forward; it won't change past charges.


2. Is tax charged on all MxP products?

Most MxP products and services are taxable. If you're unsure whether something specific is subject to tax in your country, your tax advisor is the best person to ask.


3. When does tax show up on my invoice?

For subscriptions, tax is charged when your invoice is generated and appears as a separate line item. For wallet usage, tax is handled differently — see the wallet tax FAQ below.


4. How does tax work for wallet charges?

Tax on wallet usage isn't charged when you top up your wallet. Instead, we add up your taxable usage at the end of each month, calculate the tax on that total, and the charge appears on your account between the 1st and 10th of the following month. By using wallet products, you authorize MxP to charge this tax to your wallet or payment method on file.


To see a breakdown, go to Settings → Billing → Wallet & Transactions → Detailed Transactions in your Agency and click the chevron (›) next to any transaction.


When you top up your wallet, you get a receipt confirming the funds were added. At the beginning of the following month, you get a separate tax invoice for your actual usage during the prior month.


The month-end invoice shows the services you used and the tax charged on them. Your accounting team or tax advisor can confirm how to record these documents and whether you can reclaim any tax. 


5. How do I add or update my Tax ID?

You can update your tax ID in your account settings. Please contact us using 24/7 Customer Support Options

if you need assistance. 


6. What if I don't have a Tax ID?

If your business isn't registered for VAT/GST/HST, you can indicate that in your billing profile. Tax will continue to be applied to your invoices as normal.


If you're not sure whether your business should be registered, check with your local tax authority or a tax advisor.


7. What if I have more than one Tax ID?

If your business operates across multiple countries and has separate Tax IDs for each, please contact us using

24/7 Customer Support Options and we can help make sure everything is set up correctly on your account.


8. Am I being charged tax?

Not everyone is. MxP collects tax in the countries where it is required to do so. If we do not charge tax, you may still need to account for tax locally under your country’s rules


Even in countries where we do collect tax, registered businesses that provide a valid Tax ID are often subject to reverse charge. In that case, we do not collect tax, and you account for it yourself. 


Tax rules can change, so what applies today may be different in the future.


9. I gave you my Tax ID — why am I still being charged?

In some countries, all customers are charged tax regardless of whether they have a Tax ID. This is because those countries don't apply "reverse charge" purchases from overseas vendors.

It's also possible your Tax ID has a formatting issue. Check Stripe's Tax ID formatting guide for the correct format for your country.


10. I got an error entering my Tax ID. What do I do?

If you're getting an error, the most common cause is a formatting issue. Check Stripe's Tax ID formatting guide to make sure your number matches the expected format for your country.


If you're an EU customer, we use the EU's VIES system to verify VAT numbers. You can check whether your number is valid on the VIES website (ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies) before reaching out.


If the error persists, contact us and we'll help sort it out.


EU customers: When selecting your Tax ID type, always choose eu_vat — regardless of which EU country your business is in. Using a country-specific type instead of eu_vat is the most common cause of validation errors for EU customers.


11. I said I don't have a Tax ID, but now I do. How do I add it?

You can update your Tax ID any time in your billing profile. Once it's saved and verified, we'll adjust tax on your future invoices accordingly.


12. Can I get a refund of tax I've already paid?

If you believe you were charged tax in error, please contact us and we'll take a look.


13. How do I download a tax invoice?

You can find and download your invoices through the Account Billing Dashboard.


14. Which address do you use to calculate my tax?

We check your shipping address first. If there's no shipping address on file, we use your billing address. If neither is available, we use the address tied to your payment method.


15. I moved to a different country. How do I update my tax location?

Update your billing address and your tax location will update automatically. See the Account Billing Dashboard guide for step-by-step instructions.


16. What if I was charged the wrong tax rate?

If something doesn't look right, contact us and we'll review it.


17. Who can I contact with more questions?

For questions about whether tax applies to your specific situation, we recommend speaking with your tax advisor. For account questions, contact MxP directly. This article is general information and is not tax advice. You are responsible for keeping your Tax ID and address information accurate and for any tax you must account for locally.
