MxP Support Journey: What to Expect at Every Stage

Wherever you are in your MxP journey, you do not have to figure everything out alone. The support available to you evolves as you move from exploring MxP to building your account and running your business.


During your trial, the focus is helping you find the right place to start. During your first 90 days, you get more hands-on guidance while you build. After that, MxP Support remains available around the clock whenever you need help.

Your MxP Support Journey at a Glance


Your support needs change as you become more familiar with MxP. Each stage gives you a different starting point, from personalized onboarding guidance to ongoing technical support and optional dedicated assistance.


How to Find Support Inside MxP


IMPORTANT: Only Agency Admins/Users Can Access MxP Support

MxP Support agents are unable to make changes to an agency account without the consent of a validated Agency Admin or Agency User. If you do not have access to the Agency view, you must reach out to your Agency Admin.


When you need direct help, you can open MxP’s support experience without leaving the application. This gives eligible agency users a central place to choose the support option that fits their situation.


  1. Log in to your MxP account.

  2. Locate the blue question-mark button in the top-right corner of MxP.



  3. Click the button to open the Help & Support experience.

  4. Choose the available support option that best matches what you need.



For complete instructions on the individual support channels, see 24/7 Customer Support Options.

During Your Trial: Find the Right Place to Start


A MxP trial gives you a lot to explore. Your first priority does not need to be learning the entire platform. Instead, your Trial Experience is designed to help you identify the tools and setup steps that matter most for your business.

You can join a personalized Kickoff call with a MxP expert to discuss your business, goals, and intended use of the platform.


Your kickoff can help you:


  • Identify the most important areas of MxP to focus on first.

  • Connect your goals to the features and setup steps that support them.

  • Build a clearer path from exploring the platform to putting it to work.


Ready to get started?
Start or schedule your MxP Kickoff

Your First 90 Days: Build with MxP Experts


Once you become a MxP customer, the focus shifts from exploring the platform to building the systems your business needs.


During your first 90 days, you can receive hands-on guidance while you configure and adopt MxP. These working sessions can help you turn what you planned during onboarding into a working setup.


Depending on what your business needs, that can include:


  • Working through important setup tasks alongside a MxP expert.

  • Building or configuring the MxP tools that support your business.

  • Getting guidance based on your use case and industry.

  • Following up on what you built and addressing questions as you continue.

  • Identifying additional MxP tools that can help you make progress.


You also continue to have access to MxP support when you need additional help.


During your first 90 days, MxP Customer Success is your primary support resource.
Connect with the Customer Success team

After Your First 90 Days: Stay Supported


Getting established in MxP does not mean you stop needing help. As your account grows, new questions, troubleshooting needs, and more advanced use cases can come up.


After your first 90 days, MxP Support remains available 24/7 for product questions, technical troubleshooting, and help determining your next step.


Support options include live experiences such as Zoom and chat, along with support tickets when asynchronous follow-up works better. Issues can also be escalated when deeper technical investigation is required.


You do not need to learn every support process here.


See 24/7 Customer Support Options for detailed guidance on Live Chat, Zoom, support tickets, phone support, and other ways to contact MxP.

Need More Hands-On Support? Explore Premium Support


Some businesses need a closer support relationship as their MxP setup becomes more important or complex.


After completing your first 90 days with MxP, you can choose Premium Support as an optional paid add-on. Premium Support adds more personalized and proactive assistance to the standard support experience.


Premium Support includes services such as:


  • A dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM).

  • Priority support handling and faster support access.

  • Dedicated communication channels, including Slack.

  • Proactive guidance based on your business and MxP setup.

  • More direct support for complex technical needs.


For subscription, billing, setup, and cancellation instructions, see Upgrade to Premium Customer Support at MxP.
