How to Get Customer Support for WordPress Issues

MxP’s WordPress Setup Guide now includes three time‑saving Quick Links—Book a Call with WordPress Customer Success, join the Product Townhall, and open the WordPress Knowledge Base inside the app. These tools speed up troubleshooting, give you direct access to roadmap conversations, and keep help one click away. This article explains what each link does and how to use them to accelerate results.


Note: These Quick Links are only visible to Agency Admins.

What is the WordPress Customer Support Upgrade?


The WordPress Setup Guide now surfaces three support resources directly in your workflow so Agency Admins can get expert help, self‑serve answers, and real‑time product updates without leaving MxP. Each link focuses on a different need—guided help, product visibility, and instant knowledge access—to reduce delays and improve outcomes during site setup and beyond.


Book a Call with WordPress Customer Success: Schedule a guided call with the WordPress CS team. • Direct Access to Product Townhall: Join live townhalls to hear roadmap updates and share feedback. • WordPress Knowledge Base in‑App: Open the full WordPress KB from inside MxP to search and self‑serve. • Visibility: 

Key Benefits of WordPress Customer Support Upgrade


  • Faster Time to Value: jump from problem to solution by booking a CS call or opening the KB instantly.

  • Proactive Empowerment: learn, self‑serve, and track product direction without leaving your workflow.

  • Global Coverage: connect with support across most time zones for near‑always‑on help.

  • Trust & Retention: combine expert guidance, transparent roadmap access, and reliable documentation to build confidence with your clients.

Where to Find the Quick Links for WordPress Customer Support


Use this path to reliably locate the quick links 


  1. Login to your sub-account.

  2. Go to Sites > WordPress.



  3. Click the Quickstart button.



  4. Scroll to Quick Links (visible to Agency Admins only).

  5. Choose Book a Call, Join Product Townhall, or WordPress Knowledge Base.
