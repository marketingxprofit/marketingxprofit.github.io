Marketing Template Conversations Do Not Move to the Top of the WhatsApp Business App

Why a conversation stays in place after a Marketing template is sent from the CRM, and how to confirm that this is expected behavior.

Overview

When you use WhatsApp Coexistence with the CRM, Marketing template messages sent from the CRM may not move the associated conversation to the top of the WhatsApp Business app's Chats list.

The message is still delivered and synchronized correctly. You can find the conversation by searching for the contact or opening the existing chat. It moves to the top of the Chats list only after the customer replies.

Expected behavior: Meta has confirmed that this is expected for Marketing templates. It does not indicate a missing message or an account configuration issue.

Who Is Affected?

This behavior may affect accounts that:

✓Use WhatsApp Coexistence.
✓Have a WhatsApp Business app account linked to the CRM.
✓Send Marketing template messages through the CRM.
Note: Meta notes that this functionality is currently limited and may not be generally available to all users.

Expected Behavior

When a Marketing template is sent through the CRM:

1

The message is delivered

The recipient receives the message.

2

The message synchronizes

The message appears in the WhatsApp Business app.

3

The conversation stays in place

The conversation remains in its existing position in the Chats list.

4

The conversation moves to the top after a reply

The conversation moves to the top only when the customer replies.

You can still open the conversation by searching for the contact's name or phone number.

Why Does This Happen?

Meta designed this behavior to prevent Marketing messages from automatically moving conversation threads to the top of the WhatsApp Business app's inbox.

This is controlled by Meta. It is not caused by the CRM or by a synchronization failure.

How to Confirm the Expected Behavior

Test the conversation using one of the following message types:

  • A Utility template.
  • A free-form message sent within an active 24-hour customer service window.

After sending the test message, check whether the conversation moves to the top of the WhatsApp Business app's Chats list.

If the Issue Also Occurs With Other Message Types

If a Utility template or an eligible free-form message does not move the conversation to the top, collect the following information:

1.The message ID (wamid).
2.The exact message timestamp, including the time zone.
3.A screenshot of the WhatsApp Business app's Chats list.
4.A screenshot of the opened conversation showing the message.

Send these details to Support so the case can be shared with Meta for further investigation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the Marketing message missing?

No. The message is delivered and synchronized with the WhatsApp Business app. It simply does not move the conversation to the top of the Chats list.

Q: How can I find the conversation?

Search for the contact's name or phone number in the WhatsApp Business app and open the conversation.

Q: When will the conversation move to the top?

For Marketing template messages, the conversation moves to the top after the customer replies.

Q: Do I need to change a setting in the CRM?

No. This behavior is controlled by Meta and does not require a CRM configuration change.

Additional Information

For more information, refer to Meta's documentation on Marketing templates.
