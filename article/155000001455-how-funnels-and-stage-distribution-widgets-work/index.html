How Funnels and Stage distribution widgets work

Dashboards • Opportunities • Pipeline Reporting
Funnel vs. Stages Distribution Widgets in MxP
MxP's Funnel and Stages Distribution widgets provide two different views of opportunity performance. Funnel shows how opportunities progress through sequential pipeline stages, while Stages Distribution shows where open opportunities currently sit. Choosing the right widget helps you separate conversion analysis from day-to-day pipeline monitoring.
What You'll Learn

Learn how Funnel and Stages Distribution calculate opportunity data, how Funnel conversion and revenue work, how to control stage visibility, and how to add either widget to a dashboard.

Important

Funnel is designed for sequential progression. Stages Distribution is designed for current placement. The same opportunity can therefore be represented differently in each widget.

What Are Funnel and Stages Distribution Widgets?

Funnel and Stages Distribution are General dashboard widgets that visualize opportunity data from MxP pipelines.

The Funnel widget shows sequential progression through visible pipeline stages so you can evaluate conversion and drop-off.

The Stages Distribution widget shows where open opportunities currently sit, with Won and Lost opportunities displayed separately.

2

Key Benefits

Using the appropriate widget gives you a clearer view of either opportunity progression or current pipeline health.

  • Track Pipeline Progression: Use Funnel to understand how opportunities advance through sequential stages.
  • Identify Conversion Drop-Offs: Compare Funnel sections to see where fewer opportunities continue forward.
  • Monitor Current Pipeline Distribution: Use Stages Distribution to see where open opportunities currently sit.
  • Separate Outcomes from Active Stages: Review Won and Lost opportunities separately in Stages Distribution.
  • Control Reporting Visibility: Choose which pipeline stages appear in Funnel and Stages Distribution reporting.
  • Switch Between Pipelines: Use the pipeline dropdown in the widget to review different pipelines.
3

Prerequisites and Limitations

Correct pipeline structure, visibility settings, and dashboard access help ensure both widgets display the expected opportunity data.

  • Create at least one pipeline with opportunity stages.
  • Enable the applicable stages for Funnel or pie-chart reporting.
  • Ensure the user has the required Dashboard Stats permission.
  • Ensure the user can access the dashboard containing the widget.
  • Arrange Funnel stages in the order opportunities are expected to progress.

Keep in mind: Won and Lost are opportunity statuses and do not need to be created as pipeline stages. Funnel calculations also use the opportunity's last status change date rather than its Created date when evaluating the selected reporting period.

4

Funnel vs. Stages Distribution

The main difference is whether you want to measure progression through a process or see current placement inside the pipeline.

WidgetBest ForHow Opportunities Are Shown
FunnelSequential conversion analysisLater-stage opportunities also contribute to earlier Funnel sections because they progressed through the process.
Stages DistributionCurrent pipeline monitoringOpen opportunities appear in the individual stage where they currently sit. Won and Lost are shown separately.

Example: If an open opportunity currently sits in Proposal Sent, Stages Distribution shows it under Proposal Sent. Funnel can also count that opportunity toward earlier visible sections because it progressed through those stages.

5

How the Funnel Widget Works

Funnel uses cumulative logic so each section represents opportunities that reached that point or progressed beyond it.

Core Formula: Opportunities in a Funnel section = opportunities from the current visible stage through the final visible stage, including applicable Won opportunities.

As the Funnel moves forward:

  • Opportunities still in earlier stages are removed from later Funnel sections.
  • Opportunities that reached later stages contribute to applicable earlier sections.
  • Won appears as the final successful outcome.
  • Won opportunities contribute to preceding Funnel sections because they completed the process.

Funnel Conversion Rates

Funnel provides two conversion views so you can evaluate both overall progression and stage-to-stage performance.

  • Cumulative Conversion: Shows progression relative to the beginning of the Funnel.
  • Next-Step Conversion: Shows progression relative to the preceding Funnel section.

Funnel Revenue

Revenue follows the same sequential logic as opportunity counts. Opportunity value from later stages and Won opportunities contributes to the applicable earlier Funnel sections.

6

How the Stages Distribution Widget Works

Stages Distribution shows where open opportunities currently sit instead of using cumulative stage progression.

  • Open opportunities are shown in their current visible pipeline stage.
  • Won opportunities are displayed separately.
  • Lost opportunities are displayed separately.
  • The widget also provides conversion information for the displayed distribution.

How To Configure Pipeline Visibility

Pipeline visibility controls determine which stages are included in Funnel and Stages Distribution reporting.

  1. From the Sub-Account View, click Opportunities.
  2. Select Pipelines.
  3. Open the pipeline you want to configure.
  4. Locate the pipeline stages.
  5. Use the funnel icon to control Funnel reporting visibility.
  6. Use the pie-chart icon to control Stages Distribution reporting visibility.
  7. Save the pipeline changes.

Note: Won and Lost are created automatically as opportunity statuses and do not need to be added as pipeline stages.

8

How To Add Funnel or Stages Distribution to a Dashboard

Adding both widgets to the same dashboard lets you compare sequential conversion with current pipeline placement without leaving the Dashboard area.

  1. From the Sub-Account View, open Dashboard.
  2. Open the dashboard you want to modify.
  3. Click Edit Dashboard.
  4. Click + Add Widget.
  5. Locate the General widget category.
  6. Select Funnel or Stages Distribution.
  7. Configure the available widget settings.
  8. Position or resize the widget as needed.
  9. Click Save Changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the main difference between Funnel and Stages Distribution?
Funnel shows sequential opportunity progression. Stages Distribution shows where open opportunities currently sit, with Won and Lost displayed separately.
Q: Why does Funnel count an opportunity in a stage it is no longer in?
Funnel uses cumulative progression logic. An opportunity that reached a later stage contributes to applicable earlier Funnel sections because it progressed through those steps.
Q: Do I need to create Won and Lost pipeline stages?
No. MxP supports Won and Lost as opportunity statuses, so you do not need to create them as pipeline stages.
Q: Which widget is better for stages such as No Show, Waiting on Customer, or Follow-Up Required?
Stages Distribution is generally more useful when you need to see the exact stage where open opportunities currently sit.
Q: Why is a pipeline stage missing from Funnel or Stages Distribution?
Open Opportunities → Pipelines, edit the pipeline, and confirm the applicable Funnel or pie-chart visibility control is enabled for that stage.
Q: Which date does Funnel use for reporting?
Funnel uses the opportunity's last status change date when evaluating the selected reporting period.
Q: Why might these totals differ from another opportunity report?
Different widgets can use different calculations, date properties, filters, visibility settings, and reporting behavior. Compare those settings before comparing totals.
Q: Are more configurable Opportunity widgets available?
Yes. MxP provides dedicated Opportunity widgets with configurable chart types, conditions, pipeline stages, statuses, assignees, attribution data, and other filters.
