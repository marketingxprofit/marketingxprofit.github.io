The Affiliate Commander Program

Affiliates • Commander Status • LevelUp
MxP Affiliate Commander Status
MxP Affiliate Commander Status recognizes top-performing affiliates who reach the annual commission requirement. Commanders receive additional recognition, support, and access within the MxP affiliate community. Qualification resets annually, so affiliates must meet the current requirement during each qualification period.
What You'll Learn

Learn the current Commander commission requirement, annual qualification period, available perks, and how to monitor your affiliate performance.

Important

Commander Status requires at least $100,000 in annual affiliate commissions during the applicable qualification period. It is not a lifetime rank and must be earned again in future annual cycles.

What Is Affiliate Commander Status?

Affiliate Commander is an elite status awarded to top-performing MxP affiliates who generate at least $100,000 in annual commissions. Commander Status is based on annual affiliate performance and is not a permanent or lifetime rank.

2

Key Benefits of Affiliate Commander Status

Commander Status recognizes significant affiliate performance and gives qualified affiliates additional recognition, access, and support.

  • Performance Recognition: Be recognized as one of MxP's top-performing affiliates.
  • Commander Recognition: Receive a MxP Commander jacket with annual badges.
  • Dedicated Slack Access: Gain access to a dedicated channel inside the MxP workspace.
  • Priority Support: Access advanced ticket-escalation protocols for you and your referrals.
  • Annual Recognition: Earn Commander Status through MxP's annual affiliate qualification cycle.
3

Eligibility and Qualification Requirements

Understanding the annual qualification criteria helps affiliates monitor their progress toward Commander Status throughout the year.

To qualify for Affiliate Commander Status, an affiliate must generate at least $100,000 in annual commissions under the MxP Affiliate Program during the applicable qualification period.

  • Requires at least $100,000 in annual commissions.
  • Is based on qualifying affiliate performance during the annual cycle.
  • Is evaluated annually.
  • Is not a lifetime achievement or permanent rank.
  • Must be earned again based on performance during future qualification periods.

Note: Affiliate commissions and eligibility remain subject to MxP's current Affiliate Program Agreement and policies.

4

How the Annual Qualification Period Works

MxP uses an annual qualification period so Commander Status reflects recent affiliate performance instead of lifetime commission totals.

Current Qualification Period

September 1 through August 30

The qualification period resets annually. Affiliates must meet the applicable Commander commission requirement within the relevant annual cycle to qualify.

5

Commander Status Perks

Commander perks provide additional recognition and support for affiliates who reach the annual performance requirement.

  • Commander Jacket: Qualified Commanders receive an exclusive MxP Commander jacket with annual badges.
  • Dedicated Slack Channel: Commanders receive access to a dedicated channel inside the MxP workspace.
  • Priority Ticket Escalation: Commanders receive advanced ticket-escalation protocols for themselves and their referrals.

Program Updates: MxP may update Commander benefits as the affiliate program and annual recognition program evolve.

6

How To Track Your Affiliate Performance

Monitoring your affiliate performance throughout the qualification period helps you track commissions, referrals, and progress toward Commander Status.

  1. From Agency View, click Affiliate Portal in the left navigation menu.
  2. Use the available Affiliate Portal tabs to review performance, referral, commission, and payout information.
  3. Monitor affiliate activity regularly throughout the annual qualification period.
  4. Confirm your payment and affiliate account information remains current.
  5. Contact your Affiliate Manager if you need clarification about your Commander qualification status.

Affiliate Portal: Use the portal to access affiliate reporting, referral information, commissions, payment details, and payout history.

7

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much do I need to earn to become an Affiliate Commander?
The current requirement is at least $100,000 in annual commissions during the applicable qualification period.
Q: What is the annual Commander qualification period?
The current qualification period runs from September 1 through August 30 and resets annually.
Q: Is Affiliate Commander Status permanent?
No. Commander Status is not a lifetime rank. Affiliates must continue meeting the applicable annual qualification requirements.
Q: Does earning $100,000 in lifetime commissions automatically qualify me?
No. Commander qualification is based on commissions earned during the applicable annual qualification period, not lifetime commission totals.
Q: What perks do Affiliate Commanders receive?
Current perks include a MxP Commander jacket with annual badges, dedicated Slack channel access, and priority ticket-escalation support.
Q: Where can I track my affiliate commissions?
From Agency View, open Affiliate Portal to review available referral, performance, commission, and payout information.
Q: Who should I contact about my Commander eligibility?
Contact your MxP Affiliate Manager for questions about your individual qualification status or current program requirements.
