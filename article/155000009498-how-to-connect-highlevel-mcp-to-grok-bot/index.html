How to Connect MxP MCP to Grok Bot

MxP is now available as an official connected app in Grok Bot, allowing you to access CRM information and perform supported actions using natural-language requests. The integration uses OAuth authentication, so you can securely connect your MxP sub-account(s) without manually configuring API tokens. With 600+ operations across 38 CRM domains, you can manage supported CRM activities directly through Grok Bot.



What is MxP MCP for Grok Bot?


MxP MCP connects your CRM to Grok Bot using Model Context Protocol (MCP), allowing the AI assistant to interact with supported MxP services. Once connected, you can retrieve CRM information and perform authorized actions through simple conversational requests.


The integration uses four unified MCP tools to discover and execute operations based on your approved OAuth permissions.

Key Benefits of MxP MCP for Grok Bot


Connecting MxP to Grok Bot makes it easier to access CRM information and complete supported tasks without navigating multiple CRM screens.

  • Native Integration: Connect MxP directly through Grok Bot's Connected Apps directory.

  • Secure OAuth Authentication: Authorize access without manually creating API tokens.

  • Natural-Language Requests: Retrieve information and perform supported CRM actions through conversations.

  • Extensive CRM Coverage: Access 600+ operations across 38 CRM domains.

  • Sub-Account Control: Select the CRM sub-account(s) available for authorization.

  • Permission-Based Access: Control available operations through approved OAuth scopes.

Supported MxP CRM Operations


MxP MCP provides access to supported CRM services through four unified tools. The operations available depend on your approved permissions and connected sub-account.


Examples of supported CRM domains include:

  • Contacts: Search and manage contact information.

  • Opportunities: Retrieve and update opportunity records.

  • Calendars: Access scheduling information.

  • Conversations: Retrieve supported conversation information.

  • Payments: Access supported payment-related operations.


For example, you can ask Grok Bot:

  • "Find the five most recently created contacts."

  • "Show me the open opportunities in my CRM."

  • "Find the contact with the email jane@example.com."


Actual results depend on your authorized permissions and available operations.

How to Set Up MxP MCP in Grok Bot


Connecting MxP to Grok Bot requires selecting the official app, authorizing your CRM account, and approving permissions. Completing these steps ensures Grok Bot can access the intended MxP information.


Step 1: Open Connected Apps

  1. Sign in to Grok Bot.

  2. Navigate to Connected Apps.

  3. Open the app search directory.

Step 2: Search for MxP

  1. Search for MxP.

  2. Select the official MxP app.

  3. Follow the available option to begin connecting.


Step 3: Authorize MxP

  1. Sign in to your MxP account if prompted.

  2. Select the CRM sub-account(s) you want to connect.

  3. Review the requested OAuth permissions.

  4. Approve access to complete authorization.

Note: Multiple sub-account selection may depend on availability for your account.


Step 4: Verify the Connection

  1. Return to Grok Bot.

  2. Confirm MxP appears as connected.

  3. Test the integration with a simple request, such as:

    "Show me the five most recently created contacts in my MxP CRM."

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Do I need an API key or Private Integration Token?

No. The official Grok Bot integration uses OAuth authentication, eliminating the need to manually configure API tokens.


Q: Can I connect multiple MxP sub-accounts?

You can select the sub-account(s) available during authorization. Multi-sub-account support may vary depending on rollout availability.


Q: Can Grok Bot perform all MxP CRM operations?

Grok Bot can perform supported operations based on the OAuth scopes you approve and your applicable account permissions.


Q: Is my CRM data protected?

The connection uses OAuth authorization, and access is restricted by the approved permissions. Review requested scopes before authorizing the integration.


Q: What if Grok Bot cannot access my CRM data?

Check that MxP is connected, the correct sub-account is authorized, and the required permissions have been approved.
