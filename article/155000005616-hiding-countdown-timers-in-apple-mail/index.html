Marketing • Countdown Timers • Email
Hiding Countdown Timers in Apple Mail
Countdown timers in emails are displayed as animated images, and Apple Mail may cache those images instead of refreshing them as expected. The Hide Timer in Apple Mail setting lets you hide the countdown for Apple Mail users so they do not see an outdated or misleading timer.
What You'll Learn

What Is the Hide Timer in Apple Mail Setting?

Hide Timer in Apple Mail prevents the countdown timer from displaying in Apple Mail when image caching could cause the timer to appear outdated. Other supported email clients can continue displaying the countdown normally.

Important: Hiding the countdown timer only changes whether the timer image is displayed in Apple Mail. It does not automatically change the campaign deadline or the destination used by your email calls-to-action.

2

Why Apple Mail Can Show an Outdated Timer

Countdown timers in emails are displayed as animated images. Apple Mail may cache the timer image, which can prevent the countdown from refreshing normally when the same email is opened again. Hiding the timer in Apple Mail avoids showing a cached countdown image that may no longer match the current timer state.

3

How to Hide a Countdown Timer in Apple Mail

Enable this setting when Apple Mail caching could cause the countdown image to display outdated timer information.

  1. Go to Marketing → Countdown Timers.
  2. Open an existing timer or click + Newto create one.
  3. Open the Styling settings.
  4. Scroll to the Apple Mail setting.
  5. Enable Hide Timer in Apple Mail.
  6. Save the timer.
  7. Preview and test the email before sending it to your audience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why can Apple Mail show an outdated countdown timer?
Apple Mail may cache the animated timer image instead of refreshing it normally when the email is opened again. This can cause the displayed countdown to no longer match the timer's current state.
Q: Will the countdown still display in other email clients?
Yes. Hide Timer in Apple Mail is specifically intended to hide the timer for Apple Mail users.
Q: Can I enable this setting on an existing timer?
Yes. Open the timer, enable Hide Timer in Apple Mail in the Styling settings, and save your changes.
Q: Can my email CTA still route contacts based on whether the timer is active or expired?
Yes. Hiding the timer image in Apple Mail does not prevent you from using the timer's Redirection Link on supported email buttons, images, or text links. The Redirection Link can route contacts to the configured Active or Expired destination based on the timer state.
