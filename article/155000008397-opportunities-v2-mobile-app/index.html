What is Opportunities V2 Mobile App?

Opportunities V2 is MxP's redesigned mobile lead management interface that provides personalized card layouts and integrated functionality for sales teams on the go. This update consolidates opportunity details, quick actions, and contextual information into a unified workspace, reducing the need to navigate through multiple screens or tabs.

The mobile experience allows you to customize which fields appear on opportunity cards, execute immediate actions like calling or messaging, and access comprehensive lead information—all from a single, intuitive interface designed to accelerate your sales process.

Customizable Opportunity Cards

Opportunity cards can be tailored to display only the fields and quick actions that matter most to your workflow. Choose from over 40 available fields and configure up to 7 custom fields per card, ensuring your pipeline shows exactly what you need at a glance.

Default Fields
Standard Card Layout

Each opportunity card displays Name, Value, and Owner on the top line by default. You can select up to 4 additional custom fields from options including Source, Business, Lost Reason, and 37+ other available fields.

Quick Actions
Configure Card-Level Actions

Default quick actions include Message, Add Tag, Add Task, and Move Opportunity. You can customize which actions appear on your cards to match your team's most frequent workflows.

Pro Tip

Customize your card layout to prioritize the fields your team checks most frequently, reducing the time spent opening detailed views for routine information.

Redesigned Pipelines

The updated pipeline interface provides a modern, streamlined view of your opportunity stages with interactive cards that support direct actions. All critical lead management functions are accessible without leaving the pipeline view, eliminating context switching and reducing friction in your workflow.

Interactive Cards
Direct Actions from Pipeline View

Take immediate action on opportunities including calling, adding notes, creating tasks, applying tags, and changing stages—all directly from the card without opening detailed views.

Activity Badges
Visual Status Indicators

Active badges display counters for conversations, notes, tasks, and tags on each opportunity card, providing instant visibility into lead activity without opening the full record.

All-in-One Opportunity View

The detailed opportunity view consolidates all lead information into a single screen with tabbed navigation for different information types. This unified interface eliminates the need to navigate between separate screens to view call logs, messages, tasks, appointments, or transaction history.

Quick Actions Bar
Immediate Engagement Options

Access top quick actions including call, message, and tag directly from the opportunity detail view for instant lead engagement without leaving the screen.

Integrated Tabs
Consolidated Information Access

Switch between Details, Tasks, Notes, Appointments, and Transactions tabs to view and manage every aspect of the opportunity in one unified interface. Edit information, add updates, and track activity seamlessly without changing screens.

Pro Tip

Watch for counters on conversations, notes, tasks, and tags in the tab bar—these indicators help you identify which areas need attention without opening each tab individually.

How to Set Up Opportunities V2

Setting up the enhanced mobile opportunity experience requires customizing your card layout and familiarizing yourself with the updated pipeline interface. Follow these steps to configure your workflow for maximum efficiency.

Customize Your Opportunity Cards
Step 1
Access Card Customization Settings

Open the mobile app and navigate to Opportunities → Settings → Customize Cards.

Step 2
Select Fields and Quick Actions

Choose from 40+ available fields and select the quick actions you want to appear on your opportunity cards.

Step 3
Activate Your Configuration

Select up to 7 fields (including the 3 default fields: Name, Value, and Owner) and choose your preferred quick actions.

Step 4
Save and Apply Changes

Tap Save. Your personalized opportunity cards will refresh instantly in your pipeline view.

Navigate the Updated Pipelines
Step 1
Open Pipeline View

In the mobile app, navigate to Opportunities → Pipelines to view your updated stages.

Step 2
Interact with Customized Cards

Explore the updated stages and interact with customized cards. Execute quick actions such as calling, adding notes or tasks, tagging, and changing stages directly from each card.

Use the All-in-One Detail View
Step 1
Open Opportunity Details

Tap any opportunity card within your pipeline to open the detailed view.

Step 2
Execute Quick Actions

Use the top quick actions for immediate engagement, including call, message, and tag functions.

Step 3
Navigate Information Tabs

Switch between tabs—Details, Tasks, Notes, Appointments, and Transactions—to manage every aspect of the opportunity in one unified screen. Edit information, add updates, and track activity without leaving the detail view.

Note

This update will reset your current mobile card layout and fields to the default configuration. You will need to reconfigure your preferences using the customization settings after updating the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When is this update available?
The Opportunities V2 mobile app update is live. Update your MxP mobile app to access the enhanced features.
Q: Will this update reset my current mobile card layout?
Yes, the update will reset your current mobile card layout and fields to the default configuration. You will need to reconfigure your custom fields and quick actions through the customization settings.
Q: What are the default fields on opportunity cards?
Default fields include Name, Value, Owner, Source, Business, and Lost Reason. Name, Value, and Owner always appear on the top line of each card when selected.
Q: How many custom fields can I display on each opportunity card?
You can display up to 4 custom fields on each card. Additionally, 3 bonus fields—Name, Value, and Owner—always appear on the top line by default when selected.
Q: What default quick actions are available on cards?
Default quick actions include Message, Add Tag, Add Task, and Move Opportunity. You can customize which actions appear on your cards through the settings.
Q: Will these customization changes affect how opportunities appear on desktop?
No, these customization changes are exclusive to the mobile app. The desktop view remains unchanged.
Q: Do changes to mobile customization affect the web layout?
No, changes made on the mobile app apply only to the mobile layout. The web layout continues to work according to your existing web configuration. Customizations are platform-specific.
Q: Is the Opportunities V2 experience available only on mobile?
Yes, the updated customization and layout enhancements described in this article apply exclusively to the mobile app. Similar functionality is already available on the web platform and continues to function according to your existing web configuration.
