Social Post on TikTok

Create, schedule, and publish TikTok videos, single-image posts, and photo carousels directly from MxP's Social Planner. Add captions, configure TikTok-specific settings, and choose when to publish without switching to TikTok.

What is TikTok Posting in Social Planner?


TikTok posting in Social Planner lets you publish videos and photo posts to connected TikTok Personal and Business accounts. You can upload a single image, create a photo carousel with up to 35 images, or publish a video. Configure your post settings, review the preview, and publish immediately or use an existing scheduling workflow.

Key Benefits of TikTok Posting


  • Efficiency: Say goodbye to manual push notifications and save time in content distribution.

  • Consistent Scheduling: Plan ahead and ensure a well-timed presence on TikTok without daily manual intervention.

  • Enhanced Planning: Strategize your content effectively, considering optimal posting times and frequency for better audience engagement.

  • Better Analytics: Track post performance and optimize your content strategy over time.

  • Multi-Platform Integration: Streamline content distribution across multiple social media accounts effortlessly.

How to Post on TikTok from the Social Planner


Social Planner supports single-image TikTok photo posts and carousels containing up to 35 images. You can add an optional title and let TikTok automatically select recommended background music.


  1. Navigate to Marketing → Social Planner.

  2. Click + New Post → Create New Post.



  3. Under Post To, select your connected TikTok Personal or Business account.

  4. Upload a single image or multiple images to create a photo carousel. You can add up to 35 images per post.

  5. Enter your caption in the post composer.



  6. Under TikTok options, enter an optional title in Add a title. Titles support up to 90 characters.

  7. Enable Add recommended music from TikTok if you want TikTok to select and add background music automatically.

  8. Review your post in Post Preview.

  9. Click Post and choose your preferred publishing option.



You can publish immediately, schedule a post, create a recurring post, submit a post for approval, or add it to a category queue. TikTok photo posts are also supported through CSV bulk uploads.




Add Recommended Music to TikTok Photo Posts


The Add recommended music from TikTok option lets TikTok automatically select background music for your photo post.


  1. Create a TikTok photo post in Social Planner.

  2. Expand TikTok options.

  3. Enable Add recommended music from TikTok.

  4. Review your post and proceed with publishing.


TikTok selects the recommended track during publishing. You do not need to select a track manually.


Note: This option is available for TikTok photo posts only.



TikTok Photo Post Requirements


Before publishing TikTok photo posts, review the following requirements:

Media Optimization for TikTok Photo Posts


Social Planner can automatically prepare uploaded images for TikTok when Media Optimization is enabled.


When enabled, Media Optimization:


  • Converts unsupported image formats, including PNG, into supported JPEG or WebP formats.

  • Resizes portrait images to a maximum of 1080 × 1920 pixels.

  • Resizes landscape images to a maximum of 1920 × 1080 pixels.


When Media Optimization is disabled, upload images in JPEG, JPG, or WebP format.



Publishing TikTok Photo Posts with Existing Workflows


TikTok photo posts work with Social Planner's existing publishing workflows, including:


  • Post Now: Publish photo posts immediately.

  • Scheduled Posts: Publish at a selected date and time.

  • Recurring Posts: Republish content using a recurring schedule.

  • Approval Flow: Submit photo posts for review before publishing.

  • CSV Bulk Upload: Create and schedule photo posts in bulk.

  • Category Queue: Organize photo posts in your content queues.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can I edit a scheduled TikTok post?
 You can reschedule time and update certain fields. If the media or requirements change, create a new post to avoid processing errors.


Q: Can I manage TikTok comments inside Social Planner?
Not at this time in Social Planner. Handle comments directly on TikTok or via MxP conversation/automation features (where available). You can setup TikTok comment automations. Click here for more info on TikTok DMs & Comment Automations.


Q: Why did my scheduled post fail?
The most common causes are a disconnected TikTok profile, expired permissions, or video specs not being met. Reconnect or re-export the file and reschedule.
