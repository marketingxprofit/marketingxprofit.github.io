This article explains what the Wallet Pricing page includes, how to access it, and how to use it to understand product costs across different markets.

What is the Wallet Pricing Page?

The Wallet Pricing page is a dedicated pricing reference inside the Billing section that displays pay-as-you-go rates for every product that uses wallet billing. This includes products across multiple categories such as AI, Communication, WhatsApp, Domain, and more.

Prior to this feature, agencies needed to search across multiple pages or contact support to understand product costs. The Wallet Pricing page consolidates all pricing information into a single, searchable view with organized categories, making it easy to find rates and understand what you're being charged for.

This is phase one of MxP's Billing Transparency initiative, designed to eliminate confusion around wallet charges and provide agencies with a clear reference for reconciling invoices, planning pricing strategies, and explaining costs to clients.

2

Key Features

The Wallet Pricing page includes several capabilities designed to help you quickly locate and understand product costs:

Feature 1
Full Product Catalog With Pricing

Every wallet-billed product is listed with unit costs, grouped by category and sub-category. This makes it easy to scan what you're being charged for without searching across multiple pages. All rates are kept up to date automatically.

Feature 2
Search and Filter

Use the search bar to find products by category or product name instantly. You can expand or collapse categories to focus on the section that matters to you, making navigation faster when you need to reference a specific product.

Feature 3
Country-Specific Rates

Switch between 27 supported countries to see localized pricing. Products like WhatsApp messages and calls display country-specific rates so you always know what applies to your market. Additional countries will be added over time.

Feature 4
Product Detail on Demand

Expand any product row to see a full breakdown including category, SKU, description, pricing, billing unit, and country rates. This gives you everything you need to understand exactly how a product is billed without leaving the page.

Feature 5
Enterprise Pricing Included

Enterprise product pricing is covered alongside standard rates. Agencies on enterprise plans have full visibility into what applies to them, ensuring consistent pricing transparency across all account types.

3

Why This Matters

Wallet billing has traditionally lacked a centralized reference for product costs. Agencies were charged for products without an easy way to verify rates, leading to confusion when reconciling invoices or explaining charges to clients.

The Wallet Pricing page eliminates this issue by providing a single source of truth for every wallet product rate. Whether you're reconciling an invoice, planning your pricing strategy, or explaining costs to a client, you have immediate access to accurate, up-to-date pricing information.

This is the foundation of MxP's Billing Transparency initiative. Future phases will include sub-account pricing with markup controls and a pricing calculator to further enhance billing visibility and control.

How to Access and Use the Wallet Pricing Page

Follow these steps to enable and begin using the Wallet Pricing page:

Step 1
Enable the Feature from Labs

Navigate to Settings → Labs in your MxP account. Locate the Wallet Pricing feature and enable it. This will activate the Pricing page in your Billing section.

Step 2
Navigate to the Pricing Page

Go to Billing in the main navigation. Under Wallet and Transactions, you will see the new Pricing page. Click to open it.

Step 3
Browse Products by Category

Products are organized by category and sub-category. Expand or collapse categories to focus on specific product areas. All products within a category display their unit costs and billing units.

Step 4
Use Search to Find Specific Products

Use the search bar at the top of the page to search by category name or product name. The page will filter results in real time as you type, making it faster to locate the product you need.

Step 5
Switch the Country Selector

Use the country selector dropdown to switch between the 27 supported countries. Pricing will update automatically to reflect the rates applicable to the selected market. This is especially useful for products like WhatsApp messages and calls, which have country-specific rates.

Step 6
Expand Product Rows for Full Details

Click on any product row to expand it and view the full breakdown. This includes the product category, SKU, description, pricing, billing unit, and country-specific rates where applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to enable this feature from Labs?
Yes, the Wallet Pricing page is currently a Labs feature. You must enable it from Settings → Labs before it appears in your Billing section. Once enabled, it will be available under Wallet and Transactions.
Q: Are the prices on this page updated automatically?
Yes, all pricing displayed on the Wallet Pricing page is kept up to date automatically. You do not need to refresh the page manually to see current rates.
Q: Does this page show pricing for sub-accounts?
Currently, the Wallet Pricing page displays agency-level pricing. In future phases, you will be able to extend this to sub-accounts with markup controls. This functionality is part of the roadmap for the Billing Transparency initiative.
Q: Can I export the pricing data from this page?
Export functionality is not available in this phase. Future updates may include the ability to export pricing data as part of enhanced reporting capabilities.
Q: What if my country is not listed in the country selector?
If your country is not currently supported, the page will display default pricing. Additional countries will be added over time. Check back for updates as the feature expands to more markets.
Q: Does this page include enterprise pricing?
Yes, enterprise product pricing is included alongside standard rates. Agencies on enterprise plans can view the pricing that applies to them directly on this page.
Q: Will there be a pricing calculator in the future?
Yes, a pricing calculator is on the roadmap as part of future phases of the Billing Transparency initiative. This will allow you to estimate costs based on projected usage before charges are incurred.
Q: Where can I find more information about wallet billing?
For more information about wallet billing, how charges are calculated, and how to top up your wallet, visit the Wallet and Billing section of the MxP Help Center or contact support.
