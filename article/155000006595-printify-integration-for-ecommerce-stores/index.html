Printify Integration for Ecommerce Stores



Skip the inventory hassle and still sell branded merch. Connect your MxP store to Printify’s catalog of 1,000+ products and 85+ global print providers, and every order is made on demand—no boxes, no bulk buys.

What is Printify Integration?


Printify Integration connects your MxP Ecommerce store to Printify’s print-on-demand (POD) platform. Once connected, you can import products you designed in Printify, automatically forward customer orders for production, and receive real-time fulfillment updates—so you can sell custom goods with zero upfront stock risk. Print-on-demand lets items be printed only after a sale is made, freeing you from inventory costs and bulk-order commitments. 

Key Benefits of Printify Integration


  • Fast launches: Access 1,000+ customizable products to spin up new lines quickly.

  • Regional speed & pricing: Tap 85+ print providers for competitive rates and quicker local delivery.

  • Always accurate listings: Variants, prices, and images sync automatically.

  • Hands-off fulfillment: Orders auto-push to Printify and go to production—no manual re-entry.

  • End-to-end tracking: Get automatic partial and complete fulfillment updates.

  • Multi-store control: Manage multiple storefronts from one Printify account and view performance by location.

Important Notes & Limitations


  • Imported products publish live by default—double-check before you launch sales.

  • Uninstalling the integration does NOT delete previously imported products.

  • Printify only creates an order if a print provider can fulfill the destination country, variant, and shipping method you selected.
     
  • All design work happens inside Printify; MxP only manages inventory and orders for already-synced items.

  • You’ll need a Printify API token to authenticate the connection.

How To Generate Printify API Token


  1. Log in to Printify and click on Account.

  2. Click on Connections.

  3. Click on the Generate button under API Tokens.



  4. Name the token, select scopes, and click on Generate.



  5. Copy the key by clicking on Copy to Clipboard.

How To Connect Printify with MxP


Connecting takes just a few minutes, correct setup ensures orders flow smoothly:


  1. In your sub-account, open App Marketplace.



  2. Search Printify and click on the Install button.



  3. Paste your Printify API token in the Printify Token box and click on Continue.



  4. Choose an existing Printify store or create a new one. Under Convert Product Currency to, select the currency you want to use for imported Printify product prices, then click Connect.

  5. Import products and publish them directly into your store.

  6. Start selling — orders will sync automatically to Printify for fulfillment. Enable the automatic fulfilment in Printify.

Shipping Profiles and Live Rates at Checkout


Printify uses a dedicated shipping profile to provide live shipping options for synced products at checkout. Keeping Printify products assigned to this profile ensures eligible live Printify shipping rates remain available to customers during checkout.


When Printify products are synced, MxP automatically creates the required Printify shipping profile.


  • Live Printify shipping rates are shown at checkout for products assigned to the Printify shipping profile.

  • Products must remain assigned to the Printify shipping profile to receive live shipping rates at checkout.


Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can I design products inside MxP?

No, design and mockups are created in Printify. MxP mirrors what you import.


Q: What happens if I delete a product in Printify?
It’s disabled in your MxP catalog on the next sync.


Q: Does Printify automatically choose the closest print provider?

If your chosen provider has multiple facilities, Printify routes to the nearest one.


Q: Can I manually fulfill an order instead of using Printify?

Yes. Disable automatic fulfillment and fulfill in Printify; MxP will still receive status updates.


Q: Is there an extra fee from MxP for using Printify Integration?

No, fees come only from Printify for production and shipping.


Q: How do refunds work?

Process the refund in MxP; a refund request is sent to Printify for any unshipped items.


Q: What if a customer’s address isn’t serviceable by my provider?

Printify rejects the order. MxP shows “Unfulfilled – No provider available.” Edit the order and choose a deliverable variant/provider.


Q: Do product changes in Printify sync to MxP?
Yes, descriptions, mockups, variants, prices, and images sync automatically.


Q: Are orders created in Printify automatically?

Yes, each checkout in MxP creates a matching Printify order.


Q: Are taxes and shipping preferences passed through?

Yes, tax and shipping details are included in the synced order.


Q: Do I need to re-enter order details manually?

No, automation removes double entry and speeds fulfillment.


Q: Are shipping and production statuses synced back?

Yes, Printify pushes real-time updates (in production, shipped, delivered).


Q: Are partial shipments supported?

Yes, line-item tracking and status updates sync as items ship.


Q: Can one Printify account connect to multiple stores?

Yes, connect one Printify account to multiple MxP store locations.


Q: Will orders stay separated by location?

Yes, orders and metrics are grouped per store while product creation/billing stay centralized.
