How to View and Use Dashboards in the MxP Mobile App

Mobile Dashboards bring your existing MxP web dashboards to your mobile app, allowing you to monitor business performance wherever you are. View key metrics, apply filters, explore interactive charts, and generate AI-powered insights without opening your desktop.

What Are Mobile Dashboards?


Mobile Dashboards allow you to access your existing MxP web dashboards directly from the mobile app.

A dashboard is a collection of charts and numbers that helps you monitor business activities such as appointments, conversations, payments, opportunities, calls, and social media performance.


The new Dashboards experience replaces the previous Insights module, giving you access to your available web dashboards and supported widgets from your phone.


When you open Dashboards, your default dashboard loads automatically. You can switch dashboards, apply filters, explore charts, and review business performance without accessing the desktop application.


Note: Mobile Dashboards are designed for viewing and analyzing data. Creating or editing dashboards and widgets is not supported in this initial release.

Availability and Requirements


Mobile Dashboards were introduced in mobile app version v4.14.1 (823).

The initial release supports:

  • MxP Mobile App

  • MxP Mobile App

  • Supported White-Label Apps, depending on configuration and rollout

The feature was initially introduced through Labs with a phased rollout.


Important: If Dashboards is already available in your app, you may not need to enable it through Labs. Availability depends on your app version and account configuration.


How to Enable and Access Mobile Dashboards


Mobile Dashboards are available from the Apps section of your MxP mobile app.


Step 1: Update Your Mobile App

  1. Open the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

  2. Search for your MxP or MxP app.

  3. Update the app to a version that supports Mobile Dashboards.


Step 2: Enable Mobile Dashboards in Labs (If Required)

  1. Open your mobile app.

  2. Navigate to Labs.

  3. Find Mobile Dashboards.

  4. Turn on the toggle.

If Dashboards is already available, you can skip this step.


Step 3: Open Dashboards

  1. Open your mobile app and sign in.

  2. Navigate to the Apps section.

  3. Tap Dashboards.

Your default dashboard will load automatically.

You can now view your existing dashboards and supported widgets without creating separate mobile dashboards.

How to Switch Dashboards and Apply Filters


Mobile Dashboards allow you to switch between available dashboards and filter the information displayed.


How to Switch Dashboards

  1. Open Dashboards in your mobile app.

  2. Tap the dashboard name or dropdown arrow at the top.

  3. Select the dashboard you want to view.

The selected dashboard will open with its available widgets.


How to Change the Date Range

  1. Tap the calendar icon at the top of the dashboard.

  2. Select your preferred reporting period.

  3. Apply the date range.

For example, choose Last 30 Days to review recent business performance.


How to Apply Quick Filters

Quick Filters help you focus on specific information within a dashboard.

  1. Open a dashboard with available filters.

  2. Tap a filter, such as User, Calendar, or Group.

  3. Select your preferred option.

Compatible widgets will update based on the selected filters.

Note: Quick Filters only affect compatible dashboard widgets.

How to Explore and Interact with Dashboard Widgets


Dashboard widgets display individual business metrics using numbers and charts.

Mobile Dashboards support the following widget types:

  • Numeric: Displays key metrics such as appointment counts or revenue.

  • Line: Shows how values change over time.

  • Donut: Displays how a total is divided into categories.

  • Vertical Bar: Compares values using vertical bars.

  • Horizontal Bar: Compares values using horizontal bars.

  • Supported System Widgets: Displays additional built-in dashboard information.

How to View Dashboard Widgets

Open a dashboard and scroll through the available widgets.

For example:

  • An Appointments dashboard can display booking counts and appointment statuses.

  • An Opportunities dashboard can display pipeline stages and conversion information.

  • A Social Planner dashboard can display post counts and publishing trends.



How to Expand a Widget

You can expand supported widgets for a closer look at the data.

  1. Open your dashboard.

  2. Locate the widget you want to explore.

  3. Tap the Expand icon in the upper-right corner.

  4. Review the chart in its expanded view.

  5. Tap X to close the expanded view.


How to Interact with Charts

Interactive charts allow you to explore individual values.

  1. Open a supported chart.

  2. Tap a bar, select a donut chart segment, or move your finger across a line chart.

  3. Review the displayed tooltip showing the selected value, date, or category.

For example, tapping a revenue chart data point can display the revenue recorded for a particular date.


How to Refresh Dashboard Data

Tap the Refresh icon in the dashboard header to request updated information.

Dashboard refresh handling also helps keep mobile data aligned with the web experience.


How to View Dashboards in Dark Mode

Mobile Dashboards support both light and dark themes.

When Dark Mode is enabled in your mobile app, dashboard backgrounds, charts, and text adjust to the selected theme.


How to Use AI Insights

AI Insights help you understand information displayed in supported dashboard widgets.

Instead of manually analyzing every chart, you can generate a plain-language summary highlighting important metrics and trends.


Step 1: Select a Widget

  1. Open a dashboard.

  2. Locate a supported widget.

  3. Look for the AI sparkle icon.

Step 2: Generate AI Insights

  1. Tap the AI sparkle icon.

  2. Review the generated summary.

  3. Use the information to understand performance changes or trends.

For example, a Calls dashboard may show a summary of total calls placed, changes from the previous period, and team activity.

Step 3: Review the Insight

Depending on the available controls, you can:

  • Provide feedback using the thumbs-up or thumbs-down options.

  • Regenerate the insight.

  • Copy the generated summary.

Note: AI insights are available for supported individual widgets. Dashboard-level AI summaries are not included in this initial release.


Important Limitations


Mobile Dashboards provide access to existing web dashboards, but some features are not supported in the initial 

mobile release.


View-Only Access

You can view dashboards and interact with supported widgets, but you cannot create, edit, or rearrange dashboards and widgets from the mobile app.

Use MxP on the web to make these changes.


Unsupported Widgets

Some web dashboard widgets may not be supported on mobile. Unsupported widgets display a placeholder message instead of an interactive chart.


AI Insights

AI-powered insights are supported at the individual widget level. Full dashboard-level AI summaries are not available in this release.


Campaigns Module

Campaign data, previously accessible through Insights, is now available through a separate Campaigns module in the mobile app.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: What happened to Insights in the mobile app?

The previous Insights experience has been replaced by Dashboards, providing access to your existing web dashboards and supported widgets.


Q: Do I need to create separate dashboards for mobile?

No. Your existing web dashboards are available in the mobile app, and your default dashboard loads automatically.


Q: Can I switch between different dashboards?

Yes. Tap the dashboard name or dropdown arrow at the top of the screen to select another available dashboard.


Q: Can I apply filters and change date ranges?

Yes. You can choose reporting periods and use available Quick Filters to focus on specific information.


Q: Can I expand dashboard widgets?

Yes. Tap the Expand icon on a supported widget to view its chart in a larger format.


Q: Can I edit dashboards from my phone?

No. Mobile Dashboards are view-only in this release. Use the MxP web application to create or edit dashboards.


Q: Can I use AI to analyze dashboard data?

Yes. Supported widgets include AI-powered insights that provide plain-language summaries of business metrics.


Q: Why can't I see Mobile Dashboards?

Ensure your app is updated, check whether Labs activation is required, and verify your account permissions and feature availability.


Q: Does Mobile Dashboards support Dark Mode?

Yes. Mobile Dashboards support light and dark themes.
