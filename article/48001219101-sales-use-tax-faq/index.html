Sales & Use Tax FAQ

Applies only to customers with a US shipping or billing address.  If you are a NON US-based customer, see the  VAT/GST/HST FAQs instead.

1. Why is MxP charging me sales tax?

In accordance with sales tax regulations, MxP is legally required to collect state and local tax on taxable purchases made by customers in certain locations. Whether or not tax is due depends on the buyer's location and whether the product purchased is taxable in that location.


2. Is tax included in the price of my subscription?

No. Sales tax is calculated and added on top of your subscription price at the time of invoicing. The tax amount will vary depending on the applicable tax rate for your location and whether the specific product purchased is taxable.


3. When does MxP charge tax and how is usage-based sales tax billed?

For subscription orders, tax is charged at the time your invoice is issued.

For wallet products, no tax is assessed at the time the wallet is funded or loaded. Instead, taxable usage is totaled at the end of the month, tax is calculated on that amount, and the charge appears between the 1st and 10th of the following month. By using wallet products, you authorize MxP to charge this tax to your wallet or payment method on file. 


4. Why am I being charged tax now when I wasn’t before?

Tax laws change over time and MxP automatically updates its billing processes to reflect those changes. If you recently updated your shipping or billing address, that change may have placed you in a new tax jurisdiction. 


5. Why are intangible (digital) products taxed?

Many states require tax on digital products and software-as-a-service (SaaS). Some of MxP’s products fall under these rules, which is why you may see sales tax on your invoice even for a digital purchase.


6. Which address is used to determine my sales tax?

We use your shipping address first. If no shipping address is on file, we fall back to your billing address. If a billing address is also unavailable, we use the billing details associated with your payment method.


7. How do I update my shipping and/or billing address?

You can update your shipping and/or billing address in the Account Billing Dashboard. Keeping your address current ensures the correct tax rate is applied to your account.


8. Why did my tax amount change?

Your tax amount can change for a few reasons: the tax rate in your state or locality was updated, the address on your account changed (which may put you in a different tax jurisdiction), or the taxability rules for a specific MxP product changed. If you see an unexpected change and can’t identify the cause, contact support for clarification.


9. Where can I find my tax invoices or receipts?

Tax charges appear as line items on your invoices. For details on where to find your invoices, please refer to the Account Billing Dashboard article.


10. How do I submit my tax exemption certificate?

If your company is tax-exempt or holds a Direct Pay Permit or reseller certificate, submit a support ticket to update your account. Include a valid, fully completed exemption certificate, resale certificate, or Direct Pay Permit accepted by your state, listing MxP LLC as the seller. MxP will continue to charge sales tax until it has reviewed and accepted your documentation. Please send updated documentation if your information changes or your certificate expires.


MxP cannot advise on whether your company qualifies for exemption from sales tax. Please consult your tax advisor to determine what’s appropriate for your situation.


11. Do I need to enter my US tax ID number in my billing profile?

No. There is no need to provide your Federal tax ID number (FEIN) unless you’d like to. Providing your FEIN does not change how MxP calculates sales tax on your invoices. Please note, a FEIN cannot be used to claim exemption from sales tax.


12. Can I get a refund of sales tax?

Sales tax is calculated based on the billing profile information at the time of invoicing. For this reason, retroactive refunds of sales tax are generally not refunded because of address or exemption updates made after invoicing. Please ensure your billing profile is kept up to date, including all address information and exemption documentation. If you believe you were charged tax in error, please submit a support ticket and we will review your situation.

13. My business is in a US territory — am I subject to sales tax?

Some territories have their own local tax regimes. MxP cannot provide tax advice. Please consult your tax advisor to understand your specific obligations.


14. I’m outside the US — does any of this apply to me?

No. MxP collects US sales tax only from customers with a billing or shipping address in the United States. If your account address is outside the US, MxP will not collect US sales tax on your invoices. Other taxes (such as VAT or GST) may apply depending on your country, and MxP collects those taxes where  required by local law. See our VAT & GST FAQ or consult your local tax advisor for guidance.


15. Who can I contact with more questions?

For questions about whether a specific product is taxable, which states apply, or your exemption status, consult your company’s tax advisor. For account-specific questions, please submit a support ticket. This article is general information and is not tax advice. If MxP does not collect sales tax on a taxable purchase, you may be responsible for paying use tax directly to your state.
