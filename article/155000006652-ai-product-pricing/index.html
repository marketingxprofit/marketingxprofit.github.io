AI Product Pricing

MxP offers AI tools to help businesses create content, build websites, automate workflows, manage customer conversations, and more. Many AI assistants are included with MxP agency plans at no additional cost with fair-usage protections, while other AI products require an AI Employee subscription or are billed based on usage.

This article explains which AI features are included with MxP, compares AI Employee plans, provides detailed pricing for individual AI products, and explains how AI usage and billing work.


Always use the product-specific section below before quoting pricing, estimating usage, or setting up rebilling.


Important: Pricing and model availability may change. Third-party model/provider rates may also change based on provider updates, model version, service tier, or MxP implementation. When quoting pricing externally, confirm the current in-app pricing and billing settings first.

AI Features Included with MxP


For agency users on all MxP agency plans ($97, $297, and $497), AI products fall into two main categories:


  • AI Assistants (Included for Agency Users): Tools that help users create, build, and manage their business, such as Ask AI and AI Studio. These are included at no additional cost, subject to fair-use limits.

  • AI Agents (Paid): Tools that perform ongoing tasks on behalf of a business, such as handling customer conversations, answering calls, and managing reviews. These remain paid products and may be billed through subscriptions or pay-per-use pricing.


Sub-account users have different AI pricing. Some AI assistants are available at no additional cost, while others, including Ask AI, AI Studio, and Content AI, are billed based on usage unless the sub-account has an AI Employee subscription. Pay-Per-Use


Pay-per-use pricing applies to supported AI products based on the user's access type, subscription, and included usage allowances.


  • Agency users: Supported AI Assistants are included with agency plans at no additional cost, subject to fair-use limits. Additional charges may apply when included allowances are exhausted and Extra Usage is enabled.

  • Sub-account users: Supported AI products are billed at pay-per-use rates when they are not covered by an AI Employee subscription or when included allowances are exceeded and additional usage is enabled.


Charges vary by product and may be calculated using tokens, generated content, minutes, or other usage-based units. For exact rates and billing calculations, see the product-specific sections below.

How Conversation AI Cost Is Calculated


Conversation AI pricing is based on token usage. Tokens are small units of text used to measure how much information is sent to the AI model and how much text the AI generates in response.


Conversation AI usage has two parts:


Token typeWhat it meansExamples
Input tokensText sent to the AI modelCustomer messages, conversation history, AI instructions, contact details, and knowledge-base content
Output tokensText generated by the AI modelAI replies, suggested responses, and generated messages


To estimate Conversation AI cost, calculate the input token cost and output token cost separately, then add them together.


StepWhat to calculateFormula
1Input token costInput tokens ÷ 1,000,000 × input token price
2Output token costOutput tokens ÷ 1,000,000 × output token price
3Total estimated costInput token cost + output token cost


Example: If a conversation uses 100,000 input tokens and 25,000 output tokens on a model priced at $1.25 per 1M input tokens and $10.00 per 1M output tokens, the estimated cost would be $0.375.

Why Two Conversation AI Conversations May Cost Different Amounts


Two Conversation AI conversations can have different costs because pricing is based on token usage.

A conversation with short messages and short AI replies will usually use fewer tokens. A conversation with more back-and-forth messages, longer AI replies, more conversation history, or larger knowledge-base content will usually use more tokens.


Because of this, two conversations using the same AI model can still have different costs depending on how much text is processed and generated.

Speech-to-Speech Cost Calculations


Why use Speech-to-Speech Voice AI?


Speech-to-Speech Voice AI lets your agent hear audio and respond with audio through a single realtime model. Traditional Voice AI converts speech to text, processes it, then converts text back to speech — a multi-step pipeline that adds latency at every hop. S2S removes those steps entirely.


The difference shows up in the conversation itself. Your agents pick up on tone and emotion that plain text loses, respond with noticeably lower latency, and can adapt when a caller switches languages mid-call. The result sounds far closer to talking with a person than to querying a system.


All-in-one per-minute rate


We've bundled audio, text, cache tokens, transcripts, and infrastructure into a single, predictable per-minute price.
With most Voice AI models, cost swings based on how the conversation goes. S2S gives you one rate for every minute — which makes it far easier to understand your costs, price confidently for clients, and scale without surprises.


Note: Agent prompts are capped at 15K tokens on both models. This applies to your agent's instructions, not to call length or conversation history.

How Does the 5 Hour Window Work?


Ask AI, AI Studio, and Creator Studio use separate five-hour usage windows to manage included AI usage. Each product has its own allowance based on the user's access type and AI Employee subscription.

Here's how the usage windows work:


  • When it starts: A window begins when the first action is performed in the applicable AI product, rather than at a fixed time of day.

  • Rolling windows: Each five-hour window is anchored to the first action. Once the window ends, a new window begins with the next action.

  • Usage resets: The full included allowance becomes available again five hours after the window begins.

  • Unused usage: Remaining usage does not carry over into the next window.

  • Separate allowances: Ask AI, AI Studio, and Creator Studio each have their own usage allowance and five-hour window. Usage in one product does not reduce the allowance for another.


When an included allowance is exhausted, additional usage is paused by default. To continue using supported AI products at applicable pay-per-use rates, enable Extra Usage in AI Suite.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: What happens when I reach my included AI Employee usage limit?

By default, additional usage is paused when your included allowance is exhausted. To continue using supported AI products beyond the included allowance, enable Extra Usage in AI Suite. Additional usage is billed at the applicable pay-per-use rates.


Q: What does "Usage Included" mean for Ask AI, AI Studio, and Creator Studio?

"Usage Included" means your AI Employee subscription provides an allowance for Ask AI, AI Studio, and Creator Studio at no additional usage charge. AI Employee Starter ($20/month) includes a base usage allowance, Growth ($50/month) provides 2x included usage, and Unlimited ($97/month) provides 5x included usage.


Each product has its own five-hour usage window. When an included allowance is exhausted, additional usage is paused by default unless Extra Usage is enabled in AI Suite. Additional usage is billed at applicable pay-per-use rates.


Q: What is "At token cost" on Pay per use?

"At token cost" means you are billed based on your actual AI usage with no monthly fee. Every action consumes a certain number of tokens, and you are charged based on the volume of tokens processed.


Q: Does the 100 Voice AI minutes on AI Employee Growth cover inbound, outbound, and widget calls?

Yes. The 100 AI Agent minutes on AI Employee Growth applies across all Voice AI usage types - inbound phone calls, outbound phone calls, and the Voice AI widget. Combined usage counts toward the same monthly limit.


Q: Why can Voice AI still create charges even on a subscription plan?

Subscription plans cover AI usage, but Phone System charges still apply to all phone calls. On AI Employee Growth, Voice Engine, TTS, and LLM token costs also apply once the 100-minute limit is exceeded.


Q: Why do two Voice AI calls with the same duration cost different amounts?

The time-based parts may be similar, but token usage can vary depending on the conversation length, number of turns, prompt size, retrieved context, and selected model.


Q: Where can I see AI usage charges?

Agency Admins can use the AI Suite at the Agency level to review supported AI product usage, token usage, transaction counts, pay-per-use charges, and transaction-level details.


Q: Are Workflow AI Builder and Generate with AI billed through AI credits?

No. These features are free with fair-usage protections. If a user reaches the daily limit, access resets within the reset window.


Q: Is Agent Studio (Flow Builder) included in any plan?

No. Agent Studio (Flow Builder) is pay-per-use across all plans (Pay-Per-Use, AI Employee Starter, AI Employee Growth, and AI Employee Unlimited).
