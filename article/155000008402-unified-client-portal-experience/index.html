What is the Unified Client Portal?

The unified Client Portal brings Dashboard, Courses, Communities, billing, shared files, and account settings into one connected and branded customer environment. Previously, these features existed as separate experiences with different navigation patterns, requiring customers to switch between tabs and re-orient themselves repeatedly.

The unified portal eliminates that fragmentation. Customers now have one login, one navigation system, and one seamless experience for all customer-facing interactions with your business.

This architectural upgrade moves Client Portal, Courses, and Communities from legacy platforms to a shared, modern foundation. The result is a faster, smoother, and more consistent customer journey that feels like a single product rather than disconnected applications.

Faster performance — The portal delivers faster page loads, smoother transitions, and a more responsive experience across desktop and mobile devices.

Modern architecture for future growth — The shared foundation makes it faster to deliver improvements and unlock new customer-facing capabilities without adding disconnected products.

SEO and Discoverability

The unified portal expands how businesses can reach customers beyond the portal itself. Eligible public Course and Community pages are now SEO-ready, with search-friendly rendering, crawlable URLs, and page metadata that help search engines understand and discover content.

This gives businesses more opportunities to increase reach, help new audiences discover valuable content, build organic visibility around their expertise, and turn public content into a stronger growth channel.

Important

Private, gated, and customer-only content remains protected. Only public Course and Community pages are eligible for search engine indexing.

How to Enable the Unified Client Portal

Follow these steps to enable the unified Client Portal experience for your business and customers:

Step 1
Navigate to the Client Portal Dashboard

In the MxP app, go to Client Portal → Dashboard.

Step 2
Enable the Unified Portal

Locate the button to enable the unified portal experience and click it to activate the feature.

Step 3
Customers Begin Using the Unified Portal

Once enabled, customers can begin using the unified portal experience. There are no changes in routing or other parts of the system.

Success

Customers can now navigate seamlessly between Dashboard, Courses, Communities, billing, shared files, and account settings in one unified experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What changes when I enable the unified Client Portal?
When you enable the unified portal, customers will see a redesigned experience that connects Dashboard, Courses, Communities, billing, shared files, and account settings into one seamless environment. The backend routing and other system parts remain unchanged.
Q: Will my customers need to log in again?
No. Customers use the same login credentials. The unified portal provides a single login experience that works across Dashboard, Courses, Communities, billing, files, and account settings.
Q: Are public Courses and Communities automatically indexed by search engines?
Eligible public Course and Community pages are now SEO-ready with search-friendly rendering, crawlable URLs, and page metadata. Private, gated, and customer-only content remains protected and is not indexed by search engines.
Q: Will the unified portal affect my existing workflows or automations?
No. The unified portal changes the customer-facing experience but does not affect backend routing, workflows, automations, or integrations. Your existing configurations remain unchanged.
Q: Can I customize the unified portal with my business branding?
Yes. The unified portal keeps your business brand front and center throughout the customer experience. Additional branding and customization options are planned for future releases.
Q: Is the unified portal available on mobile devices?
Yes. The unified portal is optimized for both desktop and mobile devices, delivering faster performance and a more responsive experience across all screen sizes.
Q: What upcoming features are planned for the unified portal?
Future updates will include appointments inside the Client Portal, custom navigation links, deeper portal customization, expanded branding options, richer Community and event experiences, and more customer self-service capabilities.
Q: Can I disable the unified portal after enabling it?
The unified portal represents the future of the Client Portal experience and is built on a modern architecture designed for ongoing improvements. If you encounter issues after enabling it, contact MxP support for assistance.
