Why SMS in Mexico May Appear from a Different Number

SMS Compliance
SMS Availability on Mexico Local Phone Numbers
 Learn why SMS is no longer supported on Mexico local phone numbers, how to identify affected numbers, and which communication methods remain available. 
Overview

Mexico local phone numbers no longer support SMS due to an upstream carrier migration. This applies to both existing Mexico local numbers that previously supported SMS and newly purchased Mexico local numbers. MxP now displays an SMS unavailable status pill so admins can identify the restriction directly from the Phone Numbers table. Voice continues to work, and WhatsApp continues to work where applicable.

SMS Availability on Mexico Local Numbers

Understanding the current capability of Mexico local numbers helps admins avoid unnecessary SMS troubleshooting and choose the appropriate communication method.

  • SMS is no longer supported on Mexico local phone numbers.
  • The restriction applies to existing Mexico local numbers, including numbers that previously supported SMS.
  • The restriction also applies to newly purchased Mexico local numbers.
  • This is a permanent capability restriction associated with an upstream carrier migration.
Important

The SMS restriction is not caused by message volume, a temporary provisioning delay, or an account configuration issue.

2

How to Identify an Affected Number

MxP displays the restriction directly in the Phone Numbers table so admins can identify affected Mexico local numbers before attempting to use SMS.

  1. From the Agency or Sub-Account view, go to Settings.
  2. Select Phone Numbers.
  3. Select Manage Numbers.
  4. Open the Phone Numbers tab.
  5. Locate the Mexico local number.
  6. Look for the SMS unavailable status pill alongside the number's other status indicators.

Why the SMS Unavailable Status Appears

The status pill helps admins distinguish a permanent carrier-related capability change from a temporary sending or configuration problem.

The underlying service provider migrated affected Mexico local numbers to a new carrier. As a result, SMS capability was permanently removed from Mexico local numbers. MxP surfaces this restriction directly in the Phone Numbers table so admins can identify the change before attempting to send SMS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this affect Mexico local numbers I already own?
Yes. The restriction applies to existing Mexico local numbers, including numbers that previously supported SMS.
Q: Does this also affect newly purchased Mexico local numbers?
Yes. Newly purchased Mexico local numbers are also subject to the SMS restriction.
Q: Can I restore SMS by changing a setting or waiting for provisioning?
No. The release identifies this as a permanent carrier-related capability change rather than a temporary provisioning issue.
Q: Can I still make voice calls with the number?
Yes. Voice continues to work.
Q: Can I still use WhatsApp with the number?
WhatsApp continues to work where applicable.
