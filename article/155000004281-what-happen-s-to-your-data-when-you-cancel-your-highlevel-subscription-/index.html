What happen​s to your data when you cancel your MxP Subscription?

Canceling your MxP subscription impacts your Agency account and all associated sub-accounts, including access to important data and features.


MxP retains canceled account data for 90 days after cancellation. During this retention period, you can reactivate your account to regain access and keep your data.


However, some services—such as phone numbers and A2P registrations—follow a shorter timeline and are deleted 14 days after cancellation.


This article explains what happens after cancellation, how long your data is retained, what you should save before canceling, and the options available if you want to reactivate, pause, or request earlier deletion.

What Happens Immediately After Cancellation?


After cancellation, all of your sub-accounts will lose access to the following:


Feature or DataWhat Happens After Cancellation
ContactsAll saved contact data will no longer be accessible.
ConversationsOngoing and past conversations will no longer be accessible.
CalendarsAny scheduled events and appointments will no longer be accessible.
AutomationsWorkflows and automations will stop functioning.
FunnelsAll funnels and related data will no longer be accessible.
Phone Numbers and A2PPhone numbers and A2P registrations will be deleted 14 days after cancellation. You will continue to be charged for them during this 14-day period. After 14 days, the phone numbers will be permanently deleted.
Add-ons / Reselling SubscriptionsWhen your company's MxP (MxP) subscription is canceled, any associated add-on subscriptions will also be automatically canceled. Access to these add-ons and related features may no longer be available once the subscription ends.


Important: Losing access to your account does not mean that all account data is immediately deleted. Most canceled account data is retained for 90 days, as explained below.

Phone Numbers and A2P Registrations


Phone numbers and A2P registrations follow a different timeline from the general 90-day account data retention period.


Your phone numbers and A2P registrations will be deleted 14 days after cancellation.


During those 14 days:

  • Your phone numbers and A2P registrations have not yet been deleted.

  • You will continue to be charged for the phone numbers.

After the 14-day period, the phone numbers will be permanently deleted.


Important: Do not assume your phone numbers will remain available for the full 90-day account retention period. Their deletion timeline is 14 days after cancellation.

Data Retention and Account Deletion


MxP keeps canceled account data for 90 days after cancellation.


During this 90-day period, you can reactivate your account to regain access and retain your data.


If your account is not reactivated within 90 days, your:

  • Agency account

  • Sub-accounts

  • Associated account data


will be permanently deleted.


Cancellation Timeline


TimelineWhat Happens
After cancellationYour sub-accounts lose access to the data and features listed above. Workflows and automations stop functioning.
Within the first 14 daysPhone numbers and A2P registrations remain pending deletion, and you continue to be charged for your phone numbers.
After 14 daysPhone numbers and A2P registrations are deleted. Phone numbers are permanently deleted.
During the 90-day retention periodMxP retains canceled account data, and you can reactivate your account to regain access and retain your data.
After 90 days without reactivationYour Agency account, sub-accounts, and associated data are permanently deleted.

Reminder Emails Before Permanent Deletion


MxP sends automated reminder emails before your account reaches permanent deletion.


These emails are sent from: customercare+MxP.com@mailbox.MxP.com


You may receive the following notifications:


  • “Your MxP account will be deleted in 60 days”

  • “Your MxP account will be deleted in 30 days”

  • “Final Notice: Your MxP account will be deleted in 5 days”


These reminders give you an opportunity to reactivate your account before the 90-day retention period ends.

Permanent Deletion Is Irreversible


MxP cannot restore deleted Agency accounts, sub-accounts, or account data after the 90-day retention period.


MxP does not maintain backups of purged account data, and MxP teams cannot override or reverse the deletion process.


If you prefer to have your data deleted sooner, you can submit a request using the Data Deletion Request Form.


Important: Once an account is permanently deleted, the process is final and cannot be reversed.

Before You Cancel


Before canceling your MxP subscription, export or download any important data you want to keep.


This may include:


  • Contacts

  • Conversations

  • Calendars and appointments

  • Funnels and websites

  • Automations

  • Invoices, payments, orders, and transactions

  • Products

  • Memberships and courses

  • Reports, audit logs, and other account assets


Important: Once your account is permanently deleted or purged after 90 days, this data cannot be recovered.

Options Before or After Cancellation


Depending on your situation, you may have the following options.


1. Reactivate Your Account


You can reactivate your account within the 90-day retention period to regain access and keep your data.


If you do not reactivate within 90 days, your Agency account, sub-accounts, and associated data will be permanently deleted.



2. Request Earlier Data Deletion


If you want your data deleted before the 90-day retention period ends, submit the Data Deletion Request Form.



3. Pause Your Account


If you plan to cancel temporarily, consider using the Pause Account Feature for $27/month to keep your data active.


Before canceling your subscription, make sure to export or download any important data you want to keep. This may include contacts, conversations, calendars, appointments, funnels, websites, automations, invoices, payments, products, memberships/courses, and other account assets.


Once the account is permanently deleted or purged after 90 days, this data cannot be recovered.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q. Will my data be deleted immediately when I cancel?

No. MxP keeps canceled account data for 90 days after cancellation.

However, you will lose access to the applicable data and features in your sub-accounts after cancellation, and workflows and automations will stop functioning.

Phone numbers and A2P registrations follow a separate 14-day deletion timeline.



Q. Can I access my data after canceling?

Your sub-accounts will lose access to contacts, conversations, calendars, funnels, and other applicable data and features after cancellation.

You can reactivate your account within the 90-day retention period to regain access and retain your data.



Q. How long does MxP keep my data?

MxP retains canceled account data for 90 days after cancellation.

If your account is not reactivated within that period, your Agency account, sub-accounts, and associated data will be permanently deleted.



Q. What happens to my workflows and automations?

Any workflows and automations will stop functioning after cancellation.



Q. What happens to my phone numbers and A2P registrations?

Your phone numbers and A2P registrations will be deleted 14 days after cancellation.

During this period, you will continue to be charged for the phone numbers. After 14 days, the phone numbers will be permanently deleted.



Q. What happens to my add-ons and reselling subscriptions?

When your company's MxP subscription is canceled, any associated add-on subscriptions are also automatically canceled.

Access to these add-ons and related features may no longer be available once the subscription ends.



Q. Can I reactivate my account?

Yes. You can reactivate your account during the 90-day retention period to regain access and keep your data.



Q. Can MxP recover my account after the 90-day retention period?

No.

Once an account is permanently deleted, the process is final and cannot be reversed.

MxP cannot restore deleted Agency accounts, sub-accounts, or account data after the 90-day retention period. MxP does not maintain backups of purged account data, and MxP teams cannot override or reverse the deletion process.



Q. Can I have my data deleted before the 90-day retention period ends?

Yes. If you prefer to have your data deleted sooner, submit a request using the Data Deletion Request Form.



Q. Can I pause my account instead of canceling?

If you plan to cancel temporarily, you can consider the Pause Account Feature for $27/month to keep your data active.
