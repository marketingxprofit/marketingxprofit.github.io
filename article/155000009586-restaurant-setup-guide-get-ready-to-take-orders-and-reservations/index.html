Restaurant Setup Guide: Get Ready to Take Orders and Reservations

Restaurant Setup in MxP helps you prepare your restaurant for online orders and table reservations. Follow a six-step checklist to add your business information, connect payments, create a menu, configure fulfillment, and set up seating and bookings. Once completed, preview and publish your restaurant website so customers can order and reserve tables.

Key Benefits of Restaurant Setup


The checklist organizes initial configuration and shows what remains incomplete.

  • Guided progress: Green checks identify completed tasks.

  • Fast menu import: Build and review items from uploaded files.

  • Flexible fulfillment: Set pickup hours, delivery areas, and fees.

  • Table booking: Match reservations to your seating plan.

  • Shared settings: Reuse business information and store hours.

  • Resume anytime: Skip and revisit steps.

How to Open Restaurant Setup


Access the six-step checklist from your restaurant dashboard.

  1. Open Restaurants > Overview.

  2. Find Restaurant setup and select Restaurant details (Start here) or another step.





Completed steps show a green check. Use Back, Skip for now, or the left panel to navigate.

Step 1: Add Your Restaurant Details


Configure the information customers see and the hours used by restaurant services. Save each card.

 

CardHow to Configure
BrandEnter the business name, upload a PNG/JPG/GIF logo, adjust its square crop using Zoom image, and choose a brand color.
LocationEnter address, city, state/region, postal code, country, phone, email, and time zone.
Store hoursCheck open days and select times. Use + for another shift; uncheck closed days.
LinksChoose a link type, enter the URL, and use Add link for more.





Important: Changing your name, logo, or location may affect your sub-account's business profile, emails, invoices, and website. Choose Update details to confirm or Keep editing to return.



Holiday closures can be added later under Restaurant Settings > Restaurant details > Add closure.


When all four cards are saved, the step gets a green check.

Step 2: Connect a Payment Provider


Connect a provider for restaurant payments. Payment Integrations opens in another browser tab.

  1. Select Open Payments.



  2. In Payment Integrations, select Connect beside your provider and follow the prompts.



  3. Return to setup, confirm Your payment provider is connected, and select Continue.



For Stripe, see Getting Started - Connect Stripe. Change providers later in Payments.

Step 3: Add Your Menu


Create categories, items, and prices using one of three methods.


MethodDescription
Upload your menuImport a photo, PDF, or spreadsheet for review.
Use your websiteUse your existing online menu page.
Add items yourselfBuild categories and items manually.



Upload Your Menu


The import creates an editable draft before anything is added.

  1. Select Upload your menu, choose files, and click Upload menu. Supports PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, CSV, and XLSX (up to 50 MB each, 15 files per upload). A sample spreadsheet is available.



  2. Wait while Found so far lists recognized categories and items.



  3. Check All items and Needs review. Use the three-dot menu to Edit item, Move to category, or Remove from draft.




  4. Select Add items to menu, review Your menu, add missing items or categories, and click Continue.




Note: Items are not saved to your menu until you confirm the draft. Use Discard draft to restart. Add photos, sizes, and modifiers later in Menu.

Step 4: Set Up Pickup and Delivery


Choose when guests can order, how orders are fulfilled, and what they pay. Restaurant staff handles delivery.


Prep Time and Pickup


Set Prep time (initially 20 minutes) to your kitchen's usual preparation time; individual orders can be adjusted.


SettingConfiguration
PickupTurn on Do you want to offer pickup?
HoursUse store hours or separate pickup hours
Scheduled ordersAllow guests to choose a later pickup time
MarkupPercentage added to prices; 0 keeps menu prices
Minimum orderOptional amount before tax under Advanced settings



Delivery


Select Do you want to offer delivery?, then choose Distance bands or Per mile pricing and Miles or Kilometers. Distances are measured in a straight line from your restaurant.

  1. Select Add area and enter the following:

 

FieldWhat to Enter
Area type/nameDistance and an identifying name
DistanceStart and end of coverage range
Delivery feeCharge, or 0 for free delivery
Free for orders overOptional free-delivery threshold
Estimated delivery timeTime range shown at checkout




  1. Add further areas as needed; Already covered indicates used distances.

  2. Use Test an address to preview delivery coverage, fee, and time without modifying settings.

  3. Set delivery hours, scheduling, markup, and optional minimum order; click Save and continue.





Delivery requires at least one area and a connected payment provider. ZIP code areas are marked Coming soon.

Step 5: Add Your Areas and Tables


Your floor layout represents the tables used for reservations and walk-ins.


  1. Rename Main dining if needed. Setup starts with five round tables.

  2. Set each table's name, seats, and shape: Round, Square, Rectangle, Booth, or Bar seat.

  3. Use Add table, the trash icon, or Add another area to match your restaurant.

  4. Review the preview's table and seat counts.


Arrange Tables With Canvas (Optional)


Select Edit with canvas to drag tables, add walls or labels, and adjust layout tools. Select a table to set its label, capacity, Active, Bookable online, or Minimum party options. Click Done, then Save and continue.


Step 6: Set Up Reservations


Choose booking availability, deposits, and table times. Reservations need a completed floor layout; if prompted, select Set up floor layout first.




Reservation Hours and Online Booking

Click Save and complete setup.

After You Finish Setup


After Save and complete setup, Restaurant Website > Dashboard displays Setup complete, with Pickup, Delivery, and Reservations marked Ready.


  1. Select Preview website to review your restaurant site.

  2. Select Publish website to enable customer-facing online ordering and reservations.



Overview then shows Restaurant setup is done. Select Set up website to continue.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Do I have to finish the steps in order?

No. Click any step in the setup panel to open it. Some steps depend on others: guests can order once a payment provider is connected, delivery starts once you add a delivery area, reservations start once your floor layout is ready, and deposits need a payment provider.


Q: Why does MxP ask me to update my sub-account details?

Your business name, logo and location are part of your sub-account’s business profile. Saving them in setup changes them everywhere they are used, such as emails, invoices and your website. Click Update details to confirm, or Keep editing to go back.


Q: My uploaded menu missed or misread something. What should I do?

Fix it in the draft before you add it: click the three-dot menu next to an item and choose Edit item, Move to category or Remove from draft. You can also add and edit items any time later in Menu.


Q: How can I check what a guest pays for delivery?

In Delivery pricing, click Test an address and enter an address or a distance. You see the area, distance, delivery fee and delivery time. It includes changes you have not saved yet and does not change your settings.


Q: Does finishing setup make my restaurant live?

Not on its own. Guests can order and book once you publish your restaurant website. Click Publish website on Restaurant Website > Dashboard.


Q: Where do I change these settings after setup?

In Restaurants > Restaurant Settings. Change your menu in Menu and your payment providers in Payments.
