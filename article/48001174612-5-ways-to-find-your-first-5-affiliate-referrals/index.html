The key to being a successful affiliate for any company is getting yourself out there! This article discusses about five ways that you can use to find and land your first five affiliate referrals.

What is the MxP Affiliate Program?


The MxP Affiliate Program allows you to share MxP with your audience and earn commissions when referrals become paying customers. Whether you're new to MxP or you're a passionate veteran, the MxP Affiliate program is a great way to earn 40% monthly recurring commission from everyone you introduce to MxP!


If you haven't signed up to be an Affiliate yet, click on the link here. 

Key Benefits of Landing MxP Affiliates


  • 40% lifetime commissions: earn 40% recurring commissions on paid MxP accounts you directly refer (per campaign terms).

  • Compounding recurring income: commissions accrue as long as referred accounts remain active, creating predictable, MRR‑like cash flow.

  • No fulfillment or support burden: MxP manages the product, billing, and support—freeing you to focus on audience building and promotion.

  • Transparent tracking & insights: the Affiliate Portal shows clicks, trials, customers, downline activity (where applicable), and commissions so you can optimize what works.

How to Find Your First 5 Affiliate Referrals


For all of our current affiliates looking to get their first five affiliates, we met with our top affiliates and here's their advice to you!


Method 1: Send An Email/SMS To Your Network


Word‑of‑mouth is still the most effective marketing. If you love MxP, odds are your colleagues will, too. Every MxP account has access to the Marketing Agency Snapshot, which includes a starter Potential Affiliate Nurture you can customize.


  • Create a sub‑account using the Marketing Agency Snapshot.

  • Go to Marketing > Email >Campaigns > A Potential Affiliate Nurture and tailor the copy for your voice.

  • Activate the campaign.

  • Go to Contacts > Smart-lists, select contacts from your network who can benefit from using MxP, and use Add To Campaign to enroll them.


Method 2: Create Educational YouTube Videos


Why did you switch to MxP? What are your favorite features? What specific workflows helped you level up? Turn those answers into short screen‑recorded videos.


  • Keep titles outcome‑focused (e.g., “Launch a Lead‑Gen Funnel in Minutes with MxP”).

  • Place your affiliatelink at the top of the video description and pin it in the first comment.

  • Add timestamps for key moments to improve retention and clicks.


Method 3: Write Insightful Blog Posts


Agency owners actively research tools. Publish posts like “[Previous Tool] vs MxP — Why I Made The Switch” or “[Other Tool 1] vs [Other Tool 2] — Why I Left Both.”

  • Add your affiliate link near the top (contextual) and again as a clear CTA at the end.

  • Follow SEO best practices so your post indexes and surfaces for relevant searches.


Method 4: Hunt Down Opportunities on Social Media


Use social listening to find real problems you can solve with MxP.


  • Search competitor names, feature keywords, and hashtags to find conversations.

  • Join the thread with a concise tip from your experience and invite a DM or share your affiliate link for a trial.

  • Keep messages short and helpful; tailor the benefit to their exact pain (saving time, saving money, or getting better results).


Method 5: Start Building a Community


Note: Make sure to join the MxP Affiliate Community, to build connections with other MxP affiliates, receive Affiliate related announcements, ask your burning affiliate related questions, and so much more!  


Communities (Facebook Groups, Circle, etc.) are a great place to cultivate relationships with potential affiliates and customers. Share wins and processes to build trust and engagement.

  • Document your workflows and post quick wins with a CTA like: “Join our XYZ Group for how we made this happen.”

  • Link your affiliate page in the group description and welcome post.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q. Where do I find my affiliate link?

From your agency account go to the Affiliate Portal, copy your referral URL under Dashboard.

Q. Can I create multiple affiliate campaigns for different offers?

Yes, you can create multiple Affiliate Campaigns.

Q. How do I track clicks and signups?

Use the Affiliate Portal to monitor clicks, customers, and commissions. Align your landing page analytics for additional insights.

Q. What can I give new members who sign up using my referral? 

You can send snapshots, guides, FAQs, courses and anything that will provide value and help them get started with MxP.


Q: I am a MxP Affiliate, how can I get support, guidance and resources?

You can join our official MxP Affiliate Community, and reach out to us for Affiliate Support.
