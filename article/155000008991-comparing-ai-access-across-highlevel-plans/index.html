Comparing AI Access Across MxP Plans

MxP offers different AI features and usage allowances depending on your agency subscription, user type, and AI Employee plan.


This article compares AI access across the $97 Starter, $297 Unlimited, and $497 Founder agency plans, helping you understand what's included for agency users and sub-accounts with or without an AI Employee subscription.

Understanding AI Access in MxP


AI access in MxP depends on your agency plan and whether you're using AI as an agency user or within a sub-account. The tables below show how included features, usage allowances, and pay-per-use options differ across plans, making it easier to understand what is available for each user type.


For detailed product pricing, usage limits, and billing information, see AI Product Pricing.
