Fill out this form to register your interest for the private beta.

Welcome to the private beta of Field Services, MxP's new tool for home and field service businesses. Field Services connects requests, jobs, scheduling, dispatch and invoicing in one place, so your office and your field team work from the same picture of the day.

This guide explains what is included in the beta, what to expect, how to get started, and how to share feedback. Your feedback directly shapes what we build next.

Overview

Field Services helps businesses that send staff to the customer's location, such as plumbing, HVAC, electrical, cleaning and landscaping. With Field Services you can:

  • Capture customer requests from your booking page, or create them in the app.
  • Quote and organize by turning requests into estimates and jobs, with services, products and pricing.
  • Schedule one or more visits per job, find open times and assign staff.
  • Dispatch by seeing every visit on a calendar and map, and optimizing each staff member's route.
  • Deliver and bill by sending reminders and updates, tracking visit progress, and invoicing the customer.

Who is this Beta for?

This private beta is designed for agencies and businesses in field and home services. By taking part, you will be among the first to use Field Services and help shape what we build next.

You will get the most from the beta if:

  • Your team travels to customers' addresses to do the work.
  • You manage jobs that need more than one visit, or more than one technician.
  • You want customers to book or request work online, with your coverage rules applied.
  • You are willing to try the product with real scenarios and tell us what works and what does not.

How to Get Started

  1. Fill out this form to register your interest for the private beta.
  2. Once added to the beta, you will see Field Services with a Beta tag in the left navigation of your enrolled sub-account.
  3. Start on Home. New accounts open on a welcome page with a three-step checklist: add your staff, review your service area and add your services. See Field Services Home Overview.
  4. Set up your coverage. Review the Default service area and add more if needed under Services & staff > Service areas. See Service Areas in Field Services.
  5. Add staff. Set working hours, assigned services, service areas and route start and end for each person. See Staff in Field Services.
  6. Add services. Create services or use Services & staff > Import to copy existing ones. See Services and Add-ons in Field Services and Importing Services into Field Services.
  7. Review settings. Open Field Services Settings to choose how customers book, how they pay, and which notifications go out. See Field Services Settings: Online Booking.
  8. Create a test request or job and schedule a visit to see the full flow.
Note: If you already use Services, your existing setup is not changed. You choose what to copy into Field Services with the importer.

Suggested Test Plan for Your First Week

If you are not sure where to begin, this plan covers the full flow in about a week. Adjust it to fit your business.

Day 3Create a request, convert it to an estimate, then create a job.Does the job hold the right scope and pricing?
Day 4Schedule visits with Find availability, including a job with two visits.Are the suggested times sensible? Can you override staff when you need to?

Known Limitations

Field Services is in private beta, so keep the following in mind:

  • Some features listed under Upcoming Features are not yet available.
  • The AI receptionist supports chat. Voice options are not yet available.
  • You may occasionally see minor performance or interface issues as we keep improving the product.

If you run into an issue, share it with us. Your feedback helps us fix things faster.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Who has access to the Field Services beta?
A: Field Services is a private beta for selected customers. This lets us gather feedback and make improvements before a wider release. You can fill out this form to register your interest for the beta.

Q: How can I check if Field Services is active on my account?
A: Open your sub-account and look for Field Services in the left navigation. If you do not see it, contact us using one of the feedback options above.

Q: When will Field Services be available to everyone?
A: We are planning a wider release after the private beta. Watch the Release Notes for updates.

Q: Will Field Services change my existing calendars or Services?
A: No. Field Services is a separate module. Your existing calendars, Services and workflows keep working as they do today.

Q: Can I move my existing services into Field Services?
A: Yes. Use Services & staff > Import and choose CSV or Services as the source. Services whose name already exists in Field Services are skipped, and existing services are not updated. See Importing Services into Field Services.

Q: Do I need a payment provider?
A: Only if you want customers to pay online at booking. You can use Pay in-person without one. See Field Services Settings: Online Booking.

Q: Where can I learn the basics?
A: Start with Field Services - Overview & How to Get Started, then follow the articles in the Related Articles list.
