How to Summarize Opportunities with AI

AI Opportunity Summaries in MxP help you quickly understand where a deal stands without reviewing every interaction manually. With a single click, AI analyzes conversations, activities, and opportunity details to summarize deal progress, highlight pending items, and recommend the next action. You can also verify information using source citations and save summaries to opportunity notes for future reference.

What Are AI Opportunity Summaries?


AI Opportunity Summaries provide a quick overview of an opportunity's current status using information from associated conversations, activities, and opportunity records. Instead of manually reviewing messages, calls, and appointments, you can generate a summary that highlights the deal's progress, outstanding items, and recommended next steps.

What Information Is Included in an AI Opportunity Summary?


AI Opportunity Summaries analyze available interactions and opportunity details to provide context about each deal. This helps you understand what has happened across different communication channels and identify information that may require attention.


The AI uses information from:


  • Conversations: Messages exchanged through SMS, Email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and other connected channels.

  • Calls: Call activity and transcripts, when available.

  • Appointments: Booked, rescheduled, canceled, and missed appointments.

  • Forms: Submitted form information associated with the contact.

  • Automated Communications: Messages sent through workflows and campaigns, distinguished from manually sent messages.

  • Opportunity Details: The opportunity's current pipeline stage, status, and value.


The generated summary highlights three important details:


  • Current Deal Status: Where the opportunity stands based on recent interactions.

  • Pending Items: Unanswered questions, outstanding decisions, or actions awaiting a response.

  • Recommended Next Step: A suggested follow-up action, including the relevant contact and communication channel when available.

How to Generate an AI Opportunity Summary


AI Opportunity Summaries can be generated directly from an opportunity record without navigating through individual conversations or activity logs.


Note: AI summaries can identify potential discrepancies in opportunity records, but you should review the supporting information before making changes to deal details.


  1. Navigate to Opportunities in account.



  2. Open the opportunity you want to review.

  3. Click Summarize at the top of the opportunity details panel.

  4. Wait for AI to generate the summary. Review the current status, outstanding items, and recommended next action.

  5. Click More detail to expand the summary and review its supporting citations.

  6. To adjust the date range, click the dropdown next to Summary and choose your preferred range.

  7. Click + Note to save the generated summary to the opportunity's notes.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: What happens if a call transcript is unavailable?

Transcript-specific information is included only when a transcript is available.


Q: Does AI distinguish between automated messages and manually sent messages?

Yes. Messages sent through workflows and campaigns are distinguished from messages sent directly by a person.


Q: Can I save multiple summaries for the same opportunity?

Yes. You can save summaries to opportunity notes at different points in the sales process to maintain a record of how the deal progresses.


Q: Should I verify the AI-generated information before taking action?

Yes. Use the source citations to confirm important details, especially when the summary identifies conflicting information or recommends a follow-up based on previous interactions.
