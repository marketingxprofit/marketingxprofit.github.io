Meeting Room in MxP: Overview

Meeting Room is MxP's built-in video conferencing solution for client calls, team meetings, training sessions, and more. You can create reusable meeting links, start video calls instantly, collaborate with participants, and access recordings, transcripts, and AI-powered meeting summaries in one place.

What Is Meeting Room?


Meeting Room lets you host video meetings directly in MxP without using a separate video conferencing platform.



The Meeting Room section in your sub-account includes three main areas:


  • My room: Create and manage your personal Meeting Room with a permanent, reusable link.


  • Team rooms: Find and access team members' Meeting Rooms.


  • Integrations: Connect Meeting Room with other MxP products.


You can also generate unique Meeting Room links for individual calendar appointments.

Key Features of Meeting Room


Video Meetings and Collaboration


  • Instant meetings: Start video calls without scheduling an appointment.


  • Guest access: Invite participants using a meeting link without requiring a MxP account when guest access is enabled.


  • Screen sharing and chat: Share content and communicate during meetings.


  • Interactive tools: Use reactions, hand raising, and different meeting layouts.


  • Picture-in-Picture: Keep your meeting visible while switching browser tabs.


Meeting Management and Security


  • Host and co-host controls: Manage participants, meeting permissions, and host responsibilities.


  • Waiting rooms and passcodes: Control who can access your meetings.


  • Video and audio settings: Configure cameras, microphones, backgrounds, lighting, and noise cancellation.


Recordings, Transcripts, and AI


  • Meeting recordings: Record meetings and access available recordings afterward.


  • Speaker-labeled transcripts: Review conversations with speaker names and timestamps.


  • AI meeting assistance: Ask questions during meetings and generate summaries, key decisions, and action items from transcripts.


Meeting Room Integrations


Meeting Room works with several MxP products:

How to Set Up Your Personal Meeting Room


Create a personal room with a permanent link that you can reuse for meetings.


  1. Open your MxP sub-account and go to Meeting Room > My room.

  2. Click Create room.

  3. Under Room details, enter your Name and Room link.

  4. Under Security, configure your passcode, waiting room, guest access, and room availability as needed.

  5. Optionally, add a co-host under Co-hosts.

  6. Click Save changes.


Your personal Meeting Room is now ready to use.






How to Start an Instant Meeting


You can start a meeting anytime without scheduling an appointment.


  1. Go to Meeting Room > My room.

  2. Click Start room.

  3. Review your microphone, speaker, and camera settings.

  4. Click Start now to begin your meeting.


To invite participants, copy your Room link from My room and share it with them.


Important Notes


  • Guests can join without a MxP account when Allow guest users is enabled.


  • Custom background uploads are available to logged-in users.


  • Recording a meeting does not automatically generate a transcript. Transcription must be enabled during the meeting for transcript-based AI features.
