How to buy a toll free number

Understanding how to buy a toll-free number can be critical for your business operations. This article provides a straightforward walkthrough on purchasing a toll-free number for your sub-accounts. Equip yourself with this knowledge to enhance your customer communications strategy.


Note: If the sub-account is on the LC phone, you can sign up for Toll-Free Number Registration for LC - Phone (US/Canada) from the application. [Only applicable to US/Canada Toll-Free Numbers]

If the sub-account is using your/their own Twilio account, you will need to contact Twilio support to register your toll-free number.

What is a Toll-Free Number?


Toll-free numbers are business phone numbers that use recognizable prefixes such as 800, 888, 877, 866, 855, 844, and 833. They can provide businesses with a broader, non-local presence and can support capabilities such as Voice, SMS, and MMS when those capabilities are available for the selected number. Availability depends on the country, number type, selected capabilities, provider inventory, and applicable regulations.


A toll-free number purchased through an MxP Phone-managed MxP sub-account is associated with that specific sub-account. After purchase, the number can be configured for calling, routing, workflows, and other supported communication features.

Key Benefits of Toll-Free Numbers


  • Professional business presence: Use a recognizable business number that is not tied to a particular local area code.

  • Voice and messaging capabilities: Search for toll-free numbers that support capabilities such as Voice, SMS, or MMS when available.

  • Flexible number selection: Filter available numbers using number type, capabilities, or specific digits to narrow your search.

  • MxP integration: Use purchased numbers with supported MxP communication, routing, automation, and workflow features.

How to Buy a Toll-Free Number


  1. From the  sub-account, click Settings in the bottom-left corner.

  2. Open Phone System.



  3. Click + Add Number.

  4. From the dropdown menu, select Add Phone Number.




  5. Click Filters to open the number filters.



  6. Under Number Type, select Toll-Free.

  7. Click Apply to update the search results.



  8. Select the number you would like to but then click Proceed to Buy.

Q: Does purchasing a toll-free number automatically verify it for SMS or MMS?

No. Purchasing the number and completing Toll-Free Verification are separate processes. Toll-free numbers used for messaging to US or Canadian recipients must successfully complete Toll-Free Verification before SMS or MMS traffic can be sent.


Q: How can one register their US/Canada Toll-Free numbers?

Q: Can I make calls while Toll-Free Verification is pending?

Yes. Toll-Free Verification applies to messaging. Voice calling is not affected by the Toll-Free Verification status.
