What is the Printful Integration?

The Printful integration allows you to connect your Printful print-on-demand account directly with MxP's e-commerce platform. Printful is a Drop Shipping company that handles printing and fulfillment of custom apparel, accessories, and other products featuring your own designs and logos.

This integration enables you to sell Printful products through your MxP website or funnel without manually managing inventory, order processing, or shipping. When customers purchase products from your MxP store, orders automatically sync to Printful for fulfillment.

The integration is available through MxP's Integrations section and can also be discovered in the MxP Marketplace at marketplace.MxP.com, where you can explore apps from external developers and private companies.

2

Key Benefits of Printful Integration

Automated Product Sync — Products you create or import in Printful automatically appear in your MxP store with titles, images, variants, and pricing intact.
Seamless Order Fulfillment — When customers purchase products through your MxP store, orders sync directly to Printful for automatic fulfillment based on your Printful subscription settings.
Customer Data Integration — Shipping addresses, email notifications, and customer information flow into your MxP CRM so you can manage everything from one platform.
Use Existing Product Templates — Import product templates from existing Printful stores so you don't have to recreate designs or variants from scratch.
Workflow Automation Support — Trigger MxP workflows based on order submissions for review requests, shipping updates, team notifications, and more.
Opportunities Pipeline Integration — Track orders through custom pipelines with stages like "Order Placed" and "Order Fulfilled" for team coordination and follow-up.

How to Install the Printful Integration

Follow these steps to connect your Printful account to your MxP sub-account:

Step 1
Navigate to Integrations in MxP

In your MxP sub-account, go to Settings > Integrations. Scroll to locate the Printful  option or search for it.


Step 2
Install the Printful App

Click Manage, then click Install. Review the permissions the app is requesting for your sub-account and click Allow and Install.


Step 3
Authorize Access in Printful

You'll be redirected to printful.com. Login to your Printful account or Sign up and create a new account.


Step 4
Create or Select a Printful Store

Choose whether to create a new Printful store or connect an existing store then click Accept. Your MxP account will now have access to Printful, and a new store named "MxP" will be created in Printful.

How to Add Products to Your MxP Store

Once the integration is active, you can add Printful products that will automatically sync to your MxP product catalog:

Step 1
Access Your MxP Store in Printful

In Printful, go to the Stores section in the left sidebar. You'll see your new MxP store listed. Click on the three dots and then on View Products for this specific MxP integration.


Step 2
Add Products to the Store

Click Add Product, then select Create New Products to design a product from scratch using Printful’s catalog of apparel, accessories, home goods, and more.

Step 3
Configure Mockups and Product Details

After selecting a product or template, proceed through Printful's product setup flow. You'll generate mockup images, and configure variants (sizes, colors, etc.). Once done, click on Save Template.


Step 4
Publish the Product

Choose mockup images to display, pricing and product name then click on Publish.


Success

Your product is now synced between Printful and MxP. Any changes you make to titles, pricing, or variants in Printful will automatically update in MxP.

Managing Product Sync and Pricing

Once products are added in Printful, they automatically appear in your MxP product catalog with most details intact:

Viewing Synced Products
Check Your MxP Products

In MxP, navigate to Payments > Products. You'll see your Printful product(s) listed with the same names, images, variants, and pricing you configured in Printful.


Editing Products in MxP
Make Adjustments as Needed

Click the three dots next to any product and select Edit. You can modify details just like any other MxP product. Note that product descriptions do not currently sync from Printful, so you'll need to add those manually in MxP.


To make a product available on your MxP e-commerce site, toggle on Include in Online Store in the product settings. This ensures the product appears on your live storefront.


Important

Product descriptions are not synced from Printful. You must manually add descriptions in MxP's product editor before publishing to your store.

The following product data syncs automatically from Printful to MxP:

  • Product titles
  • Product images and mockups
  • Variants (sizes, colors, etc.)
  • Pricing for each variant

Order Fulfillment and Customer Data Sync

One of the most powerful aspects of this integration is automated order fulfillment and customer data synchronization:

Automatic Order Sync
Orders Flow to Printful for Fulfillment

When a customer purchases a Printful product from your MxP store, the order automatically syncs to your Printful account. Depending on your Printful subscription settings, orders may be fulfilled automatically or require manual approval before production begins.

Customer Data Import
Shipping and Contact Information Sync to MxP

Customer information such as shipping addresses and email addresses is imported into your MxP CRM. This allows you to manage customer relationships, send follow-up communications, and track order history directly within MxP.

Viewing Orders in Printful
Monitor Fulfillment Status

All synced orders appear in your Printful dashboard under the MxP store. You can view order details, fulfillment status, and shipping tracking information from within Printful.

Pro Tip

Check your Printful subscription plan to understand whether orders are fulfilled automatically or require manual confirmation. This ensures you maintain control over quality and timing.

Shipping Profiles and Live Rates at Checkout

To enable live shipping rates for Printful products at checkout, the system automatically manages a dedicated Printful shipping profile.

What to Expect:
  • A system-generated Printful shipping profile is created and configured automatically.
  • For existing installations, syncing or updating Printful products can activate the shipping profile and start showing live rates at checkout.
  • Products must stay assigned to the Printful shipping profile. Removing them disables live rate calculation for those products.
  • If the Printful shipping profile is deleted, it is not recreated automatically. Reinstall the Printful integration to restore it.


Note

Live shipping rates are calculated based on customer location and product specifications. Ensure your Printful products remain assigned to the correct shipping profile to maintain accurate checkout pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need an existing Printful account to use this integration?
Yes, you need a Printful account to use this integration. If you don't have one, you can create a free account at printful.com before installing the integration in MxP.
Q: Can I use products from multiple Printful stores in one MxP account?
The integration creates a dedicated "MxP" store in Printful that syncs with your MxP sub-account. While you can import templates from other Printful stores, active sync only occurs between the MxP store and MxP.
Q: What product information syncs from Printful to MxP?
Product titles, images, variants (sizes, colors), and pricing sync automatically. Product descriptions do NOT sync and must be added manually in MxP's product editor.
Q: Are orders automatically fulfilled by Printful?
It depends on your Printful subscription and account settings. Some plans support automatic fulfillment, while others require manual approval before production begins. Check your Printful settings to confirm your fulfillment workflow.
Q: Can I edit product details after they sync to MxP?
Yes, you can edit products in MxP just like any other product. However, changes made in Printful (titles, pricing, variants) will sync back to MxP and may overwrite your edits. For best results, manage titles and pricing in Printful, and use MxP for descriptions and store-specific settings.
