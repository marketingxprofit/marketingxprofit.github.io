Social Posting Rate Limits


These rate limits are for each active user and are different based on which platform you're using:


  • Google – 100 posts / 24 hours 
  • Facebook – 200 posts / 1 hour
  • Facebook Reels – 30 posts / 24 hours
  • Instagram – 25 posts / 24 hours
  • LinkedIn – 200 posts / 24 hours 
  • TikTok – 15 posts / 24 hours
  • YouTube – 25 posts / 24 hours
  • YouTube Global – 2000 posts / 24 hours 
  • Pinterest – 25 posts / 5 minutes

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Why can a file be supported by the social network but rejected in Social Planner?

Social Planner uses the requirements supported by MxP and the connected platform's API. These limits can differ from what the social network allows when posting directly on its website or app.


Q: Can Social Planner optimize media automatically?

Social Planner can optimize supported media for social publishing. You should still review the applicable file size, duration, aspect ratio, and post-type requirements before publishing.


Q: Can I publish images and videos together in the same post?

It depends on the social network and post type. Refer to the Additional Social Posting Guidelines section to confirm whether multimedia is supported.


Q: Can I add a custom thumbnail to every video?

No. Custom thumbnail support depends on the platform and post type. Review the Thumbnail Support column in the Video Guidelines table before publishing.


Q: Why are the YouTube limits in Social Planner different from YouTube's native limits?

This article documents the limits supported when publishing through MxP Social Planner. The limits available when uploading directly to YouTube may be different.
