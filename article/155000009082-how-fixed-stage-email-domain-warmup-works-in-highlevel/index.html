How Fixed-Stage Email Domain Warmup Works in MxP

Email domain warmup in MxP helps businesses gradually increase their email sending volume while establishing a positive sender reputation. The fixed-stage warmup system uses predefined daily sending thresholds to guide new and existing dedicated sending domains through a structured process. It helps reduce the risk of spam filtering, delivery delays, and reputation issues caused by sudden increases in sending volume. This guide explains how warmup works, how to enable it, and how to monitor your progress.

What Is Email Domain Warmup in MxP?


Email domain warmup is the process of gradually increasing the number of emails sent from a dedicated domain so mailbox providers such as Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo can evaluate its sending behavior. MxP's fixed-stage model organizes this process into 15 stages, each with a daily sending threshold.


As your domain reaches each stage's threshold, it becomes eligible to advance to the next stage. This provides a structured approach to increasing sending volume while encouraging consistent, reputation-conscious email practices.



Important: Fixed-stage domain warmup is available for dedicated sending domains at both the agency and sub-account levels. It is being rolled out in phases, so availability may vary by account. New domains automatically enter warmup after successful creation and verification when the feature is available, while existing domains require manual activation.

Key Benefits of Email Domain Warmup


Gradually increasing sending volume helps mailbox providers recognize your domain's sending patterns instead of encountering sudden, unfamiliar activity. MxP's fixed-stage warmup process provides a clear progression path while helping businesses develop more consistent sending practices.



  • Improved Email Deliverability: Gradual volume increases help reduce the likelihood of emails being filtered into spam folders.

  • Stronger Sender Reputation: Consistent sending activity helps establish trust with mailbox providers over time.

  • Structured Sending Growth: Predefined warmup stages provide clear daily volume targets as your sending needs increase.

  • Personalized Starting Stages: Existing domains can receive recommended starting stages based on historical sending activity and available Google Postmaster data.

  • Automatic Stage Progression: Domains advance when their current stage's daily sending threshold is reached, without requiring users to manually select each subsequent stage.

  • Flexible Domain Management: Agencies and sub-accounts can manage warmup for their eligible dedicated sending domains.

How Fixed-Stage Domain Warmup Works


MxP's fixed-stage warmup model helps businesses increase sending activity through predefined daily thresholds rather than an unrestricted increase in email volume. Understanding how these thresholds work is essential for planning email campaigns and determining when a domain can progress to a higher stage.


The warmup model follows these rules:

  • Every warmup stage has a predefined daily sending threshold.

  • A domain must reach its current stage's full daily threshold before progressing.

  • If the threshold is not reached, the domain remains at its current stage.

  • The fixed-stage warmup model does not enforce hourly sending limits.

  • Emails must be sent through the domain undergoing warmup for the system to track progress.

  • Stage progression is managed automatically.


Example:


A domain in Stage 1 has a daily sending threshold of 1,000 emails.

  • If the domain sends 600 emails in a day, it remains in Stage 1.

  • If the domain reaches 1,000 emails in a day, it meets the requirement to progress to Stage 2.

  • Stage 2 has a daily threshold of 2,500 emails.


Important: Currently, reaching or exceeding a dedicated domain's warmup threshold does not automatically block additional emails. A future update is expected to introduce strict enforcement. Until then, users should still follow recommended sending volumes to help protect deliverability.


Warmup thresholds are also separate from other account-level sending restrictions, recipient-provider rate limits, and email compliance controls.

The fixed-stage warmup system contains 15 stages, beginning at 1,000 emails per day and increasing to 250,000 emails per day. These predefined thresholds allow senders to understand how much sending activity is needed to advance through the warmup process.

How Stage Progression Works


MxP tracks sending activity from the domain undergoing warmup. When the domain reaches the daily threshold assigned to its current stage, it satisfies the volume requirement for advancement.


If the threshold is not reached, the domain remains in its current stage. Warmup does not advance simply because a certain number of days have passed.


Note: The 15-stage model applies to dedicated-domain warmup. Shared-domain ramp-up operates under separate rules and may enforce actual sending restrictions.

Warmup for New vs. Existing Domains


New and existing dedicated sending domains enter warmup differently because they may have different sending histories. New domains begin automatically after verification, while existing domains can use previous sending activity and reputation data to receive a recommended starting stage.


New Dedicated Sending Domains


For newly created domains, warmup is automatically enabled after the domain has been successfully created and verified, provided the feature is available for the account.

  • Create a dedicated sending domain.

  • Complete the required DNS verification.

  • Warmup starts automatically.

  • Begin sending emails using the verified domain to build warmup progress.


No separate Start Warmup action is required for newly created domains.


Existing Dedicated Sending Domains


Existing dedicated domains require manual warmup activation. When you click Start Warmup, MxP analyzes available sending information to recommend an appropriate starting stage.


This may include:

  • Historical email sending activity.

  • Available domain reputation information.

  • Google Postmaster data, when connected.


An existing domain may receive a higher recommended starting stage than a newly created domain based on its previous sending behavior.


Note: Recommended starting stages help account for a domain's established history. They do not provide a general option to manually skip ahead through warmup stages.

How to Enable Email Domain Warmup


Correctly configuring and verifying a dedicated sending domain allows MxP to recognize its email activity and track warmup progress. Agencies and sub-accounts can access the feature through Email Services, but the activation process depends on whether the domain is new or already exists.


Enable Warmup for Agency Domains


Agencies can manage warmup for eligible dedicated sending domains from the agency settings.

  1. Open your MxP Agency account.

  2. Navigate to Agency Settings.



  3. Select Email Services.

  4. Open the SMTP Service tab.

  5. Select Dedicated Domain and IP.




  6. Locate the existing dedicated domain you want to warm up.

  7. Click Start Warmup.

  8. Review the recommended starting stage provided by MxP.



  9. Begin sending emails through the configured domain to progress through warmup.


Enable Warmup for Sub-Account Domains


Sub-accounts can activate warmup for existing dedicated sending domains from their own Email Services settings.

  1. Open the appropriate Sub-Account.

  2. Navigate to Settings.

  3. Select Email Services.

  4. Open SMTP Service.

  5. Select Dedicated Domain and IP.

  6. Locate the existing dedicated sending domain.

  7. Click Start Warmup.

  8. Review the recommended starting stage.



  9. Send emails through the selected domain to begin progressing through the warmup stages.

Monitoring Warmup Progress


Monitoring a domain's warmup status helps businesses understand whether their sending activity is sufficient for stage progression. It also provides an opportunity to identify reputation problems before significantly increasing email volume.



To review your domain:


  1. Navigate to Settings → Email Services → SMTP Service → Dedicated Domain and IP at the applicable account level.

  2. Locate the domain currently undergoing warmup.

  3. Review its displayed warmup stage and status.

  4. Compare your sending activity against the daily threshold for that stage.

  5. Review available deliverability and reputation information.


When monitoring warmup, consider the following:

  • If sending volume remains below the stage threshold, progress remains at the current stage.

  • Emails sent through other domains do not contribute toward the domain being warmed up.

  • Increasing volume too quickly can negatively affect reputation even when the platform allows the emails to be sent.

  • Available Google Postmaster data can provide additional insight into the domain's sending health.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: Can I use fixed-stage warmup with a third-party SMTP provider?

The documented feature applies to eligible dedicated sending domains managed through MxP's email services. Third-party SMTP services may have their own warmup mechanisms and sending restrictions.

Q: Does the warmup system automatically send emails on my behalf?

No. The fixed-stage process tracks actual sending activity from the configured domain. You must send emails using that domain for its warmup progress to advance.


Q: What if I do not see the Start Warmup button?

The feature is being introduced in phases, so it may not be available to every account at the same time. Also, newly created domains do not require manual activation after successful verification. Confirm that you are viewing an eligible dedicated sending domain in the correct settings area.


Q: Does exceeding a warmup threshold guarantee that all additional emails will be delivered?

No. Although the dedicated-domain warmup threshold currently does not block excess sending, other platform restrictions, mailbox provider filtering, and rate limits can still affect delivery.


Q: Will connecting Google Postmaster automatically change my current warmup stage?

Connecting Google Postmaster after warmup has started provides additional reputation data but does not retroactively change the current stage.


Q: Can I warm up multiple dedicated sending domains simultaneously?

Warmup progress is associated with the domain used to send emails. Each domain's sending activity must be considered separately when planning campaigns across multiple domains.
