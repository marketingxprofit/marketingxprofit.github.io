WhatsApp Onboarding Error: Payment Method Already Exists

WhatsApp Onboarding Error: Payment Method Already Exists

Why Meta blocks Coexistence onboarding when your WhatsApp account is already connected to another partner, and how to off-board the number and connect it to the CRM.

Error: The business already has this WABA shared with a partner. Switching partners is not supported in this flow.

Table of Contents

Why You See This Error

This error appears when your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) is already connected through WhatsApp Coexistence to a different Business Solution Provider (BSP), which is the partner or platform you used before.

Meta does not support switching partners directly through the Coexistence onboarding flow. Meta lists the matching error as 2655093. Disconnecting the previous partner is the first step, but in many cases it is not enough on its own, and the number has to be offboarded completely before it can be connected again.

This is controlled by Meta, not by the CRM. The CRM cannot remove another partner's connection. The steps below are done on the phone that holds the number.

Check Which Partner Is Connected

Before you change anything, confirm that another partner is connected. You can check in either place:

In the WhatsApp Business app

Go to Settings → Account → Business Platform. A connected partner appears on this screen.

In Meta Business Settings

Go to Accounts → WhatsApp accounts, select the account, and open the Partners tab to see which businesses have access.

Before You Start

Do not delete your WhatsApp Business account during this process. Deleting it can result in the loss of your chats and data.
✓Have the phone that holds the number with you. You will register the number again and need to receive the verification code.
✓Install both the WhatsApp Business app and the standard WhatsApp app (also called WhatsApp Personal) if you will need Step 2.
✓Make sure you have enough free storage and a stable connection for the chat backup.

Step 1: Disconnect the Current Partner

This is the resolution Meta documents for this error. Try it first.

1

Disconnect in the WhatsApp Business App

Open the WhatsApp Business app and go to Settings → Account → Business Platform. Select the connected platform and tap Disconnect Account.

2

Wait 15 Minutes

Meta asks you to wait about 15 minutes after disconnecting before trying again.

3

Start Coexistence Onboarding Again

In the sub-account, go to Settings → WhatsApp and connect the number again using the Coexistence method. If the error is gone, you are finished.

Step 2: If the Error Continues, Offboard the Number Fully

If you still see the error after Step 1, the number was not fully released. Move it to the standard WhatsApp app and back to the WhatsApp Business app, then connect again. This also keeps your chat history.

1

Back Up Your Chats

In the WhatsApp Business app, go to Settings → Chats → Chat Backup. Tap Back Up Now and wait until the backup finishes.

2

Register the Number in the Standard WhatsApp App

Open the standard WhatsApp app (WhatsApp Personal) and register the same phone number.

3

Accept the Switch Prompt

When WhatsApp offers to switch the number to the standard app, accept it and wait for the process to complete. This offboards the number from the WhatsApp Business app and disconnects it from the previous partner.

4

Register the Number Again in the WhatsApp Business App

Open the WhatsApp Business app and register the same phone number again.

5

Restore Your Chat History

Wait for WhatsApp to detect the available backup, then select it to restore your chats.

6

Continue the Coexistence Onboarding in the CRM

Once your chat history is restored, go to Settings → WhatsApp in the sub-account and run the Coexistence onboarding again.

Why this works: Re-registering the number on the WhatsApp Business app creates a new WhatsApp Business Account that is not linked to any other business or partner. Meta Support recommends this full offboarding when disconnecting alone does not clear the conflict. Keep your backup until your chats are restored and the connection is confirmed.

Related Meta Errors

A WhatsApp account connected to another partner can also trigger other Meta messages during onboarding.

Error code
What Meta says
2655093
The business is already sharing this WhatsApp Business Account with a partner. Switching partners is not supported in this flow. Disconnect the current partner in the WhatsApp Business app, then connect the new partner.
2655094, 3441049
Disconnect the business platform in the WhatsApp Business app (Settings → Account → Business Platform), then wait 15 minutes before retrying.
2494028, 2655049
The account is already shared with another partner. Unshare the account from that partner, or create a new WhatsApp Business Account.

Troubleshooting

Issue
What to do
The error returns right after Step 1
Make sure you waited the full 15 minutes. If it still appears, continue to Step 2.
Business Platform is missing in the app
Update the WhatsApp Business app and check again. If no platform is connected through the app, go to Step 2 or check the Partners tab in Meta Business Settings.
The backup is not detected after registering again
Wait a little longer for WhatsApp to find it, and make sure you are signed in to the same Google or iCloud account that stored the backup.
The error continues after Step 2
Contact Support with the details below.
When contacting Support, include: your Location ID, the WhatsApp number, the name of the previous partner, the exact error message, which steps you completed and when, and a screenshot of the error.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does Meta not let me switch partners directly?

Meta's Coexistence onboarding flow does not support moving a connected account from one partner to another. The account has to be disconnected first, and sometimes fully offboarded.

Q: Is disconnecting the old partner enough?

Sometimes, which is why Step 1 comes first. If the error remains, the number was not fully released and Step 2 is needed.

Q: Will I lose my chats?

Not if you follow the steps and back up first. The most important rule is not to delete your WhatsApp Business account, as deleting it can cause data loss.

Q: Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep the same number. It is registered on the standard app, moved back to the WhatsApp Business app, and then connected to the CRM.

Q: Can the CRM disconnect the other partner for me?

No. The connection is removed in the WhatsApp Business app on the phone, under Settings → Account → Business Platform. The CRM cannot remove another partner's connection.
