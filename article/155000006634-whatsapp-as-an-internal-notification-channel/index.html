WhatsApp as an Internal Notification Channel

WhatsApp for Internal Notifications

Stay in the loop even when you step away from your inbox. MxP lets you receive internal notifications through WhatsApp, giving your team real-time updates in a channel they already check all day.

What is WhatsApp for Internal Notifications?

WhatsApp for Internal Notifications adds WhatsApp as an optional delivery channel (alongside In-App, Email, and SMS) for user alerts generated by MxP. Each user can choose which events should trigger WhatsApp messages, allowing fast awareness without needing to stay logged in to the app.

WhatsApp notifications are for internal users (you and your team). They surface key updates and include context so you can jump back into MxP to take action.

Key Benefits of WhatsApp Notifications

  • Faster Response Times: Real-time alerts reduce lag between an event and your follow-up.
  • Better Accountability: Mentions, assignments, and reminders help owners act without missing updates.
  • Flexible Channel Mix: Choose WhatsApp, In-App, Email, and/or SMS per event to fit each user's workflow.
  • Broader Coverage: Receive important updates even when you're away from your inbox or desktop.
  • Granular Control: Enable only the events you care about to avoid notification fatigue.

Supported Notification Categories

CRM sends WhatsApp alerts for the same event groups already found in Notification Preferences:

  • Conversation: new messages, mentions, and assignments
  • Tasks: new/updated tasks, reminders, daily summaries
  • App & Integrations: reviews posted, purchases completed, calendar disconnects, etc.

Users can enable different channels (including WhatsApp) for each sub-category individually.

WhatsApp Notification Availability and Prerequisites

Before enabling WhatsApp notifications, check your sub-account's WhatsApp configuration and the notification options available to your user profile. These checks help distinguish an account setup issue from a notification channel that is not currently displayed for your account or event type.

Check WhatsApp Configuration and Feature Availability

  1. Review WhatsApp Settings: In the applicable sub-account, go to Settings → WhatsApp. Review the connection status of your WhatsApp Business Account and any configured phone numbers.
  2. Confirm sub-account access: Make sure your user has access to the sub-account whose notifications you want to manage. Ask an agency administrator to review your access if needed.
  3. Open your notification preferences: Go to Settings → My Profile → Notifications. Review the notification categories and the delivery channels offered for each event.
  4. Confirm availability if WhatsApp is absent: If WhatsApp is not an available option, contact MxP Support to verify whether this internal notification channel is supported and enabled for your account.

Important: A connected WhatsApp account does not, by itself, establish that WhatsApp will appear in personal notification preferences. Available delivery channels can differ by notification type. The specific eligibility or rollout conditions for this toggle are not confirmed in the referenced documentation.

Troubleshoot Missing Notification Channels or WhatsApp Toggle

If the Notification Channels area or WhatsApp toggle shown in the setup instructions is missing, check the settings location and your account access before proceeding.

  1. Verify that you are in the intended sub-account and have opened Settings → My Profile → Notifications.
  2. Look through the available notification categories and event-specific channel choices. The channels shown can differ by notification type.
  3. Check Settings → WhatsApp for account connection and phone-number status.
  4. If appropriate, ask your agency administrator to verify that your user has access to the sub-account.
  5. If the relevant controls are still not visible, contact MxP Support for an account-specific availability check. Share your sub-account ID, the exact missing control, and a screenshot of the Notifications page.

Note: WhatsApp notifications in workflows, calendars, and other features have separate setup paths. Seeing WhatsApp in one of those areas does not confirm that it will be available as a personal notification channel.

How to Set Up WhatsApp Notifications

Proper setup ensures the right people receive the right alerts at the right time. Follow these steps to easily configure WhatsApp notifications.

1

Open Notification Settings

  • Navigate to Settings → My Profile → Notifications.
2

Turn On the WhatsApp Channel

  • If Notification Channels and the WhatsApp option are available, toggle WhatsApp to On. If either control is missing, follow the availability and troubleshooting checks above.
3

Choose Your Events

  • In each category (Conversation, Tasks, App & Integrations), check the specific events you want delivered to WhatsApp.
4

Save Your Preferences

  • Click Save (bottom-right).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I reply to a WhatsApp notification to take action in MxP?

WhatsApp notifications are designed for awareness. Open MxP from the link or app to reply, assign, or complete the item.

Q: Will I get duplicate alerts if I enable multiple channels?

Yes. Each enabled channel will deliver its own alert. Choose the mix that best suits your role (for example, WhatsApp + In-App for critical events only).

Q: Do these messages go to my contacts or customers?

No, these are internal notifications for team members only.

Q: Why can’t I find WhatsApp under Notification Channels?

Not all notification types display the same delivery channels. Verify your WhatsApp configuration and sub-account access, then contact MxP Support to confirm whether this option is available for your account.

Q: What if the Notification Channels area is missing?

Make sure you are in the correct sub-account and go to Settings → My Profile → Notifications. Review the event-level delivery options. If the expected controls remain missing, ask your administrator to review your access and contact MxP Support.

Q: Does connecting WhatsApp automatically enable this internal notification channel?

No automatic availability is established by the documentation. WhatsApp account setup and personal notification channel availability are separate considerations. Verify the option under Notifications before following the setup steps.
